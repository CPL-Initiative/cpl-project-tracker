Numbers you can say out loud (live dashboard, 10 Aug 2026)

  • 49,696 Californians with CPL documented on the MAP platform — 26,689 military, 23,087 workforce/other, 589 apprentices (~1.2%).
  • $313M in student savings to date; $1.24B 20-year economic impact. Apprentice share: $10M / $38M.
  • 12,517 faculty-approved credit recommendations — Standardized Assessment 6,543 · Industry Certification 3,522 · Credit by Exam 1,843 · Portfolio 402 · Military 116.
  • 2,111 MAP exhibits (138 statewide, 1,973 local) from 87 originating colleges; 103 active colleges.
  • Colleges that could adopt an existing statewide exhibit today, and haven't: Computer Info Systems 74 · Automotive 65 · EMS 59 · Kinesiology/Health 56 · Administration of Justice 55 · Fire Technology 45 · Welding 27.
  • Cal-JAC covers roughly 14,000 first responder trainees. AI apprenticeship CPL tools are piloting at Santiago Canyon and Norco with the construction trades and IBEW.

If you're asked about pre-apprenticeship

Three mechanisms (Sam Lee, 10 Aug 2026). A pre-apprentice can earn CPL from (a) noncredit coursework at a college, (b) an industry certification — welding is the obvious one, and (c) it can clear admission requirements for the apprenticeship itself and for other programs.

(c) is the one for this room. At stage 1, CPL isn't only credit banked for later — it can be what gets someone through the door sooner. The people who set apprenticeship entry requirements are in the audience. That makes it an ask, not just an observation.

The deck already shows (a) and (b) — no new slide needed. Stage 1 of the Ironworkers slide reads "Cerritos College — Noncredit Welding" and "American River College — Welding Credit Program," both under "Welding Apprenticeship Preparation." That box is pre-apprenticeship CPL. It just isn't labelled as such. Pointing at it is the most concrete way to make the case.

  • The high-school route has a catch worth knowing. High-school CTE articulation is CPL — specifically credit by exam under Title 5 §55050 (1,843 such recommendations live in MAP). ~48 high schools are already MAP learning partners, including Garden Grove High School on the Carpentry slide. But CSU does not recognise high-school CTE articulated credit for transfer — it does recognise credit by exam. Transcripted the wrong way, a pre-apprentice's credit dies at the CSU door, and every pathway in this deck ends at a bachelor's.
  • Where the noncredit route is empty — the cheap win. ROPs: 2–3 entries in MAP (Baldy View). Adult schools: 2 (Fontana). The four standalone noncredit institutions — North Orange Continuing Education, San Diego Continuing Education, Mt. SAC Noncredit and Calbright — appear on zero credential titles, even though noncredit coursework is a named source of pre-apprenticeship CPL. That gap is data entry, not policy.
  • A story that proves stage 1 matters. Rocio Garcia started in adult school, received 16 units for her California Registered Dental Assistant certification, finished her bachelor's a semester early, and is now a full-time tenure-track professor at West Los Angeles College. Cleared for use by name.
