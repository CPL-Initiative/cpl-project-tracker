How to Confirm and Qualify for CPL Funding

Four minimum conditions, and a guide to each

A college qualifies for CPL Implementation Funding when it meets four minimum conditions, which the Chancellor's Office publishes on the CPL Initiative Funding page. Two guides from the MAP team cover three of them, and the steps on this page cover local confirmation. Select a card to open a guide, or the steps, beside this page.

The four minimum conditions

  1. CPL Coordinator

    CPL Coordinator assigned on MAP and CPL Assistant email listed on the college CPL Landing Page.

  2. Local confirmation

    Local confirmation on file by December 31, 2026.

  3. Veterans' JSTs

    Minimum of 75% of enrolled veteran Joint Services Transcripts uploaded in MAP and processed for CPL.

  4. Noncredit CPL Lead

    For the noncredit CPL funding: Assign in MAP a Noncredit CPL Lead who meets regularly with the CPL Coordinator and attest to this on the CPL Funding Dashboard.

How to confirm

  1. Find your college

    Open the CPL Initiative Funding page and find your college in its table of institutions.

  2. Select the Confirm button

    Select the Confirm button beside your college's name. Its words carry the deadline, or read Confirm now once the deadline has passed. A short form opens.

  3. Give your email, name and title

    Start with your college or district email address, then give your name and title: vice president of academic affairs, vice president of student services, college president, or another senior administrator. Submitting attests that you are an administrator asking for your college to take part.

  4. Watch for the confirmation

    CPL Initiative staff review the request and email a confirmation. Until they approve it, your college's row reads Awaiting CPL Initiative Confirmation. Then the condition shows as complete.
