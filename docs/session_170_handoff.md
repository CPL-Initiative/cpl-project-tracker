our extract is stale because the MAP team was pulling records off MAP to correct
Exhibit references and reload them. Divergence may be **our staleness resolving**.
Confirm it; don't file it as a bug.

⭐ **CATALOG YEAR is the genuinely new dimension.** Nothing we hold carries it. It
pins an articulation to the catalog it was approved under rather than letting it
float — which is every "was this valid when the student took it" question we
currently cannot answer.

### The ITPI automation question — do not let this get decided casually

Pedro Campos (CEO of ITPI, the vendor developing and managing MAP) offered to
**automate a daily push of these tables into Supabase**, which would mean editing
the cron. Sam asked for a recommendation **before Pedro does anything**, and said
plainly he does not want to lose control of how, when and what is fetched.

**Recommendation: decline the mechanism, accept the help.** `fetch_custom_report.py`
already pulls **eight** datasets from the MAP API on the cron; three more is three
entries in `REQUEST_PAYLOAD`. ⭐ **The decisive argument is data minimization, not
risk:** `REQUEST_PAYLOAD` names every dataset *and every column*, and Session 34
used that to drop `View_CollegeContacts` and `View_CollegeUsersRoles` entirely so
staff PII **never lands on the Action runner**. That posture is implemented as the
absence of two entries in a Python list. Hand the write to the other side and it
evaporates silently.

If volume ever forces a push: dedicated Postgres role, INSERT-only on **named
staging tables**, never the service key, validated promotion we own. Full
reasoning: [`adr-pull-from-the-source-rather-than-accept-a-push`](kb-notes/adr-pull-from-the-source-rather-than-accept-a-push.md).

Pedro is now a Supabase org owner **and** an `allowed_reviewers` entry, so he
technically *can* write. That makes this a design conversation rather than an
access one — the right conversation, and the easier one.

## Patterns that worked

- **Check the premise before answering the question.** Two of three repos were
  already where Sam wanted them, and the privacy concern was not his concern.
- **State the best outcome for each question before reusing a tool** — what caught
  that Delta needed a different instrument.
- **Search the COURSE catalog, not just the program inventory.** Delta's plumbing
  apprenticeship sits under a construction-named prefix.
- **Read our own KB before generating a new argument.** The decisive point about
  shared credentials was a note from June.
- **Write the guard the moment you catch one instance by hand** — manual review
  found 2 over-claims; the guard immediately found 4 more.

## Safety patterns to honour

- **An RLS-filtered read returns 200 + `[]`.** Empty is not proof of empty.
- **A tag scan cannot see a `fetch()`** — [`methodology-a-tag-scan-cannot-see-a-fetch`](kb-notes/methodology-a-tag-scan-cannot-see-a-fetch.md).
- **Rule 7 — TOP is a last-in-line corroborator.** Nothing in the crosswalk gates
  on TOP; keep it that way.
- **Absence has two meanings and they must not collapse** — *no exhibit anywhere*
  (build) vs *exhibit exists, this college not listed* (adopt).
- **Never let authored prose assert something you compute.** `check_absence_claims()`
  is a hard failure by design; do not downgrade it.
- **Reviewer access is all-or-nothing** and reaches student-grain data plus
  `team_access` itself. Treat additions as the privilege grant they are.
- ⚠️ **`recalc.py` cannot run in this sandbox** — verify workbook formulas by
  matching each COUNTIF criterion against actual cell values, and say so.
- Rule 4 (both HTMLs identical), Rule 1 (change the generator), Rule 5 (never
  force-push `main`), Rule 10 (fresh read before any bulk `kb_curation` write).
