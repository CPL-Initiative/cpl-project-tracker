## What shipped

**① Handoff 174's two open items are closed by exercise, not by reading.** The
9-viewport Fact Sheet harness passes on current `main`, and the Curate reveal was
**clicked end to end in Chromium over `http://`** (a `file://` origin cannot hold
`localStorage`, which is why no earlier session could): public visitor hidden ·
`?curate=1` reveals, strips the param, is remembered · `?curate=0` forgets · **a
COBI sign-in in another browser tab reveals it with no param at all**. 10/10.

**② The Veteran Sprint map.** `@media (max-width:760px){#side{display:none}}` was
deleting the **entire side panel** on any phone — Details, both directories, both
searches, every CPL landing-page link. Tapping a college still selected it and
rendered its detail into a panel that was not on the page. Panes now stack 52/48.
Markers and directory rows were **mouse-only** and are now focusable, named and
Enter/Space-driven. Plus `100vh`→`100dvh`, 24px tap targets, named zoom buttons,
tab state exposed to assistive tech.

⚠️ **The map's HTML is GENERATED by `build_selfcontained.py`.** Every fix is in
the generator; the artifact is a rebuild; a test asserts it still is one.

**③ Sierra.** Beta disclaimer **2.80:1** and footer **3.12:1** (both under AA) ·
no `prefers-reduced-motion` for two animations · a false `role="radiogroup"` over
`aria-pressed` buttons · 19px of sideways scroll at 320px · and a conversation
log whose keyboard reachability **fell as its content grew** (the starter chips
inside it are the only focusable children, and `submit()` removes them).

**④ Two instruments, committed.** `tests/public_pages_a11y.test.js` (44 checks,
CI, **38 fail pre-fix**) and `scripts/check_public_page_layout.js` (Chromium, on
demand — 9 viewports, computed contrast incl. gradient stops, headings, focus
rings, reduced motion, and functional keyboard checks).

## Patterns that worked

- **Close a carryover by exercising it.** Two items sat as "reasoned, never
  clicked" for a handoff. Serving the repo over http:// and pressing the buttons
  closed both in one pass.
- **Run every new check against the unfixed code.** `git stash` the source, keep
  the harness, re-run. That is how three broken checks and one *unfailable* one
  were found.
- **When a check passes both ways, find out WHY.** "Passes both ways" and
  "cannot fail" look identical in a green run.
- **Make an exemption name its equivalent and verify it.** A silent skip rots;
  a guarded one fails the day the alternative disappears.
