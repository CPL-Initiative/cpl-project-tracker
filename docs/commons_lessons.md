**Lessons:**
- A mock settles a layout faster than a description. Each version answered one reaction; the build then had no guesswork.
- A filter-based list already holds most of a "new page": *Common subject in doubt* was a filter on the full Courses List, so the anchor-page design needed a placement and tabs, not a new view.
- A rule bullet's wording can satisfy another rule's guard: *"runs full width"* matched the prose-measure rule's pattern and broke `presentation_doctrine`'s 1:1 check. Say it another way (*"spans the page"*).
- A panel's helper defined inside another helper (`focusId` inside `panelHelp`) is not in scope where the bar draws its tabs; the test caught it only once it clicked a tab.
- The KINE M1267 PEDS ruling moved the whole card; a course in it that is not adapted (Cabrillo KIN 38A) needed its own move afterward. A card-level ruling can leave one misfit behind.
