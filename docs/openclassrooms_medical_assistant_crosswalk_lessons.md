# OpenClassrooms Medical Assistant → CCC CPL crosswalk — lessons

## 2026-11-03 — Ashley's fourth partner crosswalk

**Ashley opened the session** and asked for a separate project that collides with nothing, so this
build is new files only. Its outputs go to `kb/oc_medical_assistant_out/`, which is gitignored: the
deliverables go to the CPL Library, and the repo keeps the source. She supplied the OpenClassrooms
**Appendix A** (O*NET-SOC 31-9092.00, RAPIDS 1085CB, competency-based and degree-granting, 24 work
processes carrying 28 competencies, 400 RTI hours in eight projects) and the **degree-path syllabus**
(13 projects, including four Gen Ed modules and a ~250 h workplace project). She asked for sixteen
columns in her order, with separate Existing and Potential tabs and a summary.

Published page: artifact `YEgNe1aZU7kdQb8RWig2G2` (Library record `openclassrooms-medical-assistant-crosswalk`,
draft, team; receipt above). Workbook: `20261103_OpenClassrooms_Medical_Assistant_CPL_Crosswalk.xlsx`.

### Findings (2026-11-03)

- 118 institutions in COCI were reviewed (116 colleges, with NOCE and SDCCE counted separately and Calbright counted once).
  109 have at least one aligned course.
- **No exhibit, statewide CR or college course series exists for this training.** Unlike Digital Marketer,
  no college mirrors the OpenClassrooms projects.
- **40 existing approved CPL rows at 18 colleges**: mostly medical terminology (CBE, industry certification, Navy
  Hospitalman). The model is **Modesto Junior College**, which approves CPL for the CCBMA medical assisting
  certification across the whole MA series (MDAST 322, 323, 325, 326). Glendale (MOA 193 CPT coding via Hospital
  Corpsman) and Santa Ana (MA 54 billing and coding via NHA portfolio review) are the other non-terminology precedents.
- 1,011 potential alignments: 168 High at 59 colleges, 575 Medium, 268 Low.

### Lessons

1. **The MA field spans five TOP families.** 1208.x (MA), 0514.20 (medical office technology), 1223.x (health
   information and coding), 1205.10 (phlebotomy), 1215/1213 (ECG), plus terminology filed under nursing, biology and
   health ed. Gate on the family, with terminology let through from any TOP: seven colleges with no MA program only
   surfaced through terminology filed elsewhere.
2. **Some subjects aren't in the training, and the crosswalk says so.** OpenClassrooms has no module for anatomy, terminology or
   CPR. Anatomy is excluded; terminology stays at Low, with credit by exam as the route.
3. **Cap the narrow specialties.** Phlebotomy, EKG and pharmacology get 14–21 h projects in the training. They top out at
   Medium, and phlebotomy carries the CDPH-approved-program caveat. Clinical rows also carry the 16 CCR 1366.1
   training requirement for injections and venipuncture.
4. **Externships are a real match.** The 18-month OJL plus supervisor-validated checklists line up with MA practicum
   courses. These rate at least Medium when the title says medical assisting, even when the COCI description is thin.
5. **MAP data flags:** College of the Canyons' "Medical Assisting" CBE exhibit (MAPCXN-CNAC1/2) lists screenwriting
   courses (MEA 108/208). It looks mis-titled. Modesto's "Introduction to Medical Assisting" (MAPICP-M3-I-1-001) has no
   adopter. Appendix A's wage table is headed "Sales Development Representative", which looks like a template carry-over.
6. **Military ACE recs:** Hospitalman / Hospital Corpsman carry Medical Coding, Medical Office Administration and Health
   Information Management recs at 54–86 colleges. Almost all are Needs Action, which makes them reusable wording for a
   new OpenClassrooms exhibit (MAP Military CR Reference tab).
