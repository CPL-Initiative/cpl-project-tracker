# Sam's rulings

**What this is.** The lookup that issue #3055 item 2 asked for: one dated row per ruling, in Sam's verbatim words, with
where he said them and a link to where the rule now lives. Look here before asking Sam something he may already have
answered. What a relayed ruling counts for is set in [`conductor_mode`](conductor_mode.md) (*COBIx*). A row whose
**Replaced by** cell is filled no longer stands; follow it to the ruling that does.

**What it covers.** The rulings a session most often needs: decide-and-record and what still waits for Sam, merge and
checkpoint rules, the conductor settings and the cost limit, naming and voice, funding vocabulary, presentation rules,
where deliverables live, and the standing go for extraction cost. A lane's own product rulings (a card's wording, a
list's order, a college's data) live in `docs/reference/lanes/<lane>.md` under its DECIDED and RULED lines; the few
lane rulings below are examples only.

**How to read it.** PULL-side reference: it restates no rule as doctrine, and the linked rule governs wherever a row and
the rule differ (then fix the row). Words in quotation marks are Sam's, copied from the committed source the row names.
An ellipsis marks where a quote starts or ends inside his sentence or skips his words. **Where** says how he gave the
ruling when the source says so, and reads "Not stated" when the source is silent. Decisions a conductor, a worker or
COBIx made under decide-and-record are recorded in the lanes and on the standing decision sheet.

**How to extend it.** Add a row the same day Sam rules, newest first: the date, his words verbatim from a committed
source, where he said them, what the ruling governs with a link, the `cpl_memory` slug when one exists, and the row
that replaced it. When a later ruling replaces one, keep the old row and fill its last column; his words stay as
written. A rule of his whose words no committed file holds goes in the last section until someone records them.

Columns: **Date** · **Sam's words** · **Where** · **Governs** · **`cpl_memory`** · **Replaced by**.
