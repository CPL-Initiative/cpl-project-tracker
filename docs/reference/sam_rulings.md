# Sam's rulings

**What this is.** The file of record that issue #3055 item 2 asked for: one dated line per ruling, in Sam's verbatim
words, with where he said it and a link to where the rule now lives. A ruling recorded here needs no re-confirmation,
including one COBIx relayed (Sam, 2026-11-11, row below). Look here before asking Sam something he may already have
answered.

**What it covers.** The rulings a session most often needs: the hold list and decide-and-record, merge and checkpoint
rules, the conductor settings and cost limits, naming and voice, funding vocabulary, presentation rules, and the
standing data-write gos. **What it leaves to the lanes.** A lane's own product rulings (a card's wording, a list's
order, a college's data) stay in `docs/reference/lanes/<lane>.md` under its DECIDED and RULED lines; a few recent ones
appear below as examples, not as a complete list.

**What it is not.** PULL-side reference. It restates no rule as doctrine and never replaces `CLAUDE.md`; where a row and
the linked rule disagree, the linked rule wins and the row is fixed. Words in quotation marks are Sam's own, copied from
the committed source linked in the row. A conductor's, a worker's or COBIx's decision is not listed as Sam's.

**How to extend it.** Add a row the same day Sam rules, newest first: the date, his words verbatim, where he said them
(sheet and card, issue or PR, in session, and who relayed them), what the ruling governs with a link, the `cpl_memory`
slug when one exists, and the row or rule that replaced it. When a later ruling replaces one, keep the old row and fill
its last column; never edit his words.

Columns: **Date** · **Sam's words** · **Where he said it** · **Governs** · **`cpl_memory`** · **Replaced by**.

## Left out on purpose

- Rulings with no verbatim words in a committed source: they live only in PR comments or `cpl_memory` until a session
  copies his words into a lane. Examples named on #3055: the JST funnel, *Collected* for Harvested (#3229, recorded in
  [commons](lanes/commons.md) without his words) and *Ask an approver* for funding only (#3054).
- Decisions the conductor, a worker or COBIx made under decide-and-record. They are on the standing decision sheet
  (`CPLBrain/decision-sheets/decision_sheets.md`) and in each lane's DECIDED lines.
- Card-by-card replies on the Open Asks sheets: they live in each sheet's own reply store, which a session reads with
  the sheet (`CPLBrain/decision-sheets/decision_sheets.md`).
