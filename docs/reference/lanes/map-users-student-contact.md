**What this lane is:** Every college landing page routes a student's CPL request to a real person. MAP routes on `primary_contact_email`.

## Status

## The weekly contacts harvest (Sam, 2026-10-31; armed in the CPL queue, weekly on Sundays since the first full pass, 2026-11-02)

*"We've lacked the ability to communicate effectively with the field because the contacts are impossible to keep current with all the leadership and staff turnover. The weekly cron could solve all that."* (braindump `CPLBrain/03-professional/braindumps/braindump-2026-10-31-2215-a-weekly-contacts-harvest-to-reach-the-field.md`). **Armed (Sam, 2026-10-31: *"go"*) as step 8 of the CPL queue routine** (`docs/reference/scheduled_sessions.md`), which carries the build order (Governance, the reviewer-only `college_contacts_harvest` table, the runner, the read) and the cadence: nightly, 15 colleges read longest ago, until the first full pass, then weekly; Sam's reason for daily first: *"it might be better in the daily for a while until we get a good dataset"*. It harvests each college's and district's key positions, names, titles and addresses, from their own sites, into the MAP Colleges contacts. **Positions (Sam's list):** District CEO; College CEO; Vice and Deputy Chancellors; Associate Vice Chancellors; VPAA; VPSS; VPBS; Associate Vice Presidents; Dean CTE; Dean of Instruction; Dean or Director of A&R; VRC Director or Coordinator; Articulation Officer. **Title variants to match (Sam):** a CEO is a Chancellor (district), a Superintendent/President or a President; VPAA is also Provost, VP Instruction, VP Instruction and Student Services, or Chief Instructional Officer (CIO); VPSS is also Chief Student Services Officer (CSSO); VPBS is also Chief Business Officer (CBO) or VP Business Services and Facilities. Ask him for others as titles turn up. `map_college_contacts` already carries CEO, VPAA, VPSS and Articulation Officer from MAP; the rest are new, and a harvested value never overwrites MAP's (MAP is read-only for us; the harvest lands beside it with its source page and date). **Who uses it:** the funding tab's Nudge (its *Deans and directors* role waits on this; CEOs, VPs, Ambassadors and Initiators already read MAP) and the Letters, Memos, Reports tab (Sam: *"Will want to use it on our Letters, Memos, Reports tab on COBI as well"*). **Gates:** writing staff contacts into a shared table is a new write surface, so step 8 maps it in `kb/governance_surface_map.json` before the table ships (Rule 10 a3); it rides the queue routine Sam already runs, so no new scheduled task; sessions cannot reach college sites, so a GitHub runner reads them, as the catalog sweep's do.

**The runner (S537 SkyMayberry, 2026-11-01, piece 3).** `.github/workflows/college-contacts-harvest.yml` (hand dispatch, no schedule) runs `kb/_college_contacts_harvest.py`: `read` follows each college homepage's leadership links (at most 12 pages, robots.txt, 4 s, no sign-in page, off the college's domain only to a district or chancellor page) and commits each page's lines near a title to `kb/contacts_harvest/<run>/<college>.json`; `apply` checks a session's records file (college, position from Sam's list, title as printed, name, email only as printed, source URL), reads the before-values, posts through `college_contacts_harvest_apply` and commits `kb/receipts/college_contacts_harvest_<run>.json`. It fetches with plain HTTP, not a browser; a college whose site refuses it shows in the read's status. A student-body or foundation president never reads as a CEO. Guard: `tests/college_contacts_harvest_runner_test.py`. **First read (S537, run 41298454812, 15 colleges, alphabetical since none was read before):** 14 sites answered (American River's homepage seed returned 404; Calbright and Citrus gave one page each, no leadership links).
