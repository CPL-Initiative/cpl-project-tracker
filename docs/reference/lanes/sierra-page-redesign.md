# Sierra's page redesign (after america.gov)

**What it is.** Sam, 2026-10-08: *"Sierra is a little busy on the UI and should have fewer boxes. Should open full screen when possible and be maximally clean and simple, removing all distractions possible. I like the look of the new America.gov site. I do think it's useful to keep the chips that adjust the reposes to the audience type though. Maybe we cycle through our First Light plein air artwork like America.gov cycles through pics of americana."* Verbatim capture: `CPLBrain/03-professional/braindumps/braindump-2026-10-08-1945-sierra-clean-and-a-ui-pass-at-checkpoint.md`.

**State: the library look is live (S469, 2026-10-24).** Sam: *"Seeing the format of Sierra next to the new format is a little incongruous. Been thinking about switching the paintings out and having no pics and a simpler look so it can function as a sidebar better. Can you align her format with the new library look while preserving her basics?"*, then on the mock-up ([Sierra in the Library Look](https://claude.ai/artifact/GzauXMFiYX6kAzpeYzdPa1), `prototype/sierra_library_look_mockup.html`) *"White background for her as well"*, then *"Fades white to pale blue looks good too"* (`cpl_memory` `sam-sierra-library-look-no-paintings-2026-10-24`). A white page (the landing takes the library's fade; the conversation and the side bar stay white) in the library's tokens (`tests/sierra_redesign.test.js` (7) reads them from `library/index.html`): the greeting in Georgia navy with the Whitney ridge above *Sierra*, a gold rule, a bordered question box and a navy *Ask*, the audience as underlined words, three starter questions (`TRIES`) where the paintings were, the conversation unchanged in shape, and the library's navy band as the footer with the beta note and the public header's links out. **No header row (S500, 2026-10-27; Sam on a marked-up screenshot: *"since we have the clp logo in the footer, we can eliminate the whole header row, moving the links down to the footer"*).** The page opens on her greeting. The footer holds the CPL Initiative mark, now the link to map.rccd.edu (Sheet 53 card 2 carried over from the header's logo), the beta note, then one row: the five links out, and *About Sierra*, *Light* and *Dark* to their right. *About Sierra* opens her introduction under the row (click, Enter, Escape, a click outside; no hover opening, since text appearing under the pointer at the page's foot moves the row). Guards: `tests/sierra_header_about.test.js` (27), `tests/sierra_redesign.test.js` (59). **The audience is asked for at the first Ask** (Sam, ~23:20Z: *"Can we have her gently prompt the user to choose their population once they click Ask? I'll fit my answer to you."* with the five words, focus on the first; the pick sends the held question. A stored pick shows as the one folded control, *Answering for: Faculty*, at every width. No flash and no error color remain. **Her footer carries the CPL Initiative mark** (Sam: *"Maybe put the CPL Initiative logo in her footer"*): white on her page's navy band, navy on the bar's paper, white in dark. No paintings, no web fonts (the Fact Sheet still loads `sierra/fonts/`), no timers. The 2026-10-08 build's text moved to `docs/sierra_page_redesign_lessons.md`. Sweep: `npm run a11y -- sierra sierra-dark sierra-who sierra-who-dark sierra-asking sierra-asking-dark public-library-sierra`.

**Two open asks on her page (S500).** **NEEDS SAM ([Open Asks Sheet 136](https://claude.ai/artifact/JLNSJm65Z9s3SMmbJxE3Cm), card 2): the audience picker.** Measured 2026-10-27 on `chat_interactions`: the pick changes her wording. On questions asked under two or more audiences, insider terms (C-ID, TOP, COCI, credit recommendation) appear in 21% of student answers and 63 to 68% of administrator and faculty answers; *you* 5.7 times per student answer against 2.4 to 3.1. On *What is credit for prior learning?* (41 answers) student answers carry no insider term, faculty answers 60%. The substance (the tables of colleges, the links) stays the same. 95% of her last 30 days (5,157 of 5,422 answers, mostly the map.rccd.edu widget) carry no audience, so she already answers most readers from the question alone. Recommended, held because it reverses his rulings of 2026-07-01, 10-08 and 10-24: drop the picker on the public page and the side bar, and have her read the asker from the question and the conversation, answering in the student instruction's plain words when the question gives no sign. **NEEDS SAM (Sheet 136, card 3): the Ask button.** Enter already sends (Shift+Enter starts a line; a phone's key reads *send*); recommended, keep *Ask*: her box is a multi-line chat box, where Enter sending is less certain than in Google's one-line search box, every chat assistant keeps a visible send control, and voice-control and switch users press a named button.

**The docked Sierra goes full screen when she answers (S349, built).** Sam asked it in chat (*"What do you think about having Sierra expand full screen for when responding?"*) and ruled it on Sheet 54 card 1 (*Expand when answering*, 23:01Z, his own call) and Sheet 53 card 3 (*Expand in place*, the same minute). The docks are My College (`my-college`) and, since S398, Sierra AI opened over any COBI tab (`CPL_CHAT.open()`, which retired the CPL Assistant tab, #2163).
- **When.** The first send through a dock expands it, after the role checks (an unconfirmed role holds the question and expands nothing). *Back to the tab* or Escape returns it with the conversation; a reader who goes back stays docked for that conversation until they choose *Full screen*. A new subject (`setScope` to another college) starts a new conversation, and the next answer expands again. Arriving at another tab returns her to her seat.
- **In place.** The same `.cplchat` wrap moves to `<body>` (First Light's glass cards set `backdrop-filter`, which traps `position: fixed`), a hidden placeholder keeps its seat, and the answer keeps the tab's `scope`, `surface` and the reader's credential. It is a modal dialog named by its bar (*Sierra AI*); every other child of `<body>` is `inert` and the page's scroll is locked. A fixed layer that paints above her stays live, so First Light's greeting (z 12000; her z is 11000) can always be dismissed.
- **The column.** The public page's asking view: paper, a centered 46rem column, the bar at the top (her name, *Back to the tab*), the box docked at the bottom, Sam's Note under it. One fade, only with no reduced-motion preference.
- **The control** is one word, a direct child of the wrap: My College hoists the intro's heading into its `<summary>`, so a control in the intro would be left behind. A box a later mount superseded ignores its own control.
