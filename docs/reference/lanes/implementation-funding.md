⭐ **THE EARN DIAGNOSTIC — the finding that justified the restructure (measured 2026-09-01, live model × live feed).** The credit slice pays **34.0% of its cap** ($7,969,414 of $23,456,909), and **84% of everything earned comes from Access: Outreach, which 97 of 115 colleges already max out** (86.0% earned). **The two new measure sources (declared-before-delivered, the noncredit lane's proven pattern — `srcDelivered()` reads an undelivered key as $0, never a full cap):** `ppe`/`ppe_u` = ELIGIBLE units among portal-origin students (Sam ruled "filter now"; emits from today's feed since Potential Student is already in the pull) and `pac`/`pac_u` = APPLIED units on an accepted Student CPL Plan via the MAP lifecycle attestation (OMITTED, not zeroed, until the column lands). ⚠️ **`ppe` is `pe`'s COMPLEMENT, not `pe` filtered** — every `pe`/`pa`/`p2`/`p3` hit carries `and not is_potential`, exactly as `ppa` is `pa`'s. ⚠️ **`pac` carries NO `is_potential` condition** (the attestation is done for one student regardless of how they arrived) and is named for the credit's STATE, not who attested.

⭐ **THE COUNSELOR STEP IS A POLICY ATTESTATION, NOT A TECHNICAL GUARANTEE (Sam, 2026-09-01 — corrects a claim this lane briefly carried).** It CAN be batch-loaded, legitimately: *"we ask colleges to batch upload previously transcribed CPL from their SIS, with the assumption that they went through the counseling steps with each student before transcribing."* Its integrity rests on the CO instruction — stop auto-awarding (which *"can impact students negatively"*), confirm acceptance with the student, then check the step — plus the **audit trail recording who attested and when**. Sam ruled **either the student or the counselor/coordinator/initiator** may check it. The live risk is a college that auto-awards (military basic-training credit especially) and batch-sets the flag anyway. It still does real work — an undifferentiated applied count asks the college to assert nothing — but **the gap narrows, it does not close**, and no surface may claim otherwise.
