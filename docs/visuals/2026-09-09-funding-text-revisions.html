1 · Retire the banking sense of “draw”

Sweep
What this is
Your call today. Measured across the three surfaces: 15 sites in rendered text — 11 on the tab, 4 on the explainer. Code comments and the unrelated word withdrawn (a participation confirmation the CO revoked) are not counted and not touched.
What I propose
Replace it with earn and its forms. The model already runs on earned, unearned, Current Total and Total Possible, so nothing new enters the vocabulary and the surfaces agree with each other.

4 · “Measured in the same currency”

Sweep
What this is
On the explainer: “Every priority is measured in the same currency: units of prior learning, converted to FTES.”
What I propose
“The model measures every priority in the same unit: units of prior learning, converted to FTES…”
Why
It is a money metaphor on a funding page, where a reader may take it literally, and a metaphor is what the mannerly-language rule cuts. The sentence is also passive; the rewrite fixes both at once.

5 · The explainer’s opening claim

Sweep
What this is
The bolded lede of the second section: “What an institution receives is driven by its own CPL results, as they happen. The figure allocated to a college is what is available to it across the two years; what it draws down tracks the prior-learning credit it actually puts on students’ records…” Three things in two sentences: a passive with a business verb, an aside, and a mannerly adverb.
What I propose
“An institution’s own CPL results determine what it receives. The figure allocated to a college is what is available to it across the two years; what it earns tracks the prior-learning credit it puts on students’ records…”
Why
The active sentence is shorter and names the actor. As they happen adds nothing the next clause does not say, and actually is the writer reassuring the reader rather than telling them something.

8 · The explainer’s sections cannot be renamed or hidden yet

Needs you
What this is
Rename and Hide ride sectionShell(), the one function every section on the tab passes through — which is why they reached the model page, the $50K view, the Report and the standalone public page at once. The explainer is a different shape: nine hand-written sections, no ids, titles in the markup. Nothing to hook.
What I propose
Give each one an id and read the same titles and secHidden maps the tab already writes, so one rename or one hide applies everywhere that section appears. Proposed ids, in page order:
  • lede — what the appropriation is and how it splits
  • institutions — every institution (the embedded table)
  • allocation — how much each institution is allocated
  • noncredit — the noncredit share
  • qualify — what a college has to do to qualify
  • earning — how the funding is earned
  • timing — when the funding is disbursed
  • choices — what is a choice and what is a given
  • sources — where these numbers come from

Say if any of these should not be hideable. My one reservation is sources: hiding where the figures come from is the section a sceptical reader most wants.

Why
Doing it against the same maps is the difference between one control and two that drift. It is also the reason I stopped rather than hand-rolling a second mechanism.
