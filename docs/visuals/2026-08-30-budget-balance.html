                Ruled 2026-08-31 — the origination design's three calls

                All three ruled by number (N1 a · N2 b · N3 a, Sam, 2026-08-31). The options stay for the record; the chosen one is marked. Everything measured was from the live tab and MAP as of 2026-08-31.

                1. N1 — The veteran-JST gate cannot apply to them. The three baseline requirements are a CPL contact in MAP, a participation request, and 75% of enrolled veterans' JSTs uploaded — and the funding tab's own fine print says noncredit campuses are not obligated to collect JSTs. As full institutions with the full floor, they need a stated third gate.
                  • a · RULED: substitute an in-kind readiness gate — the campus's noncredit certificates are posted as exhibits in MAP. Same function as the JST gate (your records are in the system before money moves), checkable in MAP today, and it is already the first recommended noncredit strategy. It is also the precondition of your origination measure: CPL cannot originate from a program MAP cannot see.
                  • b · Not chosen — exempt noncredit-only institutions from the third gate.
                  • c · Not chosen — keep the JST gate, vacuously met where no JSTs exist.
                2. N2 — Origination needs a feed that carries the origin. Your linkage rule (SDCCE's CPL credited to SDCCE wherever it lands among the district's credit campuses) is a data requirement before it is a funding rule: the model's performance feed does not carry a noncredit origin today — every noncredit measure row reads no feed. The known wiring path is the noncredit origin location in MAP (each campus's own MAP location ID on its landing page and exhibits) flowing into the daily performance pull.
                  • a · Not chosen — advance at full cap until the feed lands.
                  • b · RULED: no advance on origination — the noncredit-only institutions draw only as originated CPL actually posts, from the first dollar. Consequence, stated plainly: until the origin feed exists and real CPL flows, these three campuses hold their allocations and draw nothing — which makes the origin feed the leading build item of the port.
                3. N3 — Two Calbright specifics. In the pool with the same window, its allocation is set by its size — and its noncredit FTES is your 1,000-FTES stand-in (reported figure 21,438, pending Malone's verification). At the stand-in it sits at the floor; a verified figure near the reported one moves it toward the ceiling, a swing of up to about $220,000.
                  • a · RULED: keep the stand-in for exploration, and the model refuses to publish or disburse an allocation built on a placeholder size — the verified MIS figure is the unlock (the tab's existing warning, promoted to a rule). The double count is confirmed for all three institutions, not Calbright alone — NOCE and SDCCE's origination has the same both-sides property.
                  • b · Not chosen.
