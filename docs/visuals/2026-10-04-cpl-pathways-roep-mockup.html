How the two tabs work together

The harvest tab runs the reading; CPL Pathways shows what it read. A misread found here goes back to the college's reading procedure. Nobody edits a record by hand: the procedure changes, the next read comes out right, and the change shows up here.

  1. Harvest tabProcedureEach college's reading steps: its catalog platform, where its addenda sit, where its program maps live, and what a host has refused.
  2. Harvest tabReadThe weekly census finds the catalog; the program read records each program's blocks, choices, units and printed total.
  3. Harvest tabChecksEvery course the state's file lists is placed, no course is added, the units add up, and a person reads it against the catalog.
  4. CPL PathwaysShowThe requirement map and the pathway, with CPL marked on each course, for colleges and the public.
  5. CPL Pathways to harvest tabFile the misreadA gap seen on the map is filed against the procedure that produced it, and the program is queued for the next read.

Step 5 feeds step 1. Accuracy comes from the procedures, one college at a time.
