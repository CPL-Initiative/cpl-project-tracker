Exhibits: one tab to curate, one page to adopt

You asked for the CER made simple enough to finish, so a public page can list every credential a college could adopt. This sketch folds EACR into the CER, gives the team one tab in the Catalog Commons, and draws the public page that tab feeds. Every number comes from the committed files of today's runs.

One credential, three questions

The CER and EACR describe the same record from two sides. The CER clusters what colleges typed into MAP under one common title, as SkyView does for courses. EACR shows the credit that title carries and who could adopt it. A merged tab keeps the clustering as the team's work and puts adoption on the credential itself.

Color and layout

Ground and surfaces

First Light's paper ground with white data surfaces, as drawn here. The CER's yellow wash and EACR's gold badges and gold unit text go.

Ink

Seal blue for headings, cobalt for every word you can press, gray for counts and notes. Green and red appear only where you must act.

One row of controls

A search box and two or three words. Filters open when asked for. Counts sit on the view names and the one meter, nowhere else.

Shape follows the job

A single card for a decision, a table for scanning, a side panel for one credential's detail. The public page uses cards because colleges browse it.

My recommendation, and what I need from you

  1. Merge EACR into the CER as one Exhibits tab in the Catalog Commons, with Review, Credentials and Rules. I recommend it: both tabs read the same common titles, and one record ends the three counts. EACR's statewide and college filters move to the public page, where colleges use them.
  2. Build the Review queue first. It is what finishes the CER. Sorted, the 468 take one rule (the 96 high school and ROP titles), a few group accepts, about eight pages of near-sure titles, and 120 single decisions; each Teach a rule shrinks the next night's queue. Done means every MAP exhibit sits under one canonical credential with its issuing agency, the catalog Malone's module reads.
  3. Launch the public page on the stamped credentials only, so it grows as the CER finishes and never shows a title a person or a rule has not settled. The name is yours: Credentials Your College Can Adopt is a placeholder.
  4. The look. First Light as drawn, or the CPL/AI look Malone gave the Library and Sierra, since this page is public too?
  5. The high school and ROP rule. 3D Digital Modeling HS-CNUSD reads as the course title with the district as issuer (3D Digital Modeling, Corona-Norco Unified School District). The classifier already reads that shape as guidance (a named school is the agency, 5f) and still holds these as unsure. Make it a rule, so the 96 save under it and undo together if wrong?
