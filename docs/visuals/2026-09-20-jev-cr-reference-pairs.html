Jev on the CR Reference

TypeSafe’s System One model read 51 candidate pairs from the Common CR Reference and gave each a probability that the two credit recommendations describe the same course. It took ten seconds. These are suggestions. Nothing here merges anything.

What you are looking at

The Common CR Reference holds 2,159 groups of freehand credit-recommendation wordings. 1,936 of them sit on rung 5, where nothing decides them today, and none of those holds more than one wording — so the judgment is across groups, not inside one. Comparing every rung-5 group with every other is 1,873,080 pairs.

Blocking on the shared canonical course cuts that to 51 pairs carrying 1,184 articulation rows, and every one of those clusters already contains a published statewide line to ask against. That is what Jev was asked: is this college’s wording the same recommendation as the published one?

Your verdicts are the point. The cr_reference_decisions table is empty — there are no curator judgments on this lane yet. What you record here becomes the first set, and the measure of whether Jev is worth using on the other 1,881 groups.

How to reply

Answer as many as you like in a sitting and come back — replies are saved on the sheet, not in a commit, so nothing executes until you say the package is ready.

  • Yes — take the proposal as written.
  • Keep — leave the two wordings separate.
  • Edit — a different disposition; put it in the note and I use your words verbatim.
  • Later — not now.
  • Follow up — independent of the verdict; flags it for me to chase.

An item with no reply has no verdict. I will ask about the gaps rather than read silence as a yes.
