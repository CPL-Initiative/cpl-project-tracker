From high school to the job site

One Field Ironworkers pathway at Cerritos College, rung by rung: where a learner can start, what each award requires, the credit for prior learning (CPL) Cerritos has already articulated, and the jobs at the end. A label on each line says whether it comes from our data or still needs confirming.

  1. Start

    High school, adult school and ROP

    The earliest doors into the trade, before a learner enrolls at Cerritos.

    • Columbus High School (Downey Unified) runs a welding pathway into WELD 100 at Cerritos through dual enrollment.To confirm
    • Norwalk-La Mirada Adult School and Southeast ROP teach welding.To confirm
    • MAP holds no high school, adult school or ROP credit for prior learning at Cerritos.In our data

    CPL at this rung: none articulated yet. The high school list comes from Cerritos's Educational Partnerships office.

  2. Noncredit

    Pre-Apprenticeship and noncredit ironworker courses

    Tuition-free noncredit courses at Cerritos that prepare a learner for the apprenticeship.

    • Pre-Apprenticeship certificate (control number 24102): AED 36.02, 36.03, 36.04 and 80.01.In our data
    • The certificate admits apprentices only.To confirm
    • AED 36.05 Basic Welding, and OSHA 10 in AED 90.05.In our data
    • Twenty-six noncredit courses, AED 40.01 through 41.10, mirror the credit IWAP courses one for one.In our data
    • Whether those 26 courses still run each term.To confirm

    CPL at this rung: none yet. A noncredit-to-credit articulation would let each AED course count toward its IWAP twin; Santa Ana College's noncredit exhibit in MAP is the model.

  3. Apprenticeship

    Ironworkers Locals 433 and 416

    Paid on-the-job training with classroom instruction. This training is what Cerritos credits through CPL.

    • Local 433's joint apprenticeship committee runs a 48-month program at its La Palma training center; Local 416 trains in Norwalk.To confirm
    • Classroom hours: published figures disagree, 480 or more than 700.To confirm

    CPL at this rung: the apprenticeship's coursework earns credit by exam toward the certificates and the A.S. below.

  4. CertificatesIn our data

    Reinforcing and Structural certificates

    Each certificate is one complete A.S. major option: the 19-unit core plus its option's courses.

    • Reinforcing (control number 36002), 34 units: up to 31.5 through CPL.In our data
    • Structural (control number 36003), 38 units: up to 16.5 through CPL, all on the core.In our data

    CPL at this rung: the certificate figures follow from the A.S. record below, since COCI lists the same courses.

  5. Associate degreeIn our data

    A.S., Apprenticeship: Field Ironworkers

    Control number 42158, from the 2026–27 catalog. The major is 34–38 units: the core plus one option.

    CPL at this rung: up to 31.5 of the 34–38 major units, articulated at Cerritos as credit by exam on 15 courses. Every core course but Welding I carries it, as does every Reinforcing course. The Structural option carries none yet.
