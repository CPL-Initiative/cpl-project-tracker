1 · How the career-attainment measure enters the model

reference: goal (C) · Ed. Code §78093.2(d)(1)(C) · docs/reference/lanes/implementation-funding.md

What this is
Goal (C) is funded today through the statewide project allocation, split with (D), and its card reads “Awaiting a campus measure. This outcome reports through its designated activities.” Your note of 2026-09-22 gives it a measure of a different kind: CO research measures career attainment from EDD wage data, colleges report nothing, and the model takes the figure through periodic imports. The model reads a measure it has not yet received as $0 (srcDelivered()), so a funded priority pinned to the wage measure before its first import would strand its share.
Why
Two questions turn on the same fact: when the first import lands. Showing the measure on the (C) card needs one import. Moving funding onto it needs an import that covers the window's awards, and wage records arrive quarters after the awards they follow.
What I propose
Show it first, fund it after an import. From the first import the (C) card reads the wage measure as a reported outcome, dated by that import. A share of institution funding moves onto it by a separate ruling once an import has landed and CO research has stated the lag. It might be wrong if the CO intends (C) funding to follow the wage measure from Year 1, in which case the first import has to land before the Year-1 disbursement.

2 · Which wage outcome, and whose definition

reference: goal (C) · the measure registry in cpl_funding.js

What this is
CO research already reports wage outcomes for community college students from EDD unemployment-insurance wage records, among them employment in the second fiscal quarter after exit, median annual earnings, and attainment of the regional living wage. The model measures either units of CPL, converted to FTES, or students. A wage outcome is a count of students.
Why
The definition decides what a college's figure means and whether it matches the figure CO research publishes elsewhere. One definition, applied the same way in both places, lets the leadership review read the model's (C) figure against numbers it already knows.
What I propose
CO research defines it, applied to students awarded CPL. The import carries one count of students per college, with counts under 10 masked by CO research before the figure reaches the model, per the funding-counts ADR. It might be wrong if you want the model to name its outcome now, so the leadership review sees a specific measure rather than a pending one.

3 · Does Priority 2 keep serving goal (C)

reference: prioGoals() · your ruling of 2026-09-01

What this is
Priority 2 (Awards) measures applied CPL units for students with the Counselor step checked, and prioGoals() resolves it to (B) and (C): your 2026-09-01 ruling that the advising step is the part of career attainment a campus controls. The wage measure would give (C) a second measure, one the CO takes.
Why
With a CO measure in place, (C) can rest on the wage data alone and Priority 2 return to (B), or (C) can carry both the campus step and the CO outcome.
What I propose
Keep Priority 2 on (B) and (C). The counselor step stays the campus lever toward career attainment, and the wage measure reports the outcome that lever serves. It might be wrong if the leadership review reads one goal carrying two measures as the same figure claimed twice, in which case Priority 2 returns to (B) when the first import lands.

4 · Consolidate the institution drill-in

reference: the college table expand · your screenshot of 2026-09-22 (Alameda)

What this is
Each institution's expand opens on three prose cells before its priority table: the FTES share (3,155 FTES = 0.274% of the statewide 1,151,171), the base explanation (a pure proportional share would be $69,175 … brought up to the $150,000 base award), and a baseline paragraph that restates the reserve and ends on a Mark opted-in button. Below them a second Confirm Participation control repeats the one on the row, and the line above the priority table states the reserve a third time.
Why
The row already carries the FTES, the (at base) word with its hover, and the Elig pie, and the Summary and the Baseline section state the reserve rule. The expand's work is this institution's priority figures.
What I propose
One status line, then the priority table. The line reads, for Alameda: Baseline: coordinator on file · local confirmation due 2026-11-01 · Veteran Star not yet · $1,225 reserved until confirmation, with the CO's Mark confirmed control on it. The FTES share moves into the CR FTES hover; the base cell and the second Confirm Participation control go; the line above the table keeps Current Total and Total Possible. It might be wrong if colleges use the FTES-share sentence to check their own figure, in which case it stays as a short line under the status.
