      the institutions with Base and Cap funding
        how an allocation is computed
        • Each institution's share of the funding is proportional to its total credit + noncredit FTES.
        • Shares below $150,000 are brought up to the base; shares above $400,000 are held at the cap; the split re-solves so the total lands fully allocated.
        • The award then decomposes into credit and noncredit shares by FTES share — the noncredit share is restricted to the noncredit measures.
        • Earning: each priority's Current Total = (actual ÷ target, capped at 100%) × its credit funding, at $2,824.82 per CPL FTES; unearned funding rolls forward within the window.
        Eligibility Requirements
        • A named CPL Coordinator and Counselor, listed in MAP and responsive to Landing Page and Student Portal requests.
        • Participation confirmed by the deadline — each institution attests from its own row below ("Confirm participation" inside the expand).
        • Veteran Star: at least 75% of veteran JSTs uploaded. For the three noncredit-only campuses this gate is replaced by noncredit certificates posted as exhibits in MAP (N1 a).

        Funding for institutions that have not met baseline participation is held in reserve — it is never redistributed to others.

        Two lanes, one award — ruled 2026-08-31
        Planning note — for Sam, not tab copy

        One solve over one funding total — and every award decomposes into a credit share and a noncredit share, by each lane's share of the institution's FTES. The noncredit share is not a label: it earns only against the noncredit priority measures, so the credit program cannot draw it by doing credit work. On this page it appears in the Funding Breakdown, in the Summary, and in every institution's columns and expand.

        Three Priority Outcome-Based Allocations

        Each priority funds its target at 0.50× the 2026-27 state rate of $5,649.63 — an effective $2,824.82 per CPL FTES. A college's credit share earns (actual ÷ target) on each priority, up to 100%; its noncredit share earns against the noncredit measures; unearned dollars roll forward. Units convert at 30 semester units per FTES (45 on a quarter calendar).

        the earning rules for noncredit
        • A college's noncredit share of its combined award earns against the noncredit priority measures (ruled 2026-08-31).
        • The noncredit-only institutions earn by origination — CPL that originates from their programs and is transcribed at a credit college. NOCE and SDCCE's origination counts across their district's credit campuses; Calbright's counts statewide. The receiving college earns on the same CPL under its own measures — the same CPL credits both institutions by design (ruled 2026-08-31).
        Funding Outcomes Required by Ed. Code §78093.2(d)(1)

        The subdivision, verbatim: “Upon appropriation by the Legislature for purposes of this article, the chancellor’s office shall allocate designated funds to support implementation of this article at each campus using all of the following goals:”

        (A) Equitable access

        What funds it

        Priorities 1 and 2 — the applied and eligible measures.

        How it is evidenced

        Performance-measured. Equity is by design — the base and cap equalize between colleges — and is reported in the system's three-year reports, not scored per college.

        the statute’s words

        “(A) Increasing access to credit for prior learning opportunities equitably for all eligible students.”

        (B) Completion

        What funds it

        Priority 3 — the transcribed measure.

        How it is evidenced

        Performance-measured.

        the statute’s words

        “(B) Increasing completion through credit for prior learning awards.”

        (C) Career attainment

        What funds it

        The project funding, held out of the direct college awards.

        How it is evidenced

        Demonstrated, not measured — CPL stories and the funded infrastructure; no campus measure by design.

        the statute’s words

        “(C) Advancing career attainment through credit for prior learning.”

        (D) Pilot projects

        What funds it

        The named projects on the Budget ledger.

        How it is evidenced

        Declared and delivered through the Activities register.

        the statute’s words

        “(D) Supporting credit for prior learning opportunities through the chancellor’s office’s pilot projects, such as the California Mapping Articulated Pathways Initiative.”

        §78093.2(d)(2): each campus demonstrates implementation through these metrics before receiving an allocation. The statute's goal words (access · completion · career attainment) and the model's three priorities are reconciled in the model documentation.

        Method and sources
        Planning note — for Sam, not tab copy
        • Allocations use the tab's own bounded split (proportional to total FTES, clamped to the window, re-split so the total balances) over the real 115 colleges + 3 noncredit-only institutions. Mt. SAC Noncredit is counted on the Mt. San Antonio row, once.
        • Actuals are the live per-college MAP pull of 2026-08-31 (applied portal-origin units for Priority 1, eligible units for Priority 2, transcribed units for Priority 3). Colleges absent from the pull show "no data yet" and earn $0 here rather than an invented figure.
        • Calbright's noncredit FTES is the 1,000-FTES stand-in; nothing publishes or disburses on a placeholder size (N3 a).
        • Statewide earned figures on this page sum the per-college computation above; the live port carries the tab's full earning machinery, so its earned line can read higher than here.
        • Total Possible = the ceiling: per priority, its credit and noncredit shares together; per institution, the max award; statewide, the priority's full funding. Current Total = what is earned to date. Current never exceeds the Total Possible, and the gap between them is the incentive.
        • Nothing on this page writes anywhere.
