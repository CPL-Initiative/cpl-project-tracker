        Planning note — for Sam, not tab copy

        One pool, one solve — and every award decomposes on its face into a credit share and a noncredit share, by each lane's share of the institution's FTES. The noncredit share is not a label: it earns only against the noncredit priority measures, so the credit program cannot draw it by doing credit work. On this page it appears on the pool line, in the summary, and in every institution's columns and expand.

        F1 ruled (2026-08-31): the noncredit shares are listed from day one and simply read $0 earned until the noncredit measures report — no interim policy needed, because the data feeds are wired in before this page reaches colleges. Nothing publishes on funding we cannot measure. The three noncredit-only institutions were already ruled the same way — no advances, origination only (N2 b).

        The three priorities — how the funding is earned

        Each priority funds its target at 0.50× the 2026-27 state rate of $5,649.63 — an effective $2,824.82 per CPL FTES. A college's credit share earns (actual ÷ target) on each priority, up to 100%; its noncredit share earns against the noncredit measures once their feeds report; unearned dollars roll forward. Units convert at 30 semester units per FTES (45 on a quarter calendar).

        the earning rules for noncredit
        • A college's noncredit share of its combined award earns against the noncredit priority measures; it is listed from day one and reads $0 earned until those feeds report (ruled 2026-08-31).
        • The noncredit-only institutions earn by origination — CPL that originates from their programs and is transcribed at a credit college. NOCE and SDCCE's origination counts across their district's credit campuses; Calbright's counts statewide. The receiving college earns on the same CPL under its own measures — the same CPL credits both institutions by design (ruled 2026-08-31).
        • No advances on origination (N2 b): a noncredit-only institution draws only as its originated CPL actually posts, so the origination feed (Origin + LocID2 on the Custom Reports) is the gate between these campuses and any funding.

        Outcomes-based awards — the full allocation detail on click

        Alphabetical. The award figures are each institution's max award — what it can earn by outcomes, not an automatic payment — split into its credit and noncredit shares. Click a row for its per-priority funding, targets, and actuals.
