The calls

1 · Which direction is load-bearing

What this is
We hold two stores saying subject→discipline and one saying discipline→subject, with no statement of which is derived from which. That is what makes the loop possible.
What I propose
Subject → discipline is the primary edge. Every SUBJ4 belongs to exactly one discipline; a discipline may carry several subjects. So subject_discipline_map.json becomes the authority for that edge, and the CSR’s discipline_canonical_subj4.json answers a genuinely different question — given a discipline, which of its several codes is the canonical one. The seeder’s 64-prefix table and discipline_inference.json’s subject_map become inputs to the first, not parallel authorities.
Why
The two questions only look circular because we never said they were different questions. The many-to-one edge is a fact about the vocabulary; the canonical-code choice is a judgment. Naming which is which dissolves the loop without changing a single value.

2 · Fill the 233 blanks from what we already hold

What this is
Of the 326 blank identities: 199 have a discipline in the identifier reference, 177 resolve through the subject map, 143 are covered by both — and those 143 agree without a single disagreement. Together they fill 233. Ninety-three would still be blank.
What I propose
Fill a blank discipline from the identifier reference first, then the subject map, and stamp a discipline_source of its own (coci_reference / subject_map) so the provenance shows on the row rather than arriving anonymously. The 93 that remain become a named worklist, not a silent residue.
Why
This is a generator change, so it lands once and holds. Two independent stores agreeing on 143 of 143 is about as much corroboration as this data ever offers. And it is not TOP: 219 of the 326 do carry a TOP code, and under Rule 7 it stays a last-in-line corroborator — the reference’s discipline comes from the identifier’s own subject prefix against the MQ list, which is a separate signal.

3 · Stop deriving a subject’s discipline by voting

What this is
SkyView’s subject table works out a subject’s home discipline by taking the modal discipline of the identities carrying it. When those are blank, the vote returns blank, and the table reports no discipline yet — which reads as a statement about the subject. 148 of 344 subjects on the map currently report that; for 11 of them our own map file names a discipline (AERO → Aviation, PHTO → Photography, STAT → Mathematics, and eight more).
What I propose
Read the home discipline from subject_discipline_map.json when it has an entry; fall back to the vote only when it does not; and say on the row which of the two answered.
Why
A derived blank that looks like an asserted blank is the failure here — it is self-fulfilling, and it is what sent you looking. Note this is the display layer only: your PSYC is not one of the 148. Its vote lands on Psychology 268 to 2, so the subject table is already right about PSYC. Fixing this does not fix item 2, and item 2 does not fix this.

4 · The 28 firewalled anchors, and your proposal with nowhere to go

What this is
28 of the 326 blanks are locked curated anchors — all CCN-ID — where a curator can only propose. The other 298 are editable in the CCR tab today. Your Psychology proposal on PSYC C1000 is the only row in that table, and there is no step that applies one.
What I propose
Build the maintainer step: a proposal on a locked anchor is applied by a named maintainer, the applied value carries the proposer and the date, and the queue is visible rather than a table nobody opens. Until that ships, item 2’s fill covers these 28 anyway — so the queue stops being the only route.
Why
A curator who can see the right answer, type it in, and watch nothing happen will stop typing. The firewall is worth keeping; the dead end is not.
