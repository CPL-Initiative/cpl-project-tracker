Decision 1

1. Fold the Ed. Code goal cards into the priority bands

What it is

The section already became one section on 2026-09-01: three bands — (A) Access, (B)+(C) Success, (D) Opportunities — with the priority cards inside them, and the four statutory goal cards moved one click down into Statutory reporting detail. Your question is whether that fold can go away entirely and everything live in the bands.

The one structural catch: (B) and (C) are separate goals sharing one band, and they differ on precisely the axis §78093.2(d)(2) asks about — (B) is earned against a MAP measure, (C) is funded and deliberately not measured. Decision 2

2. The flat funding ledger — you are not imagining it

What it is

It is committed: docs/visuals/2026-08-31-if-tab-simplified.html, section Funding Breakdown. Four lines, right-aligned amounts, a muted sub-line where one is needed, and the named projects behind a fold. Base and cap were lifted out into their own section with two folds under them. None of it reached the tab, which still ships seven boxes.

Why the port stopped there

The mock is read-only. The seven boxes are your editing surface — the amount and the label in each are inline editors, with a control to drop a box from the funding math and another to hide it from the public college page, plus the two Add buttons. A flat list of text looked like it would cost that, so it stayed. It does not: an inline editor sits in a ledger row exactly as it sits in a box, and the row-end controls come along.

The second thing worth saying

Two of the seven boxes are not ledger lines at all. 1,151,171 institution credit + noncredit FTES is the allocation basis, and $5,649.63 per CPL FTES is the reimbursement rate — both context for how an allocation is computed, neither one a sum that nets down to the total. Standing in the money ledger they read as though they do.

Proposed Port the ledger, keep every dial editable in place, and split the section three ways: the money ledger, the base and cap on their own line, and the two context figures moved down into How an allocation is computed, where a reader is already asking about them.

One more, unasked

Adding To go took the expand’s priority table to eight columns, and three of them are money: CR funding, NC funding, and Total Possible — which is the first two added together. That is the same redundancy you struck four times in the detail today, and it is the one column set a college has to read to answer “what could we earn here.” It would collapse to a single Total Possible with the noncredit share as its sub-line, the way the award cells in the main table already carry theirs.

Ruled 2026-09-01 — Sam Dismissed: “the lane split is worth the width.” The expand keeps CR funding, NC funding and Total Possible as three columns.
