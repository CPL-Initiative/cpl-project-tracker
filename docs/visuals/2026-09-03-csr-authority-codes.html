Authority Codes for the CSR

Sam asked whether the CSR should carry the C-ID and CCN subject codes rather than mint its own. The CSR minted its own for two reasons ruled in June 2026: the canonical code must be exactly four letters, and a code must not read as a CCN claim. Twenty of the sixty-two C-ID codes in our corpus are not four letters, so most of the divergence follows from the first rule. This sheet proposes a rule for the seed and lists every discipline where the rule changes a code, with what would re-key. Items 20 and 21 carry your second question of the day: retiring the Z band so that everything that is not a C-ID or CCN is an M-ID. Nothing is re-minted until you rule.

How to rule: reply in chat by number. yes accepts a proposal as written; or override with edit: your wording, fold: N (treat with item N), or dismiss.

Example: 1–4 yes · 8 edit: keep CRIM · 12 dismiss

A session then writes the rulings into the seed's notes, runs the measure-first dry run under the re-mint playbook, and brings the apply back as its own pull request.

The rule

1 Seed from the authorities Rule
What it is
Where CCN or C-ID publishes a subject code for a discipline's courses, the canonical Common SUBJ is that code. The CSR mints its own only where neither authority has one.
Measured
Of 146 disciplines, 23 already sit on an authority code, 28 differ from one (31,467 M-IDs, part of it inference noise, see 17 and 18), and 95 have no C-ID or CCN code at all (25,083 M-IDs, the CTE and noncredit bulk). The CSR keeps minting for most of the reference either way; the authority codes cover the transfer core, which is where faculty recognition matters most.
Proposed
Yes. Record the source of every canonical code in the seed (ccn, c-id, csr) so the provenance is visible on the CSR tab.
2 Precedence: CCN, then C-ID, then ours Rule
What it is
The two authorities disagree with each other in places: CCN says PSYC where C-ID says PSY; CCN says CDEV where C-ID says ECE. A precedence rule decides without a meeting.
Measured
All 14 CCN codes in the corpus are four letters; CCN is statute (AB 1111) and covers the transfer general-education core. C-ID covers 65 subject codes in our reference, 20 of them not four letters.
Proposed
CCN first, C-ID second, the CSR's own code last. Under this rule Psychology stays PSYC (item 15) and Child Development moves to CDEV (item 9).
3 The shape rule: four letters, or the authority's code verbatim Rule · decides most of the list
What it is
The seed's invariant is ^[A-Z]{4}$. The C-ID codes that fail it: AJ, PH, BUS, MUS, KIN, ECE, PSY, ADS, CHS, EET, EMS, HIT, MIS, PHS, SJS and the five hyphenated agriculture families AG-AB, AG-AS, AG-EH, AG-MA, AG-PS. This rule is why 122 of the 146 canonical codes are June overrides (CRIM over AJ, BUSI over BUS, MUSI over MUS).
Options
(a) Accept the authority's code verbatim, two to five characters with a hyphen allowed. The id parsers already split on the first space, so AJ M1001 and AG-AB M1001 read correctly today; the seed's invariant, the fold, and the tests that pin four letters change. (b) Keep four letters and diverge by design for those twenty.
Proposed
(a). A code the faculty already use beats a code that is tidy. If you choose (b), items 5, 6, 8, 12 and 14 fold to "no change".
4 Retire "not a CCN claim" Rule
What it is
Your 2026-06-09 rule for the language codes: our codes are synthetic so that a code never reads as a claim of CCN or C-ID alignment. It is the second reason the CSR minted its own.
Measured
The M-ID number band already carries the staging status: M1019 and Z9001 are ours, 100 is C-ID's, C1000 is CCN's. A shared subject code claims recognition of the subject, not approval of the course; C-ID's own codes are labels, not approvals.
Proposed
Retire it. The number carries the claim status; the subject code carries recognition. If you keep the rule, this whole sheet folds to "dismiss" and the CSR stays as it is.

10 Foreign Languages FLxx → the language codes · 1,492 M-IDs
Authority

Noise, not decisions

One namespace: everything that is not a C-ID or CCN is an M-ID

20 Retire the Z band SUBJ Z#### → SUBJ M#### · 4,024 identities
What it is
The Z ids are not the loners. They are the 4,024 identities the June 15 re-mint (Session 56) keyed from the machine-built UC-CUR-AUTO* variant clusters to SUBJ Z<band><seq>: 3,556 in the credit band, 468 noncredit, two members in the typical case, 22 at most, none single-member. The letter carried lineage: an identity born from consolidation rather than from the shared-course re-mint. The loners are the letter-suffixed SUBJ M##XX singletons (33,423 on the map), already in the M namespace.
Measured
The M numbers run M<band><seq:03d>, 999 per subject per band. The Z ids fit by filling gaps in every subject; Kinesiology's credit band is the tight one (619 numbers used, highest sequence 930, 193 to add). Live surface: 4,083 curation rows keyed by a Z id (35 of them written by people) and 10,704 merge targets pointing at one, all re-keyable by the alias-map path Session 56 built (kb/_rekey_kb_curation_supabase.py and the supabase-rekey.yml workflow). Four test suites and three scripts recognize the Z shape and change with it; SkyView labels these identities as a fourth system today and would read M-ID.
Proposed
Yes. The identity system says who owns the number: ASCCC for C-ID, the Chancellor's Office for CCN, MAP for M-ID. Where an identity came from is a row attribute, kept as origin: machine cluster, not a letter in the id. Keep the band digit (1 credit, 9 noncredit). Fold the re-key into the same re-mint series as items 5 to 16: one alias map, one cron window.
21 Fold the legacy ids the same way "M-ID SUBJ ###" → SUBJ M#### · 221 rows
What it is
221 identity rows still carry the pre-May form M-ID SUBJ ### (210 of them single-member), which the KB README already calls dead. The live curation placeholders UC-CUR-* are gone from the database (0 rows).
Proposed
Same rule, same re-mint: they become SUBJ M#### or SUBJ M##XX by their member count, through the existing alias map. After this and item 20, every identity that is not a C-ID or CCN reads M-ID, and the id parsers have one shape to know.
