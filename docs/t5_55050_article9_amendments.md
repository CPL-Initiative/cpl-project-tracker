# §55050 — the Article 9 gaps, against the text that is already final

> **All quotations here are primary-source.** Five authenticated PDFs supplied by
> Sam 2026-08-26: the operative §55050, the 2026-08-12 **final** revised reg text
> for §55050 + §55051, and EC §78093 / §78093.1 / §78093.2. The register's standing
> caveat about reconstructed statutory text does **not** apply to anything below.

---

## 1. ⭐ The finding that changes the task

**A final revised §55050 already exists** — *Final Revisions to Title 5 … Related
to Credit for Prior Learning and High School Course Articulation*, 2026-08-12. It
rewrites and renumbers §55050 wholesale and amends §55051 alongside it.

**It does not implement Article 9.** Verified by search across the full text:

| check | result |
|---|---|
| references to §78093 / Article 9 / SB 135 | **0** |
| reciprocity — accepting another campus's transcribed CPL | **0 occurrences** |
| NOTE authority / reference | §§66025.71, 66700, 70901 / §§70901, 70902, **88782** — no Article 9 |

That is not an error by its drafters. Article 9 took effect **2026-07-13** and the
accessibility stamp on the document reads **6/19/26**, so the substantive drafting
predates the statute. But the consequence stands: **the rulemaking now in flight
is silent on the CPL statute it will operate under.**

### ⚠ And it renumbers, which forecloses a from-scratch redraft

The final text moves nearly every subdivision — old (a)→(b), (b)→(f), (c)→(h),
(d)→(c), (e)→(i), (g)→(j), (i)→(k), (j)→(e), (k)→(g), (f)→(l) — and **§55051(d) is
amended in the same package to cross-reference `§55050(i)`** for credit by
examination.

So a second, independent renumbering would break the companion amendment to
§55051. **Recommendation: an amendment package layered on the final text, not a
fresh section.** It is also the faster path to the CO and the easier one to
explain: *"the statute landed after this was drafted; here is what it now needs."*

### Two real wins already in the final text, which no register row claims

- **New (d) reverses the residency exclusion.** Old (h): CPL units *"shall **not**
  be counted"* toward the 12 semester hours of residence for an associate degree.
  New (d): *"**may** be counted."*
- **New (e) broadens the §78212 trigger.** Old (j) referred only veterans,
  active-duty members, industry-credential holders, or students who asked. New (e)
  applies to every student *"as a component of their educational planning
  process."*

## 2. The four funding priorities — verbatim, and the seam

**EC §78093.2(d)(1)**, in full:

> *"Upon appropriation by the Legislature for purposes of this article, the
> chancellor's office shall allocate designated funds to support implementation of
> this article at each campus using all of the following goals:*
> ***(A)** Increasing access to credit for prior learning opportunities equitably
> for all eligible students.*
> ***(B)** Increasing completion through credit for prior learning awards.*
> ***(C)** Advancing career attainment through credit for prior learning.*
> ***(D)** Supporting credit for prior learning opportunities through the
> chancellor's office's pilot projects, such as the California Mapping Articulated
> Pathways Initiative."*

Against the live model (`cpl_funding_config`, saved 2026-08-23 — **Access 34 /
Outreach 33 / Success 33**):

| statute | model | |
|---|---|---|
| (A) increasing access | **Access** | covered |
| (B) increasing completion | **Success** | covered |
| (C) **advancing career attainment** | — | ⛔ **the gap Sam named** |
| (D) CO pilot projects, such as MAP | MAP is the pilot project | covered by construction |

Sam, 2026-08-26: *"I will account for career advancement through our CPL Projects
and leadership on CA Credential Registry and Career Passport."*

### ⭐ (d)(2) demands metrics the statute never supplies

> *"Each campus shall demonstrate that it has implemented the credit for prior
> learning initiative … **through the metrics described in paragraph (1)** before
> receiving an allocation."*

**Paragraph (1) describes no metrics. It describes four goals.** The statute
conditions every allocation on metrics that do not exist in it, so someone must
define them — and that someone is the Chancellor's Office.

That is the single highest-leverage opening in Article 9 for this project: the
Implementation Funding model's measures are the only worked, college-by-college
quantification of these four goals in existence. **Offering them as the (d)(1)
metrics is a far stronger play than arguing for a funding mechanism** (register
row #7), because it needs no new statute and no change to attendance accounting.

## 3. The Article 9 gaps in the final text, with proposed language

Numbering follows the **final** text's subdivisions.

### Gap 1 — Reciprocity. Nothing in the section. **(b)(2) is mandatory today.**

> §78093.2(b)(2): *"Each campus shall … Accept transcribed credit for prior
> learning from other campuses as credit, including for general education,
> transfer, and major preparation."*

**Proposed new subdivision:**

> *( ) Credit for prior learning transcribed by another California Community
> College shall be accepted as credit, including for general education, transfer,
> and major preparation. A district shall not require a student to repeat an
> assessment of prior learning that another community college has assessed and
> transcribed.*

⚠️ The first sentence restates the statute — worth including so the regulation is
readable on its own. **The second sentence is the part Title 5 adds**: the statute
says *accept as credit* and does not say a receiving college may not re-adjudicate.
That is register row #2's *"no secondary review"*, and it survives Article 9.

### Gap 2 — Evaluate, not merely advise. Final (e) is narrower than the statute.

> §78093.2(b)(1): *"…**evaluate** prior learning documents and credentials of all
> incoming students for the assessment and award of credit for prior learning."*

Final (e) requires a student be *"advised of credit for prior learning
opportunities and referred to the college's appropriate authority for
assessment."* **Advise and refer is not evaluate**, and the statutory duty is
unconditional.

**Proposed amendment to (e)** — additions in **bold**:

> *(e) The policies adopted by the governing board of a community college district
> pursuant to this section shall require that a student, as a component of their
> educational planning process pursuant to California Education Code section 78212,
> be advised of credit for prior learning opportunities**, have their prior learning
> documents and credentials evaluated for the assessment and award of credit for
> prior learning,** and referred to the college's appropriate authority for
> assessment of prior learning.*

### Gap 3 — The section rests on definitions the Legislature has now written.

§78093.1 defines **"credit for prior learning"** (c), **"credit recommendation"**
(d) and **"validated"** (f). §55050 uses none of them, so Title 5 and the Ed. Code
can drift on the meaning of the same words.

**Proposed:** add to (a), and add §§78093–78093.2 to the NOTE's Authority and
Reference.

> *(a) … For purposes of this section, "credit for prior learning", "credit
> recommendation", and "validated" have the meanings given in Education Code
> section 78093.1.*

### Gap 4 — Recommendations: the statute names three sources, the reg names one.

Final (c) keeps *"Colleges shall consider the credit recommendations of the
American Council on Education."* §78093.1(d) recognizes recommendations developed
by **ACE, CCC faculty discipline review groups, or local discipline faculty
experts**, *"documented in detailed exhibits for faculty credit evaluation."*

**Proposed amendment to (c):** extend the existing *shall consider* to
recommendations developed by California Community Colleges faculty discipline
review groups pursuant to §78093.2(a)(3). This is register rows #6 and #16 — and
the *"documented in detailed exhibits"* phrase is the MAP exhibit model in statute.

⚠️ §78093.2(a)(3) says campuses *"**may** adopt"* systemwide recommendations. So
the binding duty is still a Title 5 addition, not a restatement.

### Gap 5 — GE narrowed to Cal-GETC, against a broader statutory definition.

Final (c) changes the award target from *"California Intersegmental General
Education Transfer Curriculum, California State University General Education
Breadth, and local community college general education requirements"* to
**Cal-GETC** and local GE only — dropping **CSU GE Breadth**.

§78093.1(d) contemplates *"general education area credits"* without narrowing.
Cal-GETC is the live single-pattern policy, so this may well be deliberate — but
it should be a **decision**, not a silent narrowing, and it interacts with
register row #1.

### Gap 6 — The annotation clause, and what it now costs.

Final (l) keeps the flag, reworded: *"…annotated to reflect that credit was earned
**through an alternative method of assessment for** prior learning."*

Register row #9 wants it struck. **Article 9 does not require it**, and its cost
changed when reciprocity became mandatory: a flag on the transcript is friction in
an acceptance the statute now compels. Not a legal defect — a policy call worth
making deliberately while the section is open.

## 4. What is NOT a gap

Worth stating so the package is not padded:

- **§78093.2(c) intersegmental** — CSU/UC. Title 5 cannot bind them; that half
  genuinely needs the trailer bill. Register row #2's TBL leg survives, for the
  CSU/UC half only.
- **§78093.2(a)** — those are **chancellor's office** duties, not campus duties.
  They do not belong in a section that binds district governing boards.
- **§78093.2(d)** — allocation mechanics. Not §55050 material; see §2 above for
  where they do belong.

## 5. Next step

**Sam's call, and it changes the shape of the deliverable:**

1. **Amendment package on the final text** *(recommended)* — six targeted changes
   above, preserving the final text's renumbering so the companion §55051
   amendment stays valid.
2. **From-scratch §55050** — cleaner to read, but it re-opens the renumbering that
   §55051(d) now depends on, and discards two wins already in flight.

Either way, §2's **metrics opening** is worth raising with the CO in the same
conversation. It is the one place where work this project has already done answers
a question the statute leaves open.
