## The five findings that change something

**1. ⭐ Row #2's core ask is now law.** §78093.2(b)(2): *"Each campus shall …
Accept transcribed credit for prior learning from other campuses as credit,
including for general education, transfer, and major preparation."* That is
CCC-to-CCC reciprocity, mandatory, today. The row currently reads pathway
**Title 5 + Ed. Code**, instrument **§55050 · TBL** — i.e. it asks for a trailer
bill to obtain something the Legislature has already passed. **A CO reader will
catch this first.**
⚠️ Two things genuinely survive: *"no secondary review"* is not stated in terms
(the statute says accept as credit; whether that forecloses re-adjudication is
arguable, and is exactly what a memo or a Title 5 clause could settle), and the
**intersegmental** half is weaker in statute than in the TBL — (c)(1)(A) makes
CSU a *"shall collaborate … to ensure"* and UC only *"encouraged."*

**2. ⭐ Row #1's three-way award is in the statutory definition.** §78093.1(d)
defines a *"credit recommendation"* as recommending *"specific college-level
course credits, **general education area credits, or elective credits**."* That
is #1's ask, word for word, as a definition. The memo case stops resting on
§55050's *"if possible"* and starts resting on what the Legislature said a credit
recommendation **is** — which moves #1 towards #12's *"this is already the law"*
posture.

**3. ⭐ Row #14 is close to definitional now.** §78093.1(f) defines *"validated"*
as a formal determination by **faculty discipline review groups** that skills are
equivalent to CCC student learning outcomes **and** were assessed by
*"faculty discipline-approved methods … approved through established curricular
processes."* And §78093.1(c) defines CPL itself as credit for **validated**
skills. So under Article 9, prior learning that has not been identified and
verified **is not credit for prior learning**. §78093.2(a)(5) adds *"Enable,
document, and validate skills and competencies."*

**4. ⭐ MAP is named in statute, twice.** §78093.2(a) — the initiative is *"[b]uilding
upon the California Mapping Articulated Pathways Initiative"*; (a)(2) requires
*"a statewide technology infrastructure … to make credit for prior learning
opportunities visible and accessible to the public"*; (d)(1)(D) funds *"the
chancellor's office's pilot projects, such as the California Mapping Articulated
Pathways Initiative."* **Row #11 has been arguing from AB 123's Career Passport;
it now has a hook written for it.**

**5. ⚠️ And one that cuts AGAINST us — the only one, and it is on the row we call
the cleanest win.** §78093.1(f)(2) requires prior learning be *"approved through
established curricular processes and procedures."* Row **#12** argues CORs need
not enumerate CPL. A reader can lift (f)(2) as an objection: *doesn't
"established curricular processes" mean the COR?*
The answer is available and should be **pre-empted in the memo rather than met in
the room**: (f)(2) governs the **assessment method** — how mastery is judged —
not whether a course outline must list CPL as an eligible mode. But #12 is
currently written as though nothing could be said against it, and now something
can.

## ⭐ What Article 9 requires that the register does not cover at all

These are candidate **new rows**. The register was written before the statute
existed, so it has no entry for the statute's own campus duties.

1. **§78093.2(b)(1) — evaluate EVERY incoming student.** *"Before or upon
   completion of the education plans pursuant to Section 78212, evaluate prior
   learning documents and credentials of all incoming students."* An unconditional
   campus duty with a **defined trigger point**, naming JSTs, industry credentials,
   portfolios and AP/IB/CLEP. This is the largest new obligation in the article
   and **nothing in the register mentions it.**
2. **§78093.2(a)(1)(A)–(C) — identify, notify early, refer.** A systemwide
   intake process: collect prior-learning information *during exploration and
   admissions*, give **early notification**, and **refer** to the right person.
   Uncovered.
3. **§78093.2(c)(1)(C) — publish CPL in transfer pathways and, where feasible,
   ADTs.** Uncovered.
4. **§78093.2(d)(2) — the demonstrate-before-allocation gate.** *"Each campus
   shall demonstrate that it has implemented the credit for prior learning
   initiative … through the metrics described in paragraph (1) before receiving
   an allocation."* This is the Implementation Funding tab's gate, in statute —
   and its goals (**access · completion · career attainment**) are not the same
   three words the funding model uses. Worth reconciling deliberately.

## Method — and why this is the routine, not a one-off

Sam: *"Your sweep is the routine I want to be able to run on demand after edits."*

What this pass actually did, and what the routine must therefore do:

1. **Read the AREA's artifacts, not just one row's.** The finding that matters
   most (#2) is invisible from inside row #2 — it only appears when the row is
   read against a document filed at the area level.
2. **Take every row at once.** ↑/→/⚑ is a *comparative* judgment: #5 weakened
   **because** #2 became law. A per-row pass cannot see that, and it is also why
   the tied ranks (3,3 and 5,5,5) can only be fixed area-wide.
3. **Name what the statute covers that NO row does.** Four candidate new rows
   came out of the gaps, and a per-row routine has no place to put them.
4. **Report against each row's own current values** — pathway, instrument,
   ed_first, rank — so the output is a diff, not an essay.
5. **Propose; never write.** Same posture as Lane A and the drift detector.

⚠️ The per-row Lane B shipped in #1333 is the building block, not this. Its
envelope already carries the area's artifacts and the sibling rows, so the
area routine is the same call widened from one row to the set — plus a
gap-detection step that has no per-row equivalent.
