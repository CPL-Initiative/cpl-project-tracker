**Base text:** the **2026-08-12 final** revised §55050 (*Final Revisions to Title 5

**Convention below:** **bold** = added · ~~strikethrough~~ = deleted.

## 0. Two drafting constraints, and why they bind

**1. Do not re-letter.** §55051(d), amended in the same 2026-08-12 package, now
reads *"Except through credit by examination, as defined in section 55050(i)."* Any
insertion that shifts (i) breaks that cross-reference. **So the new reciprocity
clause is appended as (n)**, not inserted where it reads most naturally. That is a
deliberate trade of elegance for not breaking a companion amendment.

**2. Tier the asks — and SPLIT them.** Amendments 1–5 are **implementation of
enacted law**; each cites Article 9. Amendments 6–9 are **policy**, unchanged in
strength from the November 2025 proposal.

⭐ **DECIDED 2026-08-26 (Sam: *"go with your gut"*): file Tier 1 alone; Tier 2
follows as separate actions.** The two carry different burdens of proof — Tier 1's
is *"does Article 9 say this?"*, answerable by reading the statute; Tier 2's is
*"is this good policy?"*, a debate that was already had in November and lost.
Bundling makes Tier 1 inherit Tier 2's burden, trading a near-certain win for a
contested one. **Tier 2 is retained below, complete and ready to file separately**
— reasoning and per-item routing in
[`t5_55050_cover_argument.md`](t5_55050_cover_argument.md).

## 1. TIER 1 — implementing Ed. Code Article 9

### Amendment 1 — (b): the policy must reach the student and the system

*Restores four attributes from the November 2025 proposal.*

> (b) The governing board of each community college district shall adopt and
> publish policies pertaining to credit for prior learning. The policies developed
> pursuant to subdivision (a) shall be transparent and accessible to all
> stakeholders, and published at minimum in college catalogs **and on the district's
> or college's public internet website**. Procedures for students to attain credit
> for prior learning shall include, but not be limited to, credit by examination,
> evaluation of Joint Services Transcripts, evaluation of authenticated
> competencies, student-created portfolios, evaluation of industry-recognized
> certifications and documented trainings, and standardized examinations. **The
> policies shall further:**
>
> **(1) Provide a means for the public to explore faculty-approved credit for prior
> learning opportunities associated with specific prior learning experiences and
> with college courses listed on program pathways;**
>
> **(2) Provide a means for admitted or registered students to submit requests for
> timely review of their documented prior learning prior to selecting a program of
> study;**
>
> **(3) Provide a process to identify and notify students who may qualify for
> credit for prior learning, including during the college exploration and admission
> process; and**
>
> **(4) Utilize the Chancellor's Office systemwide credit for prior learning
> infrastructure, or supply regular and frequent updates of required data to that
> infrastructure, for reporting and to make credit for prior learning opportunities
> available on related Chancellor's Office, intersegmental, and agency partner
> systems.**

| clause | authority |
|---|---|
| (1) | §78093.2(a)(2) — *"statewide technology infrastructure … to make credit for prior learning opportunities visible and accessible to the public"* |
| (2) | §78093.2(a)(1)(C) referral; (b)(1) evaluation duty |
| (3) | §78093.2(a)(1)(A)–(B) — collection *"during the college exploration and admissions process"*; *"early notification"* |
| (4) | §78093.2(a)(2); (d)(1)(D) naming MAP; **(d)(2)**, which conditions any allocation on demonstrated implementation |

⚠️ **(4) is the one a district will push back on** — it is the only clause here
that imposes an ongoing operational duty rather than a one-time policy statement.
The answer is (d)(2): a district that supplies no data cannot demonstrate
implementation, and cannot be allocated funds. Worth stating in the rationale
rather than leaving to be argued.

### Amendment 2 — (c): three recognized sources of credit recommendations, not one

> (c) … Colleges shall **at a minimum** consider the credit recommendations
> ~~of the American Council on Education pursuant to Education Code section
> 66025.71~~ **developed by the American Council on Education pursuant to Education
> Code section 66025.71, by California Community Colleges faculty discipline review
> groups supported pursuant to Education Code section 78093.2(a)(3), and by local
> discipline faculty experts, as documented in detailed exhibits for faculty credit
> evaluation**. …

**Authority: §78093.1(d)**, which defines a credit recommendation as one *"developed
through evaluation by recognized entities, including the American Council on
Education, California Community Colleges faculty discipline review groups, or local
discipline faculty experts, and documented in detailed exhibits for faculty credit
evaluation."*

⭐ The *"documented in detailed exhibits"* phrase is the MAP exhibit model, in
statute. Register rows **#6** and **#16**.

⚠️ §78093.2(a)(3) says campuses *"**may** adopt"* systemwide recommendations. So
*shall consider* is a Title 5 addition on top of a permissive statute — defensible,
because §55050 already imposes *shall consider* for ACE on the same footing, but it
should not be described as merely restating the statute.

### Amendment 3 — (e): evaluate, not only advise

> (e) The policies adopted by the governing board of a community college district
> pursuant to this section shall require that **the prior learning documents and
> credentials of all incoming students be evaluated for the assessment and award of
> credit for prior learning before or upon completion of the education plan
> pursuant to Education Code section 78212, and that** a student, ~~as a component
> of their educational planning process pursuant to California Education Code
> section 78212,~~ be advised of credit for prior learning opportunities and
> referred to the college's appropriate authority for assessment of prior learning.
> **For purposes of this subdivision, prior learning documents and credentials
> include, but are not limited to, Joint Services Transcripts for veterans,
> reservists, and active duty members of the Armed Forces of the United States,
> industry-recognized credentials of working learners and apprentices, portfolios
> for self-directed and experiential learners, and standardized assessments,
> including advanced placement, international baccalaureate, and College-Level
> Examination Program assessments.**

**Authority: §78093.2(b)(1)**, tracked closely on purpose — the operative verb and
the document list are the statute's own.

⭐ The November 2025 draft already said *"shall be **assessed** for prior learning
opportunities"*. The final text weakened it to *advised and referred*. **Advise and
refer is not evaluate**, and the statutory duty is unconditional.

### Amendment 4 — new (n): reciprocity

> **(n) Credit for prior learning transcribed by another California Community
> College shall be accepted as credit, including for general education, transfer,
> and major preparation. A district shall not require a student to repeat an
> assessment of prior learning that another California Community College has
> assessed and transcribed.**

**Authority: §78093.2(b)(2)** — *"Each campus shall … Accept transcribed credit for
prior learning from other campuses as credit, including for general education,
transfer, and major preparation."*

⭐ **The first sentence restates the statute; the second is what Title 5 adds.**
The statute says *accept as credit* and does not say a receiving college may not
re-adjudicate. Register row **#2**'s *"no secondary review"* survives Article 9 and
is the reason this amendment is worth filing rather than leaving to the Ed. Code.

⚠️ **This clause exists in neither the November proposal nor the final text.** It
is the only genuinely new provision in Tier 1.

⚠️ **Scope is CCC-to-CCC only.** Title 5 cannot bind CSU or UC; §78093.2(c) makes
that intersegmental work a *"shall collaborate"* with CSU and an *"encouraged"* with
UC. The trailer-bill leg of row #2 survives for that half.

### Amendment 5 — NOTE

> NOTE: Authority cited: Sections 66025.71, 66700**,** ~~and~~ 70901**, and
> 78093.2**, Education Code. Reference: Sections 70901**,** ~~and~~ 70902**, 78093,
> 78093.1, 78093.2,** and 88782, Education Code.

⚠️ **`88782` vs `88792(b)(1)` — unresolved, and not resolvable from here.** The
November 2025 proposal's NOTE cites **§88792(b)(1)**; the 2026-08-12 final text
cites **§88782**; the November proposal's own background section describes
**§88782** (California Career Passport Program). Two of those three agree, which is
suggestive and not decisive. **Settle before filing.**

## 2. TIER 2 — policy, on the same merits as November 2025

⛔ **NOT part of the Tier 1 filing.** Retained here complete and ready, to be

### Amendment 6 — (j): a Credit (CR) symbol

> (j) Grading shall be according to the regular grading system approved by the
> governing board pursuant to section 55023~~, except that students shall be
> offered a "pass-no pass" option if that option is ordinarily available for the
> course~~. **A grade of Credit (CR) may be used to signify a minimum C passing
> proficiency or higher for the course or program requirement.**

**Companion amendment to §55023** adding to the symbol table:

> **CR — Credit (At least satisfactory — units awarded not counted in GPA. Used for
> credit for prior learning only and is not supplanted by any other symbol.)**

⚠️ **This one does not stand alone.** Without the §55023 amendment, §55050(j) would
reference a symbol that does not exist in the grading regulation. Register row **#3**.

### Amendment 7 — (k): no fee for alternative methods other than examination

> (k) A district **or college may not charge a student a fee for administering any
> alternative method of awarding course credit, except that a district** may charge
> a student a fee for administering an examination pursuant to this section,
> provided the fee does not exceed the enrollment fee which would be associated
> with enrollment in the course for which the student seeks credit by examination.

Basis: **ASCCC Resolution 103.04** (Spring 2025). §78093.2(d)(1)(A)'s *"equitably
for all eligible students"* is supporting, not dispositive.

### Amendment 8 — course **or program requirement**

The November proposal extended (c), (f) and (h) from *course* to *"course or
program requirement"*, so credit can attach to a program requirement rather than
only an individually identified catalog course.

§78093.1(d) recognizes **general education area** and **elective** credits, which
points the same way without compelling it. Register row **#1**.

⚠️ **The broadest change in this package** — it alters what CPL may attach to, and
touches three subdivisions. Worth filing separately if the package needs to be
small.

### Amendment 9 — international transcripts, and §55052 / §55052.5

- **(b)** add *"evaluation of international transcripts"* to the procedures list.
  ⚠️ **Weaker than in November.** AB 123 objective 2 named *"educational
  certificates or degrees earned in foreign countries"*; **§78093.1(c) does not.**
  Its list is *"including, but not limited to"*, so this is permitted — not required.
- **§55052** renamed *External Standardized Examinations*, adding Cambridge
  Advanced Level and the Defense Language Proficiency Test; **§55052.5 repealed**
  into it. Article 9 is silent. This was §55052/§55052.5 of the November package and
  is a self-contained consolidation.

## 3. What is deliberately NOT here

- **§78093.2(a) duties** — those bind the Chancellor's Office, not district
  governing boards. They do not belong in §55050.
- **§78093.2(c) intersegmental** — Title 5 cannot bind CSU or UC.
- **§78093.2(d) allocation mechanics** — not §55050 material. See below.
- **Apportionment / units-based FTES** (register row #7) — §58003.2 and §58050, and
  on the register's own reading, statute.
- **A central body awarding CPL** (row #5) — EC §70902 vests credit-granting in
  district governing boards.
- **The transcript annotation, (l)** — Article 9 is silent, so striking it (row #9)
  is pure policy and was not in the November proposal either. Its cost did change
  when reciprocity became mandatory: a flag is friction in an acceptance the statute
  now compels. **Raised, not drafted** — it belongs in a conversation, not in this
  package.

## 4. The separate ask, for the same meeting

**§78093.2(d)(2) conditions every allocation on *"the metrics described in paragraph
(1)"* — and paragraph (1) describes four goals, not metrics.** The statute supplies
none. The Chancellor's Office must define them.

The Implementation Funding model already quantifies (A) access and (B) completion
college by college, and (D) is satisfied by construction because MAP is the named
pilot project. **(C) advancing career attainment is the acknowledged gap**, being
addressed through CPL Projects and leadership on the CA Credential Registry and
Career Passport.

This needs no regulation, no statute, and no change to attendance accounting —
which is more than register row #7 can say.
