# The CCCCO style guide and brand basics

> **One-sentence summary** — The Chancellor's Office's 2025 Communications Style
> Guide sets the names, numbers, dates, punctuation and inclusive language of
> our outward writing, and its Brand Basics set the CO's colors, typefaces and
> logo rules; Sam adopted both for writing and for CO colors and identity on
> 2026-09-30, with no UI change to follow from them until he asks.

## Context

Sam, 2026-09-30, sharing the guide: *"Adding the current CO style guide for the
KB and to inform language and usage for COBI surfaces. I don't want to launch
any UI changes based on this, but I do want it used for our writing and
CO-related colors and identities."* (`cpl_memory`
`sam-cccco-style-guide-governs-writing-2026-09-30`.)

So there are two uses, and a boundary:

- **Writing.** Every letter, memo, explainer, report narrative and rendered
  sentence follows the guide's usage where it speaks. Where it is silent, our
  own rules (the house voice, the funding vocabulary, American spelling) govern.
- **CO colors and identity.** When a surface depicts the Chancellor's Office
  (its name, its seal, its colors), it uses the CO's own values below.
- **The boundary.** First Light stays the COBI palette. Nothing here licenses a
  restyle; a surface changes when Sam asks for the change.

## Names and identity

These are the rules we meet most often.

- **The office:** *California Community Colleges Chancellor's Office* on first
  reference, then *the Chancellor's Office*, which "replaces system office" (pp. 3, 6, 15). Always capitalized.
- **The system:** *California Community Colleges*, singular: "the California
  Community Colleges is the largest system of higher education in the nation"
  (p. 6). Later references: *the system*, *the community college system*, *the
  California Community Colleges system*.
- **Never CCC or CCCCO** in outward prose, including later references (pp. 2, 3,
  6: "Most Corrected Mistake" no. 4). Never *California Community College
  System*, which "is not the official name" (p. 15).
- **The colleges:** lowercase *community colleges* for the colleges as a group:
  "There are 116 California community colleges" (p. 15). A college takes its
  full official name with *College*: "Mendocino College, not Mendocino"; "College
  of the Sequoias, not Sequoia College" (p. 3).
- **Titles:** capitalized only before a name. *Chancellor Sonya Christian*; *the
  chancellor*; *Sonya Christian, chancellor* (p. 3). *Board of Governors* is
  capitalized in all cases (p. 3).
- **Divisions:** full name on first reference with the acronym in parentheses,
  the acronym after: *Educational Services and Support (ESS)* (p. 3; the list is
  "as of March 2024").
- **Programs and initiatives:** capitalized only in a formal name (p. 4). So *the
  CPL Initiative* (Sam's formal name, CLAUDE.md naming) keeps its capitals; *the
  initiative* alone does not.
- **Other organizations:** "use the language exactly as in their official title"
  (p. 14); a state agency is *the agency* on later reference, not its acronym (p. 6).
- **The name and the seal** belong to the Chancellor's Office: "Only officials of
  the Chancellor's Office may use the name and/or system seal/logo"; anyone else
  needs written permission from the communications director (p. 15). The mark is
  protected by Education Code section 71025 (Brand Basics).

## Usage in our prose

| Topic | The guide's rule | In our writing |
|---|---|---|
| Numbers | Spell out one through nine; figures from 10; spell out a number that opens a sentence (pp. 4, 15). In bulleted data, figures may replace words. | *eight colleges*, *115 colleges* |
| Percent | Figures and %, no space: *3.1%* (p. 16) | *43.24%* |
| Money | Figures with $, and *million*/*billion* spelled out: *$1.7 million* (p. 16) | *$35 million*, never *$35M* in prose |
| Fiscal and academic years | Year, hyphen, last two digits: *2018-19* (pp. 4, 11) | *2026-27* (already our form) |
| Year spans as nouns | *from 1989 to 2005* (p. 11) | *from 2026 to 2028* |
| Dates | Figures, no ordinals; abbreviate *Jan., Feb., Aug., Sept., Oct., Nov., Dec.* with a day; spell out alone or with a year only (pp. 10-11) | *Sept. 30, 2026*; *September 2026* |
| Times | *3 p.m.*, *7:30 p.m.*; *noon*, *midnight* (p. 11) | |
| Em dash | Spaced on both sides (p. 18) | *the model — and only the model — computes it* |
| Serial comma | "can now be used across all agency communications" (p. 17) | Use it, and use it consistently |
| Hyphens | No hyphen after *non*: *nonscholarship* (p. 18); none on *-ly* adverbs; *full-time* only as an adjective | *noncredit* |
| *-wide* | One word: *statewide*, *systemwide*, *campuswide* (p. 24) | |
| Acronyms | Avoid when possible; spell out on first reference with the acronym in parentheses (pp. 4, 6) | *credit for prior learning (CPL)*, *full-time equivalent students (FTES)* |
| Headlines | Title case; articles and short prepositions lowercase; "Do not use all caps" (p. 9) | |
| Degrees | Spell out; *associate degree*, *bachelor's degree*; no *degree* after an abbreviation (p. 7) | |
| Words | *adviser*, not *advisor* (p. 8); *fundraising* (p. 13); *email*, *online*, *homepage*, *login* (n.), *log in* (v.) (pp. 23-24) | |
| Course names | In quotation marks in running text (p. 23) | |
| URLs | Shortest form, no *http://*, no trailing slash (p. 24) | *cccco.edu* |
| File links | Lowercase type with a period: *Funding explainer (.pdf)* (p. 13) | |
| Phone numbers | Area code with hyphens, *XXX-XXX-XXXX*; online, *(XXX) XXX-XXXX* reads better on a screen reader (pp. 21-22) | |

**Inclusive language** (pp. 10, 12, 14, 20-21). *They* for a person who does not
specify; recast a generic *he* into the plural. Gender-neutral terms (*chair*,
*workforce*, *firefighter*). For disability, identity-first language for groups
or when a preference is unknown; *accessible parking*; never *afflicted with*,
*battling*, *suffers from*; avoid *special needs*. Capitalize *Black*,
*Indigenous*, *Latino*, *Native American*; lowercase *white*; *people of color*
only in broad references, never for one person, and never the shorthand *POC*,
*BIPOC* or *BAME* outside a quotation.

**Where the guide is silent, our rules stand.** It has no rule on *noncredit*,
*credit for prior learning*, district names, plain language, tone or register.
The house voice ([`reference-cccco-house-voice`](reference-cccco-house-voice.md)),
the funding vocabulary and American spelling (CLAUDE.md, Naming & terminology)
cover those. Where our rule is stricter, ours holds: the guide allows bullets in
outward prose, and Sam's house voice keeps bold, bullets and glyphs out of it.

**Where the guide disagrees with itself, follow the stated rule over its
example.** Its examples mix closed and spaced em dashes, omit the serial comma
in places, call a hyphen a "dash" for fiscal years, and spell out *eleven*
against its own one-through-nine rule. The rule states the guide's intent.

**How the palette meets First Light.** First Light's `--seal-blue` (#002F6D) is
the CO's Dark Blue exactly, so COBI already carries the CO's primary color.
First Light's golds (`--mustard-fill` #E3B341, `--gold-accent` #8B6800) are its
own and differ from the CO's Gold. The style guide's PDF itself uses a seal navy
of #002F6E and gold of #FEB500, one unit off the palette. When a surface depicts
the Chancellor's Office, it uses the palette's values; First Light keeps its own
roles everywhere else, and no surface changes until Sam asks.

### Typefaces (Brand Basics)

- **Crimson Text** is the primary typeface, a serif for display and headings:
  "No other serif typeface should be used in combination with or in place of
  Crimson."
- **Source Sans Pro** is the secondary, a sans-serif for display and text: "No
  other sans-serif typeface should be used in combination with or in place of
  Source Sans Pro."
- First Light already sets text in Source Sans 3 (Source Sans Pro's successor);
  its display face is Playfair Display. A CO-branded document (a letter, a memo,
  a deck in the CO's name) uses Crimson Text for headings.

### The logo and the seal (Brand Basics)

- The horizontal (one-line) logo is preferred; the vertical (stacked) version is
  for tight spaces.
- Clear space around the logo equals the height of its capital *C*.
- Never recolor it outside the approved combinations, rearrange its elements,
  pair the symbol with campaign elements or an old typeface, distort it, or add
  words to it; program lockups come from Communications.
- The seal is for formal use only (Board of Governors resolutions, official
  stationery, diplomas) and "should never be altered or recreated".
- Logo files, the "Powered By" mark and its guidelines, the fonts and the palette
  are on cccco.edu/About-Us/News-and-Media/Brand/assets.
