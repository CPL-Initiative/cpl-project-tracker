Catalog Description
Provides instruction and practice in the communication of ideas in written form. Emphasis on mastery of sentence and paragraph skills, including organization in terms of unity, support, and coherence in an effective, well-supported, one page composition.

Course Content
Course Lecture Content

      Major Topics                                      Time Required                      What Student Will Learn

 

 

1.  Journal Writing

	

12

	

Writing skills as they experiment with free-form writing freely and more expressively about topics of their own choice.




2.  Sentence Writing

	

12

	

Basics of grammar, mechanics, punctuation, and word use.




3.  Writing Process

	

24

	

Proficiency with the writing process, construct well-developed paragraphs and develop special writing skills by reading, completing activities, and taking tests.      




4.  Paragraphs

	

12

	

Paragraph development on a variety of topics.




5. Developing confidence in writing

                                   

	

12

	

Practical language and writing skills for use in everyday life.

Core writing strategies useful throughout the writing process as well as grammar and mechanics.

Effective practices in a group dynamic.

Pass/No Pass

P = Pass. Credit will be based on accurate completion of individual assignments as well as classroom participation. Students are required to complete all course requirements with a minimum average score of 60%.

SP = Satisfactory Progress. To be awarded “SP”, a student must demonstrate proficiency in some of the course learning outcomes. This is defined by completing one or more assignments, but without completing the requirements necessary to pass.

NP = No Pass. A student who receives “NP” has been in attendance but has neither participated in the course nor completed a course assignment.
