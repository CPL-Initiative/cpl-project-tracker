What methods will be employed to help students learn?
Class Discussions

The instructor will lead collaborative discussions to understand and clarify topics in financial literacy such as developing a savings plan, understanding credit and debt, and learnig the priciples for investing.



Handouts

Students will read and work from instructor-prepared handouts to demonstrate an understanding of various topics such as credit management, the economy and education, and investing.



Lecture

The instructor will teach lessons using PowerPoint presentations and other instruction tools. 



Oral Presentations

Students will plan, write and deliver a 2-5 minute speech to their classmates on a finance topic provided by the instructor.



What learning activities or assignments are required outside of class?
Reading Assignments

Students will read chapters and passages from financial literature and instructor-prepared reading materials. The instructor will discuss these readings with students.

Writing Assignments

Students will write responses to a variety to finance exercises for every session.  For example, students will plan and write a personal budget plan.
