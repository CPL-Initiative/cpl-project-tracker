Catalog Description
The Los Angeles County and Orange County Fire Chief's Physical Ability Test is designed to examine the physical ability of the individual when it comes to performing the functions or tasks of a firefighter.

Student Learning Outcomes
At the conclusion of this course, the student should be able to:
Demonstrate successful completion of all Physical Ability Test events without assistance with a passing score of 9 minutes and 34 seconds or less. 
Course Objectives
At the conclusion of this course, the student should be able to:
1. Demonstrate the physical skills, strength, endurance, and coordination required to complete all Biddle Firefighter Physical Ability Test tasks safely and within the validated time standard.
2. Apply proper techniques for each manipulative task to ensure safe, efficient, and effective performance under test and field conditions.
3. Evaluate their current level of physical preparedness through structured practice, feedback, and timed assessments.
4. Develop an individualized exercise and conditioning program designed to enhance performance, address areas of improvement, and reduce injury risk.
5. Incorporate sound nutrition and recovery strategies into a personal wellness plan that supports training, testing, and long-term occupational fitness.
Course Content
Course Lecture Content
TEST PREPARATION AND SAFETY
Students are provided with instructions to physically and nutritionally prepare themselves for optimal performance on the physical ability test.
 Students are assigned to view an instructional video and respond to questions regarding the video prior to the test.
 Nutrition, hydration, and proper handling of test equipment are stated and discussed.
 Each PAT event is described and demonstrated. Safety measures, rules, and performance criteria are stated.
Recommended body mechanics and technique for each event are stated and discussed. 
Course Lab Content
STUDENT EVALUATIONS / MANIPULATIVE ASSESSMENT
Each student is observed and scored by a Proctor throughout the test.
Upon completion of the test, whole-group feedback for strategies to maintain or improve fitness and performance is provided to the students.
Recommendations may include information about training concepts and methods, training tools, injury prevention, recovery, nutrition, test preparation, adn career preparation.

Representative Texts, Manuals, OER, and Other Support Materials:
The OER materials are the Santa Ana College Biddle Physical Ability Test (BPAT) event descriptions and instructional video. The BPAT is a standardized test that is the preferred industry entry-level physical ability standard for most Orange and Los Angeles County Fire Agencies. Thus, since the test is standardized and the test has not changed, the current instructions and test event descriptions are still valid. The BPAT is the result of an extensive content and criterion-validation study published in 1997 by Biddle and Associates, Inc, and thus the test has since remained exactly the same.
What methods will be employed to help students learn?
Class Discussions

Discuss physical ability testing and safety rules and guidelines.



Handouts

Biddle PAT event descriptions and training recommendations are made available online.



Instructor Demonstrations

Instructors will demonstrate test events, including test rules and violations, allowed techniques, safety guidelines, and correct body mechanics.



Lecture

Instructors will deliver test guidelines and criteria. Instructors will deliver feedback regarding training and physical performance after the physical ability test to help students develop an exercise and nutrition program to improve physical skills and abilities.



Other

View the Biddle Instructional Video and respond to a series of questions based on the video prior to the test.

Standards of Achievement
How will learning be assessed? (Multiple measures must be used)
Verbal response to and physical demonstration (Manipulative assessment) of the Biddle PAT events, rules, guidelines, and safety measures.
Manipulative Assessment. Evolutions shall include hose deployment, laddering, crawling, climbing, dragging, simulated chopping, carrying, and hoisting.
Grading Scale
Pass/No Pass

PASS = Successful completion of all Physical Ability Test events without assistance in a total time of 9 minutes and 34 seconds or less.

NO PASS = Failure to complete all Physical Ability Test events without assistance in a total time of 9 minutes and 34 seconds or less.
