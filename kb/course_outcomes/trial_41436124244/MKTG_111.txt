Course Content
Course Lecture Content

Segment 1 -  Nature and structure of retailing (traditional and category specialists), consumer buying behavior, marketing and the marketing segment, site selection, designing the product line and selecting the suppliers.

How a single proprietorship, chain, or retail conglomerate is organized, why and when the ultimate consumer buys, how markets are identified, the importance and procedures used for site selection, sources of both soft and hard lines carried by the store or chain.

Segment 2 -  Inventory control, logistics, supply chain, pricing and planning, advertising, and selling

The importance of good inventory control and its role in reducing shrinkage. Be able to develop policies and procedures for pricing goods, the role of technology, advertising and sales in the sale of merchandise.

Segment 3 -  Public relations, sales promotion, customer service, human relations, market research, sales forecasting, the legal environment, and the career potential of the retail industry.

The students will be familiar with each aspect of a retail store's promotional plan and how promotion completes the marketing image of a store.  The ultimate consumer is the function of market research.  Students will realize the importance of realistic sales forecasting and will have an understanding of a retail conglomerate.
