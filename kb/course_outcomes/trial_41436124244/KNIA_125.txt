Catalog Description
This class is designed to prepare the college football athletes to play offense, defense, and special teams. The focus is on skill development and conditioning through resistance training and field work. This course is offered as a half-unit or one-unit class.

Student Learning Outcomes
At the conclusion of this course, the student should be able to:
Student will be able to differentiate between sport and position specific resistence training techniques that enable and or hinder football performance improvements.
Students will be competent at performing linear and non-linear football and position specific movements.
Course Objectives
At the conclusion of this course, the student should be able to:
Students will be able to safely perform sport and position specific movements in a team setting.
Students will be able to safely perform a strength and conditioning regimen utilizing the strength lab and field work.
Students will understand the requirements of preparing for a season of competition and the rules that apply to be a member of the Santa Ana College football program.
Course Content
Course Lecture Content

1.  Course Orientation 1 hour
    a.  Overview
    b.  Rules and Procedures
    c.  Safety

II.  Fitness Level Improvement 15 hours
    a.  Free weight resistance training
    b.  Free weight circuit training 
    c.  Olympic power lifting

III.  Offense, Defense, and Special Team Field Work 32 hours
    a.  Field work will consist of sport specific drills and instruction including, multiple front and multiple 
         coverage defense. Will also consist of instruction of punt, punt return, kickoff coverage, kickoff return,
         and field goals.
