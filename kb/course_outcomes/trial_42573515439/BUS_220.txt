Student Learning Outcomes
Identify personal financial goals and develop strategies to meet those goals.
Analyze various investment options, account types, and other financial tools that can be used to help individuals reach their financial goals.

Topics & Scope:
The Financial Planning Process
a. Career choice and the effects on financial plans
b. Developing and achieving financial plans
c. Opportunity costs and the time value of money
(Obj 1, 2)
Creating Financial Statements and Budgets
a. The basics of federal income tax
b. Filing federal income tax
c. Create a personal balance sheet with assets and liabilities
d. Create a personal income and expense statement
(Obj 2)
Managing Basic Assets
a. Checking account options
b. Establishing a savings plan
c. The role of compound interest
d. Evaluating the decision to buy a new/used car or leasing
e. Evaluating the decision to buy or rent a house and other housing options
f. Factors involved in the home buying process
(Obj 3)
Using Credit
a. Establishing credit
b. Understanding the different types of credit
c. Using credit wisely/avoiding bankruptcy
d. Terms and features of loans
e. Understanding the basics of student loans
(Obj 4)
The Role of Insurance in Financial Planning
a. Evaluating the need for life insurance
b. Choosing among different types of life insurance
c. Understanding riders and terms of life insurance policies
d. Understanding the terms and types of different health insurance policies
e. Identifying the need for Long Term Care insurance
f. Estimating the need for disability insurance
g. Understanding the terms and needs for homeowners or renter's insurance
h. Distinguishing among different types and terms of automobile insurance
(Obj 5)
Financial Investments
a. Identifying different investment objectives
b. Understanding how a trade is executed and the different types of orders
c. Calculating risk and reward of investing in securities
d. Identifying different types of bonds
e. Understanding bond rating system
f. Comparing Exchange Traded Funds and Mutual Funds
g. Investing in Real Estate or Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs)
(Obj 6, 7)
Retirement & Estate Planning
a. Identifying the pitfalls of retirement planning
b. Identifying different sources of retirement income
c. Identifying among different annuity types
d. Describing reasons why an estate would break up
e. Describing different types of trusts
f. Identifying estate taxes
(Obj 8)
Assignments:
Independent Work (minimum 2 examples):
Create a personal budget using real numbers.
(Obj 3)
Critical Thinking Assignments (minimum 2 examples):
Choose a type of insurance not previously covered (health, life, or car/home) and create a presentation that identifies what activity
(Obj 5, 6)
Critical Thinking Assignments (minimum 2 examples):
Find an example of an Exchange Traded Fund (ETF) or Mutual Fund for someone looking to save for their child's college education (assume the child is 5 years old). Explain what about the fund meets the criteria and cite the source.
(Obj 1, 6, 7)
Independent Work (minimum 2 examples):
Estimate the amount of your expenses in retirement. Identify sources of income that can help you meet those. How much should you contribute now to help make that a reality? If you start five years from now, how much more will you have to contribute?
