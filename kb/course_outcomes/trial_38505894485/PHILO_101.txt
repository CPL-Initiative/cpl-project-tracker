Philosophy
PHILO 101

3.0 Units


Print

A careful and critical examination of some of the "Great Questions" philosophers have pursued from ancient times to the present concerning the nature of reality, knowledge, and value.  Some of these include: What is real?  What is human nature?  Do we have free will?  Does God exist?  What can we know?  How should we act?  What is the ideal community?  And, what is the nature of truth? Course also includes how to think philosophically.

Learning Outcomes
SLO:
Analyze and evaluate the issues and possible solutions to the "Great Questions" asked by philosophers.
Synthesize a world view based on an array of the possible solutions to the "Great Questions" asked by philosophers.
Construct arguments in support of, and in opposition to, the world views generated by the possible solutions to the questions asked by philosophers.
