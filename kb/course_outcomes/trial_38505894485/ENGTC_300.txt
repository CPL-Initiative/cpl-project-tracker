Computer-Aided Drafting
ENGTC 300

2.0 Units


Print

Introduction to the basic and intermediate features of AutoCAD, a 2D dimensional drawing and design software package. Creation of technical drawings in the AutoCAD environment using drawing and modification tools, dimensions, and text. Use of layers to separate components of technical drawings according to industry best practices. Use of viewports and layouts to organize drawing sheets and print.

Learning Outcomes
SLO:
