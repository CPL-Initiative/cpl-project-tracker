Fire Fighter 1 & 2 Academy
FIRE 0950:

22.5 Units

Print Course

        

The Fire Academy is an <b>Accredited Regional Training Program (ARTP)</b> that prepares students for Fire Fighter 1 and Fire Fighter 2 certification through the Office of the State Fire Marshal&mdash; CA State Fire Training and is&nbsp;<b>IFSAC</b> and <b>ProBoard</b> accredited, ensuring national recognition. The curriculum includes classroom instruction and hands-on training in structural firefighting, hazardous materials response, and wildland operations. Graduates are eligible to complete their Fire Fighter 2 certification; however, required work experience must be completed after graduation to finalize Fire Fighter 2 qualifications, and this must be submitted to State Fire Training.<br />

Limitations on Enrollment:

State Fire Training requires the following minimum&nbsp;standard. COS has chosen to require EMR as the lowest level due to Fire Advisory Board input and our course&nbsp;availability.&nbsp; <ul> <li>Public Safety First Aid or higher qualification (See State Fire Training Procedures Manual (May 2020) section 7.12.1.3 for requirements.)</li> <li>CPR healthcare provider certification or equivalent (See State Fire Training Procedures Manual (May 2020) section 7.12.1.3 for requirements.)&nbsp;</li> </ul>

Current EMT or EMR certification or higher

To ensure student safety and readiness, the Fire Academy requires applicants to complete the Candidate Physical Ability Test (CPAT) or an equivalent physical agility test. Firefighting demands high levels of physical strength, endurance, and coordination. This requirement helps confirm that candidates are physically capable of performing essential firefighting tasks and reduces the risk of injury during training and live-fire exercises.

Successfully complete the College of the Siskiyous physical agility test, or submit a current CPAT card.

A valid driver&rsquo;s license is required for fire academy cadets participating in field trips and off-site training to ensure they can legally and safely travel to training locations and meet professional standards expected in the fire service.

Learning Outcomes
SLO:
Apply the skills, knowledge, and abilities required in routine and emergency firefighting operations for both structure and wildland.
Explain and demonstrate the skills necessary to perform basic rescue operations including auto extrication, swiftwater, confined space, and first responder.
Demonstrate the proper methods to operate, inspect, and maintain firefighting and rescue equipment.
Explain the methods of executing basic fire prevention and fire investigation tasks.
Track daily individual progress in preparation for performance of all elements of the Physical Efficiency Battery to established standards.
Explain and demonstrate the skills necessary to perform FRA and FRO HazMat operations.
