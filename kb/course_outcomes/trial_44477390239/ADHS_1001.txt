Introduction to Human Services
ADHS 1001:

3.0 Units

Print Course

        

This course is an introduction to the broad spectrum of human services. The course includes an exploration of the history, theories of human service practice, and the impact of legislative mandates upon program planning for human service delivery. The course will present a study of the human service delivery systems with regards to policy and procedure, cultural competency, the politics and economics of the system and evaluation of the legal and social issues.

Learning Outcomes
Course Objectives:

Identify themes of human service needs and their significance within the United States.

Describe current roles and practices of the human service professional.

Apply knowledge, skill, and understanding for competent performance in human services settings including those related to policy and procedure, state and nationwide.

SLO:
Identify services provided by human service agencies.
Identify and analyze the impact of legislation on the services provided by human services agencies.
Identify and describe the various roles and skills needed by a human service worker.
Communicate perspectives relating to Human Services clearly and in a manner that shows respect and sensitivity to others.
