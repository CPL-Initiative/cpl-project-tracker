Principles of Microeconomics
ECON C2001:

3.0 Units

Print Course

        

An introductory course using microeconomic models to understand individual decisions by consumers and firms, market outcomes including market failure, elasticity, market structures, labor markets, inequality, and the impact of government policies. C-ID ECON 201.

Learning Outcomes
Course Objectives:

Perform and interpret microeconomic calculations.

Apply microeconomic models to analyze market outcomes, including market failures and government policies.

Model how consumers and firms make decisions under a variety of market structures.

SLO:
Define scarcity and discuss how scarcity impacts the choices of individuals and firms.
Utilize microeconomic models to explain how prices and output are determined in various market structures.
Apply the concept of comparative advantage to evaluate exchange decisions.
Calculate elasticity and discuss the implications of varying elasticity measurements.
