Introduction to Chemistry
CHEM 1000:

5.0 Units

Print Course

        

Chemistry 1000 is designed for general education students, students transferring into allied health and some biology programs, and as a preparation course for General Chemistry. This course will provide students an understanding of introductory chemical topics including the properties of matter, atomic structure, bonding, nomenclature, chemical reactions, energy, equilibrium, acids and bases, solutions and nuclear chemistry. C-ID CHEM 101.

Learning Outcomes
SLO:
Identify types of measurement and evaluate chemical reactions to solve related quantitative problems by using appropriate nomenclature, stoichiometry and other useful formulas.
Using the periodic table, atomic structure and chemical and physical processes, examine the states of matter, covalent and ionic bonding and intermolecular forces.
Safely perform basic laboratory techniques while observing and recording data to formulate and test hypotheses by applying the scientific method.
