Beginning American Sign Language
ASL 1020:

3.0 Units

Print Course

        

A basic course in American Sign Language (ASL). Emphasis will be on grammar, syntax, classifiers, vocabulary, expressive and receptive skills. History, culture, and current issues will also be covered. ASL is the third most commonly used language in the U.S. and meets foreign language requirements.

Learning Outcomes
SLO:
Produce beginning-level American Sign Language communication using fingerspelling, vocabulary, classifiers, and appropriate expressive and receptive body orientation.
Demonstrate effective communication through appropriate use of eye contact, body orientation, facial expression, and grammatical structures in dialogues and storytelling.
Apply culturally appropriate social behaviors in ASL interactions to support respectful and effective communication.
Analyze introductory aspects of Deaf culture and history to explain their influence on language use and community identity.
