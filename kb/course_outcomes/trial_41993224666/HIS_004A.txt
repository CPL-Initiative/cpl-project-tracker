HIS 004A - History of Western Civilization
3.0 Units

    

This course is a survey of Western Civilization from prehistoric times to 1600CE. Emphasis is placed on major political, economic, social, cultural and intellectual movements that shaped Western society.

Student Learning Outcomes
• Students will identify major ancient and medieval civilizations in the West from prehistoric times to 1600, including the Renaissance.
• Students will analyze of the major political, economic, social, cultural, and intellectual movements that shaped the Western World from prehistoric times to 1600, including the Renaissance.
