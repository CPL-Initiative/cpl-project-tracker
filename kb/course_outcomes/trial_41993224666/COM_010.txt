COM 010 - Persuasive Speaking
3.0 Units

    

This course  helps students understand, evaluate, and apply persuasive speaking concepts at both personal and societal levels.  Students learn to prepare and deliver compelling persuasive speeches and critically analyze oral persuasion appeals by others.

Student Learning Outcomes
• Apply nonverbal speaking techniques and principles in delivering an extended extemporaneous persuasive presentation while engaging a live audience.
• Use proper outline format, phrasing, content development, logical subordination, and citations for an extended argumentative speech.
• Responsibly use current research methods and tools to discover varied forms of information that are rhetorically adapted to clarify, amplify and substantiate main ideas.
