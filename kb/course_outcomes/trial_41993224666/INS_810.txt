INS 810 - Peer Tutor and Mentor Training
0.0 Units

    

This course provides students with effective learning strategies and academic assistance techniques for peer tutoring and mentoring in a community college setting. This course also emphasizes skills in communication, problem solving, critical thinking, structuring learning experience and handling challenging situations, as well as online tutoring. (noncredit course - not degree applicable)

Student Learning Outcomes
• Develop an effective assistance session plan and/or make a revision based on the individual needs of the tutee/mentee they are helping.
• Utilize effective questioning techniques and active listening skills.
• Demonstrate effective strategies and techniques that actively engage students in the online tutoring learning process.
