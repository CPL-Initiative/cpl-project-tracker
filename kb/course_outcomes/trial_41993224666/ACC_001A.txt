ACC 001A - Financial Accounting
4.0 Units

    

This is the study of accounting as an information system, examining why it is important and how it is used by investors, creditors, and others to make decisions. The course covers the accounting information system, including recording and reporting of business transactions with a focus on the accounting cycle, the application of generally accepted accounting principles, the financial statements, and statement analysis. Included are issues relating to asset, liability, and equity valuation, revenue and expense recognition, cash flow, internal controls, and ethics.
