ECON C2001 - Principles of Microeconomics
4.0 Units

    

An introductory course using microeconomic models to understand individual decisions by consumers and firms, market outcomes including market failure, elasticity, market structures, labor markets, inequality, and the impact of government policies.<br />
This course includes an online lab component which provides additional practice and deeper learning and applications<br />
This course was formerly known as ECN 001B

Student Learning Outcomes
• Apply the model of supply and demand and the concept of elasticity to analyze the price and quantity decisions and outcomes in different markets.
• Analyze the cost structure and consequent profit maximization of producers in various market structures.
• Explain desirable outcomes of a competitive market and assess government policies in dealing with market failures.
