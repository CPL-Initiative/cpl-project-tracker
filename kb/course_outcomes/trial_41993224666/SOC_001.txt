SOC 001 - Introduction to Sociology
3.0 Units

    

This course is an introduction to the field of sociology as a scientific discipline, an examination of human society from various sociological perspectives, an analysis of the relationship between personality development and the socio-cultural environment, the presentation of social institutions, and the possible influences of these on behavior. This course is also taught via distance education. C-ID SOCI 110

Student Learning Outcomes
• Identify and explain the three main theoretical perspectives in sociology: Structural Functionalism, Social Conflict Theory, and Symbolic Interactionism.
• Examine research methods used in gathering, organizing, and analyzing of data in sociology.
• Apply the sociological imagination to analyze your own personal issues in terms of social influences.
• Compare and contrast dimensions of social inequality with respect to race, class, and gender.
