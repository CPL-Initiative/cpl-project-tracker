Description:

This course builds the fundamental chemical concepts that are used in the Industrial Technology field. A conceptualized and contextualized approach is used to develop an understanding of the structure and properties of matter through a study of such topics as atomic structure, naming chemical compounds, types of chemical reactions, chemical formulas, mole, stoichiometry, chemical bonding, gases, solutions and acid-base chemistry. Emphasis is placed on practical aspects of chemistry used by technicians in advanced manufacturing roles.

Student Learning Outcomes:

CSLO 1:Understanding Fundamental Concepts - Demonstrate a solid understanding of fundamental principles and concepts in chemistry relevant to industrial technology applications.
CSLO 2: Chemical Safety and Hazard Awareness - Identify and assess chemical hazards, demonstrating knowledge of safety protocols and procedures in an industrial setting.
CSLO 3: Material Properties and Selection - Evaluate the chemical properties of materials commonly used in industrial processes, and make informed decisions on material selection based on desired characteristics.
CSLO 4: Reaction Kinetics and Thermodynamics - Understand the principles of reaction kinetics and thermodynamics as they relate to industrial processes, and apply this knowledge to optimize reaction conditions.
CSLO 5: Process Optimization - Apply chemical principles to optimize industrial processes, considering factors such as yield, efficiency, and environmental impact.
CSLO 6:Troubleshooting and Problem Solving - Develop problem solving skills to troubleshoot and resolve issues related to chemical processes in an industrial setting.
CSLO 7: Emerging Technologies In Industrial Chemistry - Stay informed about and adapt to emerging technologies and advancements in industrial chemistry that may impact industrial processes.
