Description:

This course is designed to provide students with a fundamental understanding of the techniques and tools used in automotive detailing. Students will learn how to clean, polish, and protect a vehicle's exterior and interior surfaces. The course will cover basic cleaning and detailing techniques, including washing, claying, polishing, and ceramic coating. Students will also learn about different types of detailing products and their uses. This is a hands on course that will develop practical skills through practice in a simulated detailing environment. They will learn how to use various detailing tools and equipment, such as buffers, polishers, and detailing brushes. Students will also learn about proper safety procedures and the importance of following industry standards.

Student Learning Outcomes:

CSLO 1: Describe the practical skills necessary to perform detailing services on a wide range of vehicles, Including mastering the use of detailing tools, understanding how to properly clean and care for various surfaces, and developing the ability to identify and correct common detailing problems. (PSLO 1,3,5)
CSLO 2: Demonstrate a deeper understanding of the science behind automotive detailing. This includes knowledge of chemistry, materials science, and surface physics, as well as an understanding of the different types of detailing products and how they work. (PSLO 1,3)
CSLO 3: Explain the importance of safety and environmental responsibility, including the proper safety protocols and personal protective equipment (PPE), as well as understanding how to properly dispose of detailing products and waste. (PSLO 5)
