Catalog Description
Study, design, and development of practical applications for basic cabinet construction as utilized by the wood product industry. Includes partitions, face frame, carcase, and basic door and drawer construction. Operation of woodworking machines, tools and processes, techniques, and care and suitability of tools and machines.
Student Learning Outcomes
Outcome
Identify different types of carcass construction and determine the proper use of solid wood versus sheet goods.
Develop, draw and construct a specific piece of carcass furniture which includes a drawer and a door.
Specific Course Objectives
Objective
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
Design and construct a basic cabinet, compatible with current industrial standards.
Apply construction methods relevant to current industrial standards.
Compare and contrast joinery as used in the cabinet industry.
Utilize skills necessary to safely operate basic tools, machinery and other equipment necessary to solve basic joinery, milling and construction problems.
Identify and analyze wood and man-made materials as they are related to design and function problems inherent in construction of furniture.

Assignments
Students are expected to research projects and solutions as dictated by specific assignments. Students are expected to spend a minimum of three hours per unit per week in class and on outside assignments, prorated for short-term classes.
Required Reading
Selected published articles on furniture making provided by the instructor.

Required Writing
1. Student generated, 120 page, three ring binder notebook of class handouts and class notes. The student will within a period of one week, rewrite their class notes taken in class, supplementing and augmenting such class notes with information derived from lectures, laboratory exercises, required and suggested reading. 2. Ten page documentation paper, including self-generated drawings, describing in detail the processes required to build and finish the project they chose to make.
Critical Thinking
Student project assignment involves problem solving and application of learned concepts.
Outside Assignments
Students are expected to complete their reading assignments, writing assignments, study for exams, complete take-home exams, and practice additional tool sharpening outside of class.
