Catalog Description
Provides an introduction to fire protection; career opportunities in fire protection and related fields; philosophy and history of fire protection; fire loss analysis; organization and function of public and private fire protection services; fire departments as part of local government; laws and regulations affecting the fire service; fire service nomenclature; specific fire protection functions; basic fire chemistry and physics; introduction to fire protection systems; introduction to fire strategy and tactics.
Student Learning Outcomes
Outcome
Illustrate the history of the fire service.
Compare and contrast effective management concepts for various emergency situations.
Analyze careers in fire and emergency services.
Specific Course Objectives
Objective
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
Illustrate and explain the history.
Evaluate the culture of the fire service.
Analyze the basic components of fire as a chemical chain reaction, as well as the major phases of fire.
Examine the main factors that influence fire spread and fire behavior.
List and describe the major organizations tha provide emergency response service, and illustrate how they interrelate.
Explain the scope, purpose, and organizational structure of fire and emergency services.
Differentiate between fire service training and education.
Explain the value of higher education to the professionalization of the fire service.
Define the role of national, state, and local support organizations in fire and emergency services.
Describe the common types of fire and emergency services facilities, equipment and apparatus.
Compare and contrast effective management concepts for various emergency situations.
Identify fire protection and emergency service careers in both the public and private sector.
Expain the primary responsibility of fire prevention personnel, including code enforcement, public information, and publice and private protection systems.
Develop the components of career preparation and goal setting.
Demonstrate the importance of wellness and fitness as it relates to emergency services.

Required Writing

A 4-6 page term paper prepared on any of the following subjects: 1. Past history of fire service 2. Today's fire service 3. Fire service in the future 4. The history of a specific fire department 5. A significant fire that impacted the fire service and changed practices.

Critical Thinking
Students will be able to define, understand, indentify, and describe the job tasks required of a firefighter. To safely and effectively think on their feet and to critically think during stressful emergencies.
