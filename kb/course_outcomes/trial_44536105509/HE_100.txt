Catalog Description
This course will require students to explore, analyze, personalize, and discuss the following issues as they relate to the essential components of health and wellness: nutrition, physical activity/exercise/fitness, weight control, mental health, stress and coping mechanisms, violence, substance use/abuse, sexuality, sexually transmitted infections, contraception, relationships, disease prevention, environment, health care, aging, and general public health issues. Students will gain the knowledge and skills necessary to adopt and apply lifestyle behaviors that enhance their overall health and well-being.
Student Learning Outcomes
Outcome
Apply the dimensions of wellness to their lives and explain how integrating these dimensions can increase their overall, lifelong wellness.
Identify and define preventative strategies for chronic diseases including heart disease, stroke, cancer and diabetes.
Describe how healthy behaviors contribute to promoting social wellness and public health.
Specific Course Objectives
Objective
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
Describe the 6 dimensions of wellness (physical, emotional, intellectual, social, spiritual and environmental) and their interrelationship.
Distinguish the difference between personal health and public health.
Identify concepts of the physical qualities of health, i.e: fitness, skill performance, body mechanics, posture, obesity, stress, diet, nutrition, exercise values as they relate to students' physical well-being towards lifelong wellness.
Identify programs for aiding students in becoming physically fit and more efficient in their daily life.
Describe the role of stress and mental health in health promotion and disease prevention.
Identify fitness principles and exercise program components to improve cardiorespiratiory endurance, muscular strength and endurance, flexiblity and body composition.
Evaluate the student’s attitude toward health, physical activity, physical dependency, substance abuse, stress induced behavior, building self esteem and relationships to develop a better understanding of one's social, intellectual and emotional well being.
Apply the dietary recommendations to diet planning in the promotion of weight management, physical fitness and disease prevention.
Examine individual and family health behaviors in relation to human sexuality, relationships, sexual orientation, and parenting.
Identify preventative measures for various diseases, infections, cancer, communicable, and cardiovascular diseases in order to acquire a better appreciation of the social, emotional and intellectual implications they have on one’s quality of health.
Describe the role of substance use, misuse, and abuse in our society and its impact on the individual, family, community, economy, and social structure.
Examine factors that lead to violence, substance abuse, addiction and preventative strategies
Identify sexual health practices and methods of contraception related to preventing sexually transmitted infections and unintended pregnancies.
Describe stimuli leading to types of violence and strategies to minimize its occurrence and promote personal safety.
Identify common practices and attitudes that contribute to intentional and unintentional injuries on a personal and community level and strategies that would reduce their occurrences.
Examine the physiological, emotional, psychological, and sexual aspects of aging.
Examine the connection between eating disorders and body image, and the influence of the media.
Describe the connection and correlation between human beings and their environment.
Analyze the health care system, including inequities and discrepancies.
Interpret and evaluate health and medical information from general and subject-specific library and credible Internet sources.
