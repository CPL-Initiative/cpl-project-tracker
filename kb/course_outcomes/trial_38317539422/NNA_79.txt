Course Description
Prerequisite: None
Course Credit Recommendation: Degree Credit


An introduction to the structure and use of medical terms used in various healthcare professions related to each body system. The course will provide healthcare professional students with an overview of basic anatomy; physiology; pathology; and diagnostic and treatment procedures related to each body system. The course will foster application-based learning and expand students’ critical thinking skills.

Course Objectives:
Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to demonstrate the following activities:

Define the meaning of medical terminology word roots, suffixes and prefixes used in a variety of healthcare professions.
Recognize and understand basic medical terms used by healthcare professionals.
Correctly spell basic medical terminology.
Analyze unfamiliar terms using the knowledge of medical terminology word roots, suffixes and prefixes.
Apply medical terms to healthcare scenarios and case studies.
Student Learning Outcomes:
Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to demonstrate the following skills:

Understand and apply medical terms used by a variety of healthcare professionals in the provision of quality, safe, patient-centered care.
Participate in collaborative relationships through the understanding and application of a common language used by healthcare professionals in a variety of settings.
Incorporate critical thinking principles into apply the use of medical terms in various healthcare scenarios.
