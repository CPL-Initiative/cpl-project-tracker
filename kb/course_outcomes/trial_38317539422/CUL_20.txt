Course Description
Prerequisite: None
Course Credit Recommendation: Degree Credit


Study of the effects of the essential ingredients and techniques of baked products; pour and drop batters; soft and stiff doughs; baking times, temperatures, and handling techniques. (Materials fee: $65.00)

Course Objectives:
Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to demonstrate the following activities:

List the properties for each baking ingredient.
Calculate formulas for all types of dough, batters, icings, and fillings.
Identify, utilize and care for equipment and tools through experience and supervision.
Demonstrate the ability to use weights and measures.
Identify standards of quality for baked goods.
Demonstrate mixing and baking procedures and operational procedures.
Produce all types of yeast and sweet goods.
Prepare baking and pastry items in a time-restricted, quality-minded setting.
Prevent food poisoning and food-borne diseases by exercising proper hygiene, food handling and storage habits.
Student Learning Outcomes:
Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to demonstrate the following skills:

Define basic ingredients and formulas in preparing baking products.
Apply skills in correcting baking failures, develop recognition of high-quality baked products and evaluate errors made in baking mixtures and products.
Develop manipulative skills necessary for employment in bake shops, school cafeterias, convalescent hospitals, community feeding programs, and commercial foods services.
