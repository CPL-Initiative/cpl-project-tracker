Course Description
Prerequisite: None
Course Credit Recommendation: Degree Credit


An introduction to professional practices in commercial and advertising photography. Students will learn how to understand and navigate the commercial market and create commercial photography clients such as; magazines, advertisements, architecture, and photographic illustrations. Materials fee applies.

Sample Assignments:
Outside-of-Class Reading Assignments

In the book Photography, read chapter eight, Setting Up A Digital Darkroom. Using information provided in the chapter, determine what your workflow would involve based upon: type of file capture, organization pattern, choice of editing software, choice of output, and archival plan.
Outside-of-Class Writing Assignments

Identify a scenario in which a model release would be required for future reproduction of the photographs that are being taken. Develop a thorough, legally-binding model release appropriate to that scenario.
Other Outside-of-Class Assignments

In a team of three or four students from this class, identify a location and time of day at which you will conduct a shoot that includes a model and set planning. Decide what lighting equipment may be needed. Recruit a model and arrange for costuming if appropriate. Complete the shoot. Execute post-production of the photographs and produce a large format inkjet print using an appropriate printing profile. The team will present the final work to the class detailing what aspect of the shoot was handled by each team member and what you learned from the experience.
