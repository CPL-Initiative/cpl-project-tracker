Course Description
Prerequisite: None
Course Credit Recommendation: Degree Credit


The Volunteer Income Tax Assistance (VITA) program is an initiative sponsored by the Internal Revenue Service. This course is intended to provide students with the opportunity to serve qualified individuals. VITA sites offer free tax help to people who need assistance in preparing their own tax returns, including: people who generally make $57,000 or less; people with disabilities; and limited English-speaking taxpayers. Teaches students about income tax preparation, prepares students to apply for IRS VITA certification, in order to work with individuals and families with limited incomes to prepare tax returns - enabling them to receive proper tax credits and refunds. Completion of this course will allow students to volunteer, providing free, high-quality income tax service.

Methods of Instruction:
Methods of instruction used to achieve student learning outcomes may include, but are not limited to, the following activities:

In-class discussions which describe income tax concepts and their application to clients.
Detailed discussion about the standards of conduct, testing, and certification.
Cooperative learning to reinforce concept usage and encourage students to apply this knowledge to interpret tax concepts within computerized tax software.
Basic comprehensive problems and exercises.
Practice using the tax preparation software.
Practice using the interview tips in publication 4012, volunteer resource guide.
Methods of Evaluation:
Students will be evaluated for progress in and/or mastery of student learning outcomes using methods of evaluation which may include, but are not limited to, the following activities:

Detailed verbal testing of the understanding of standards of conduct.
Proficiency with basic income tax forms, assessment, and data input.
Comprehensive assessment using case studies to determine understanding of income tax law and regulations.
