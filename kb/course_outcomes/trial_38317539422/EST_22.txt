Course Description
Prerequisite: None
Course Credit Recommendation: Degree Credit


A study of California Building and Mechanical Codes used for construction, maintenance, and use of buildings and grounds within the State. Emphasis on understanding and application of code sections and provisions and the relationships between building and mechanical codes.
Entrance Skills:
Before entering the course, students should be able to demonstrate the following skills:

Course Objectives:
Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to demonstrate the following activities:

Apply the mechanical code in construction, regulation, and design.
Achieve professional certification by passing applicable examinations
Achieve skills for employment in private or public construction fields, or become inspector or plans examiners.
Apply codes in construction designs.
Interpret and apply appropriate knowledge of combustion and air circulation in gas systems as per mechanical code.
Student Learning Outcomes:
Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to demonstrate the following skills:

Cite code sections and apply provisions with relation to mechanical codes in construction, regulation and design.
Interpret and apply appropriate knowledge of combustion and air circulation in a gas systems as per mechanical code.

Methods of Instruction:
Methods of instruction used to achieve student learning outcomes may include, but are not limited to, the following activities:


Lecture and Discussion of current California code requirements for new residential construction
Individualized Study of codes in preparation of weekly quizzes
Participation in group discussion comparing past and current building practices 
Presentation and use of current construction tools and materials as used by industry
Participation in the practice of sample form and document submission, as would be required by the state 
Technology-based instruction as practice for use of current industry software and for preparation of online certification exams
