Course Description
Prerequisite: AUT-50 and AUT-6A
Advisory: AUT-8A
Course Credit Recommendation: Degree Credit


Prepares students to inspect, service, diagnose, and repair hybrid and electric vehicle systems. Topics include: high voltage safety, hybrid and electric vehicle propulsion systems, battery technology, and peripheral systems used on hybrid and electric vehicles. Emphasis placed on safely working around and repairing high voltage systems.
Entrance Skills:
Before entering the course, students should be able to demonstrate the following skills:

Identify the components of major automotive systems and explain the functions of each.
AUT-50 - Identify the components of major automotive systems and explain the functions of each.
Properly use a variety of electrical system test equipment.
AUT-6A - Properly use a variety of electrical system test equipment.
Read and interpret electrical wiring diagrams.
AUT-6A - Read and interpret electrical wiring diagrams.
Diagnose and repair electrical and electronic circuits.
AUT-6A - Diagnose and repair electrical and electronic circuits.
Test and diagnose engine performance related components.
AUT-8A - Test and diagnose engine performance related components.
Course Objectives:
Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to demonstrate the following activities:

Describe safe practices when working around high voltage.
Demonstrate how to safely inspect high voltage gloves prior to use.
Describe the process to safely power down high voltage systems found in hybrid and electric vehicles.
Identify components used in hybrid and electric vehicle systems.
Describe different types of battery technology used in hybrid and electric vehicles.
Discuss different types of traction motors used in hybrid and electric vehicles.
Perform basic maintenance on hybrid and electric vehicle systems.
Demonstrate ability to safely remove and replace high voltage battery packs.
Student Learning Outcomes:
Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to demonstrate the following skills:

Demonstrate personal and shop safety procedures needed to safely work with high voltage systems.
Demonstrate knowledge of hybrid and electric vehicle component operation.
Describe basic maintenance procedures related to hybrid and electric vehicles.
Identify proper diagnostic and repair procedures as the relate to basic hybrid or electric vehicle faults.

Methods of Instruction:
Methods of instruction used to achieve student learning outcomes may include, but are not limited to, the following activities:

Presenting lectures and demonstrations by instructor or guests on hybrid/EV automobile maintenance and repair procedures.
Utilizing multi-media presentations to demonstrate and simulate complex concepts and procedures as they relate to hybrid/electric vehicle systems.
Assigning small group and individual class exercises that enable students to work cooperatively as a team to analyze and repair basic hybrid/EV electrical problems under the supervision and guidance of the instructor.
Reading assignments related to various hybrid/EV systems.
Assigning special projects outside of class that expose students to environments, tools and methods not available in class.  The projects may include field trips or research projects related to the hybrid/EV service and repair industry.
Assigning reading outside of textbook material; these may include industry publications, trade magazines, product specifications or equipment manuals related to hybrid/electric vehicles.
Methods of Evaluation:
Students will be evaluated for progress in and/or mastery of student learning outcomes using methods of evaluation which may include, but are not limited to, the following activities:

Hands-on problem-solving exercises that demonstrate students’ ability to assess hybrid/EV maintenance and service requirements.
Practical exercises and laboratory assignments to demonstrate students’ comprehension and application of procedures and techniques related to the maintenance and repair of hybrid and electric vehicle systems.
Tests and quizzes that regularly assess students’ comprehension of concepts related to hybrid and electric vehicle systems.
Group projects/presentations relating to the hybrid and electric vehicle technologies.
Participation in classroom discussion/projects.
