Ethics: Moral Issues in Contemporary Society
PHIL 105:

3.0 Units


        

An introduction into the nature of ethics and to the question as to what ethical system, if any determines right and wrong. This course will not only examine certain traditional approaches to ethical questions, but will investigate the possible application of these approaches to current ethical conflicts. An analysis of moral issues, such as violence, sex, war, abortion, capital punishment, privacy, oppression, pornography, and euthanasia will be emphasized. (C-ID: PHIL 120)  (UC, CSU, AVC)
