Beginning Weight Lifting
KINF 107:

1.0 Units


        

This course is designed for the student who wishes to learn the basic skills and techniques of weight lifting. The student will be required to learn how to design and implement a weight lifting program. The course will also cover the proper warm-up and safety techniques that are necessary when in the weight room. (UC, CSU, AVC)
