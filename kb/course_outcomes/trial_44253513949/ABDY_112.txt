Basic Auto Body Repair
ABDY 112:

5.0 Units


        

Intended for students interested in the collision repair industry. Introductory to intermediate topics will be covered. Topics will include safety, auto body equipment, basic auto body techniques, corrosion protection, and proper use of tools and equipment. (AVC)
