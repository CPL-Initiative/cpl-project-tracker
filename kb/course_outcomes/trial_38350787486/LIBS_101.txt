Catalog Description
This course provides an overview of information resources and the skills required to use them effectively. Emphasis is placed on locating, navigating, and evaluating various information resources and the applicability of research skills in both personal life and future academic endeavors. Topics include examining library resources such as print and electronic indexes, books, and periodicals; electronic databases; online and in-person library services; effective internet searching; and developing research strategies. This course is intended for students who wish to acquire research skills for academic, career, or personal use.
Short Desc (100 Characters or Less)
How to find, evaluate, and use library and other paper and electronic information resources.

Evaluation Method
WRITTEN ASSIGNMENTS: Most Modules in this course require a written Assignment, to be e-mailed to the instructor as an attachment using Word. The Research project is the focus of the course, and each Assignment contributes to its completion. The purpose of the project is to follow a topic through the research process, up to the point of writing a paper. 

QUIZZES: The Syllabus Quiz covers items explained in the syllabus. The Midterm Quiz covers terms and concepts covered in lectures and in assigned readings. 

ACTIVE PARTICIPATION: is expected during class discussions. Students are expected to post to the discussion board each time required by a Module. 

EVALUATION: will be based on student performance on the tests, active participation in the discussion boards, and the research project.
