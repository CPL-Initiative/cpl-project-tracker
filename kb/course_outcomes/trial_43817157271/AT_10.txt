AT 10: Introduction to Automotive Technology
4.0 Units / LEC-LAB

This career and technical education (CE) class develops skills needed for entry level employment in the automotive field. A variety of instructional methods are used, including hands on projects, group activities, assignments, lecture, and discussion that integrate both academic and CE standards.

Transferability:

Transferable to CSU only

Grading Options:

Letter Grade Methods

Student Learning Outcomes

1. Research and perform routine maintenance on a modern vehicle.

2. Exhibit proficiency with precise measurement instruments.

3. Perform routine inspections and repairs on a modern vehicle.
