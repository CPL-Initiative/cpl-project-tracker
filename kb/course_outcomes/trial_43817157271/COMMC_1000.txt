COMM C1000: Introduction to Public Speaking
3.0 Units / LEC

In this course, students learn and apply foundational rhetorical theories and techniques of public speaking in a multicultural democratic society. Students discover, develop, and critically analyze ideas in public discourse through research, reasoning, organization, composition, delivery to a live audience and evaluation of various types of speeches, including informative and persuasive speeches. 

Note: This course was previously known as COMM 1: Public Speaking.

Transferability:

Student Learning Outcomes

1. Research, construct and deliver effective informative and persuasive speeches using accepted classical and rhetorical practices, models and theories of communication.

2. Critically analyze oral communication, both as speakers and listeners.

3. Demonstrate ethical responsibilities in the communication process, including issues such as diversity, credibility, authority, and academic honesty.
