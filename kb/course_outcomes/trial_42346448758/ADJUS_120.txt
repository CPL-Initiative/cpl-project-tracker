Introduction to Administration of Justice
ADJUS 120:

3.0 Units

This course is designed as a career development course that will prepare the student for a career in public safety by examining the history and philosophy of justice as it evolved throughout the world. The course will also provide an overview of America&#39;s criminal justice system and its sub-systems; roles and role expectations of criminal justice professionals; theories of crime causation, punishment and rehabilitation; ethics, and multicultural awareness and education.
