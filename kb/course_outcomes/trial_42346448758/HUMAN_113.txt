African American Humanities
HUMAN 113:

3.0 Units

This course presents an integrated and historical study of African American culture and African Americans' influence upon American life. Emphasis will be upon philosophy, fine and applied arts, and ways by which they reflect the social, political, and economic realities of a culture.

Student Learning Outcomes
1. Students will identify major African American cultural works.
2. Students will exhibit their knowledge and research findings of African cultural art and how these values relate to African American art values and styles.
3. Students will analyze and contextualize cultural artifacts.
4. Students will demonstrate the importance of African American cultural expression in the American context.
