Library and Information Research Skills
LIBST 110A:

1.0 Units

This is a performance-based course that introduces students to a variety of print and electronic information resources. Students will learn how to find, use and select the most appropriate sources for different information needs, utilize an effective search strategy and evaluate search results. Students will become “information competent.” This course may be offered in lecture format or online format.
