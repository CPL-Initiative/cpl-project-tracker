Principles of Microeconomics
ECON C2001:

3.0 Units

An introductory course using microeconomic models to understand individual decisions by consumers and firms, market outcomes including market failure, elasticity, market structures, labor markets, inequality, and the impact of government policies. (Formerly: ECON 221: Introduction to Microeconomics Principles)

Student Learning Outcomes
1. Students will demonstrate the ability to assess and evaluate microeconomics conditions and microeconomics outcomes.
2. Students will demonstrate the ability to utilize Micro-Economic models to anticipate possible micro-level policy actions and potential micro-level government intervention strategies.
3. Students will demonstrate the ability to analyze Micro-economic trends to anticipate possible micro-level policy actions and potential micro-level government intervention strategies.
