Introduction to Astronomy
ASTR C1001:

3.0 Units

This course introduces fundamental concepts of astronomy, including the Solar System, stars, supernovae, galaxies, black holes, and the expanding universe. Students learn how to study the cosmos and what the latest discoveries reveal about the origins and fate of the universe.<br /> <br /> This course increases a student&#39;s knowledge of unaided eye observations of astronomy, the human endeavor to know more about astronomy, specifically: the solar system, stars, galaxies, and the universe. (Formerly: ASTRO-120: Elementary Astronomy)
