Need for the Course

This course is designed to assist the fire cadets in maintaining their physical fitness during the basic fire academy. This course provides the physical fitness certification required by local fire agencies at the time of hire. This course is associate degree applicable.

Goals for the Course
To assist cadets in developing a personal commitment to a physical fitness program as it relates to the fire service on and off duty.
To provide training and practice time to develop the skills necessary for the final physical agility examination.
To maintain and improve the physical fitness of the cadets to reduce the occurrence of injury.
To prepare the cadet for the physical demands of the emergency services workforce.
Catalog/Schedule Description
Physical fitness exercise, team work, disciplined precision cadence drilling and preparation for the fire agility physical fitness testing requirement for fire academy cadets. Graded on a Pass or No Pass basis only.
Student Learning Outcomes:
Upon satisfactory completion of the course, students will be able to:
Demonstrate the ability to participate in a physical fitness program, including both anaerobic and aerobic activities.
Improve or maintain one's current fitness level.
Perform a variety of fitness drills related to standardized testing for Firefighters.
Course Objectives
Upon satisfactory completion of the course, students will be able to:
Demonstrate the ability to follow standardized team disciplined drills.
Demonstrate the ability to participate in a physical fitness program, including both anaerobic and aerobic activities.
Participate in training which requires a) the ability to lift objects weighing a minimum of 50 lbs. from the ground to a minimum shoulder height; b) hand eye coordination; c) leg strength and coordination; d) grip strength; e) arm and upper body strength; f) correct body weight distribution and g) shoulder strength.
Successfully complete the follwoing stations of the physical agility test within the required individual and cumulative times: hose stack, stair climb, obstacle course, hose drag, and hose reposition.
