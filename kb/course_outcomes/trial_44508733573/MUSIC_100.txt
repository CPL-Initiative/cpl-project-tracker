Need for the Course

MUSIC 100 satisfies IGETC Area 3-Arts and Humanities, CSU GE Area C1.  It is also UC, CSU, and Associate Degree Applicable. MUSIC 100 applies to the AA in Music.     

Goals for the Course

This class provides an opportunity for non-music students to develop a broader understanding of music and its elements: rhythm, pitch, notation, theory, and harmony. It also satisfies general education requirements. For music students that need fundamental theory training, this class may be taken in preparation for MUSIC 101 and counts towards the AA in Music.

Catalog/Schedule Description
An introduction to the elements of music, including study of the staff, clefs, key signatures, scales, time signatures, notation, meter and rhythm, chords, basic song writing techniques, and application of theory at the keyboard.
Student Learning Outcomes:
Upon satisfactory completion of the course, students will be able to:
Identify and notate Western pitches by letter name and on a music staff
Construct scales idiomatic to Western Common Practice Era music
Construct triads idiomatic to Western Common Practice Era music
Translate basic written notation to the piano keyboard, and notes on a keyboard to notated music
Course Objectives
Upon satisfactory completion of the course, students will be able to:
Identify and explain the basic elements of notated music in the Western Common Practice Era tradition.
Notate and read pitches in multiple clefs.
Read scale patterns and chords.
Translate basic written notation to the piano keyboard.
Identify and construct time signatures.
Identify and construct key signatures.
