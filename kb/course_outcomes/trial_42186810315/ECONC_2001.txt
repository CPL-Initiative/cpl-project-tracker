ECON C2001 - Principles of Microeconomics

Units: 4.0

Print
Description:

An introductory course using microeconomic models to understand individual decisions by consumers and firms, market outcomes including market failure, elasticity, market structures, labor markets, inequality, and the impact of government policies.

Student Learning Outcomes:
1. Identify and apply fundamental microeconomic concepts.
2. Demonstrate logical and critical thinking in the context of microeconomics.
3. Recognize the factors of economic theory that impact our world at the micro level.
4. Apply and debate abstract microeconomic concepts.
