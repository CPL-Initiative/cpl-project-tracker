FIR 101 - Fire Protection Organization

Units: 4.0

Print
Description:

This course provides an introduction to fire protection. Topics covered include career opportunities in fire protection and related fields, fire loss analysis, organization and function of public and private fire protection services, and laws and regulations affecting the fire service. Students will be introduced to fire service nomenclature, basic fire chemistry and physics, fire protection systems, and fire strategy and tactics.

Student Learning Outcomes:
1. Describe the educational requirements, duties, and training sources for various occupations in fire protection.
2. Identify the basic components of fire as a chemical reaction, the major phases of fire, and the main factors that influence fire spread and fire behavior.
3. Identify the major organizations that contribute to fire protection and define the purpose and scope of fire departments.
4. Identify the types of common fire department apparatus, equipment, and personal safety equipment used for fire fighting.
5. Analyze fire fighting strategy and tactics and describe the basic tenets of fire fighter safety and survival.
