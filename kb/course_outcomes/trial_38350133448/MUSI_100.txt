CATALOG COURSE DESCRIPTION:
This course is designed to develop aural and analytical musical skills. Emphasis is placed on conceptual, contextual, and stylistic elements of music from various periods and cultures, and encompassing a range of genres and styles. This course is designed to support students in all majors who are interested in satisfying the general education requirements for Arts and Humanities.

STUDENT LEARNING OBJECTIVES:
Upon successful completion of the course the student will be able to:
Upon successful completion of the course the student will be able to:
Use appropriate vocabulary to describe the basic concepts of music.
Recognize aurally and identify the basic elements of music.
Compare and contrast stylistic differences, including basic music elements and structural characteristics, of music forms and genres from various cultures and eras.
Recognize aurally and identify the distinguishing characteristics of specific genres of music.
Analyze musical styles and genres in relation to their cultural, historical, political, socio-economic or other contexts.

B. Reading Assignments:
Reading assignments are required and may include, but are not limited to, the following:
Assigned textbook related to music appreciation.
Musical reviews from newspapers, magazines and/or online sources.
Concert program notes.
C. Writing Assignments:
Writing assignments are required and may include, but are not limited to, the following:
Essays relating emotinal responses to musical stimulae.
Summaries of historical musical figures.
Concert reviews critiquing performance.
Brief reports on recordings identifying various basic musical elements.
D. Appropriate Outside Assignments:
Outside assignments may include, but are not limited to, the following:
Listening to musical recordings.
Researching musically relevant historical figures.
Attending concerts.
Reviewing concerts attended.
Preparing a presentation on a piece of music.
Preparing essays and reports.
E. Appropriate Assignments that Demonstrate Critical Thinking:
Critical thinking assignments are required and may include, but are not limited to, the following:
Analyzing live performances.
Analyzing specific musical compositions.
Analyzing and explaining one's own taste in music using musical terminology.
