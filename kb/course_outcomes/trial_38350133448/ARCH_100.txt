CATALOG COURSE DESCRIPTION:
This course is designed for all students who wish to develop basic hand drafting skills for use in Architectural, Construction, and Interior Design programs. Topics include lettering, line control, sketching, use of drafting instruments and the development of architectural presentation drawings. This course is designed for students interested in developing hand drawing skills for use in architecture, interior design, and construction.

STUDENT LEARNING OBJECTIVES:
Upon successful completion of the course the student will be able to:
Upon successful completion of the course the student will be able to:
Use architectural terms to read, label and explain architectural drawings.
Select and use the correct tools to letter and draft construction documents.
Use a variety of scales to draft plans, sections, elevations, details and schedules.
Employ architectural lettering to label drawings.
Produce line types and weights to demonstrate line quality.
Prepare and present drawings and explain their function, logic, organization and composition.
Product drawings using pencil, pen and ink.
Apply shade and shadow theory and technique to architectural drawings.

Writing assignments are required and may include, but are not limited to, the following:
Maintenance of an architectural notebook including student comments.
Brief critiques of examples of architecture, interior design, landscape architecture, urban planning and building construction technology.

E. Appropriate Assignments that Demonstrate Critical Thinking:
Critical thinking assignments are required and may include, but are not limited to, the following:
Plan and compose a portfolio of drafting and lettering assignments.
Assess and revise drafting and lettering assignments.
Classify line, lettering and drawing types in order to produce a coherent set of construction and presentation drawings.
