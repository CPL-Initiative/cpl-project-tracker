CATALOG COURSE DESCRIPTION:
This course provides an overview of information resources and the skills required to use them effectively. Emphasis is placed on locating, navigating, and evaluating various information resources and the applicability of research skills in both personal life and future academic endeavors. Topics include examining library resources such as print and electronic indexes, books, and periodicals; electronic databases; online and in-person library services; effective internet searching; and developing research strategies. This course is intended for students who wish to acquire research skills for academic, career, or personal use.

STUDENT LEARNING OBJECTIVES:
Upon successful completion of the course the student will be able to:
Upon successful completion of the course the student will be able to:
Describe information services available in a library.
Develop search strategies in order to effectively locate information.
Compare and contrast different search methodologies (i.e., free text versus controlled vocabulary searches).
Evaluate retrieved information in order to determine the most appropriate material to use.
Document utilized information in order to communicate where the information was found.
Search appropriate research tools in order to locate relevant information.

Reading assignments are required and may include, but are not limited to, the following:
Required textbook(s)

C. Writing Assignments:
Writing assignments are required and may include, but are not limited to, the following:
A paper which details a student-designed search strategy to find information on a specific topic
Critiques of different reference books, electronic databases/indexes, or other library resources
Summaries of information found as part of library research exercises
D. Appropriate Outside Assignments:
Outside assignments may include, but are not limited to, the following:
Studying course readings
Preparing search strategies
Researching topics in the library
Preparing summaries or critiques of information resources
Completing workbook or other exercises
E. Appropriate Assignments that Demonstrate Critical Thinking:
Critical thinking assignments are required and may include, but are not limited to, the following:
Comparing different information resources to determine relevancy and usefulness
Evaluating retrieved information
Assessing the best search strategies to use to locate information
Critiquing and summarizing assigned materials
Considering inclusion, diversity, equity, and/or accessibility when evaluating resources
