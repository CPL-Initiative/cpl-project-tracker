Introduction to Accounting: Bookkeeping Concepts
ACCT 151A:

4.0 Units

Covers practical accounting, terminology, and the complete accounting cycle from documentation through journals, ledgers, and financial statements for both service and merchandising companies. Designed for careers in the field of accounting as well as business owners who wish to prepare their own accounting records. May be offered in a distance-learning format.

Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, students should be prepared to:
1. Analyze and synthesize information necessary to complete the full accounting cycle.


2. Construct fully classified financial statements for a sole proprietorship in a service and/or merchandise business.
