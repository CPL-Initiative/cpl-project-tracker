Beginning Color on Metal
ART 10A:

1.0 - 3.0 Units

Investigates concepts of color with metal and technical applications of color to metal. Color technologies may include patina, vitreous enamels, resins, felt pens, color pencil, and or combination of colored metal i.e., anodized aluminum, niobium, colored tin and re-purposed metal. May be offered in a distance-learning format.
