Introduction to Criminal Justice
CJ 1:

3.0 Units

Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, students should be prepared to:
1. Analyze and explain the historical development of the criminal justice system and its components.


2. Explain the interrelationship of the various subsystems within the justice system


3. Explain the various theories of crime causation.


4. Compare and contrast various theories of punishment and rehabilitation in the justice system.
