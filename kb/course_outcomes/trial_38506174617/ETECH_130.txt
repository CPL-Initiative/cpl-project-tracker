Introduction to Creo and Solidworks
ETECH 130:

3.0 Units

Covers an introduction to the fundamental operating principles of the Creo and Solidworks software applications. Uses Creo and Solidworks for Windows to create and revise drawings. This is a foundation course that can lead to advanced study in a variety of drafting and design fields. Some of the class hours for this course may be scheduled as To Be Arranged (TBA). May be offered in a distance-learning format.
