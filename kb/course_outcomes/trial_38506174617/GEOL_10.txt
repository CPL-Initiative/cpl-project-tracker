Physical Geology
GEOL 10:

4.0 Units

Introduces the phenomena and basic principles of physical geology with laboratory exercises illustrating the nature of minerals, rocks, geological structures and processes such as surface water, earthquakes, volcanoes, and glaciers, and the use and interpretation of topographic and geologic maps. May be offered in a distance-learning format.

Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, students should be prepared to:
1. Analyze and identify unknown nonsilicate and silicate minerals and igneous, sedimentary and metamorphic rocks to determine origin, general geologic history, economic value and age.


2. Assemble a comprehensive list of internal and external processes and demonstrate how they relate to the rock cycle, earth resources, plate tectonics, land-form evolution and geologic hazards.
