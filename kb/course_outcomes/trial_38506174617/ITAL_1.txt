Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, students should be prepared to:
1. Interact with sympathetic speakers of Italian in the present tense, demonstrating a working knowledge of structures and vocabulary used to express personal information, activities, and preferences.


2. Write descriptive sentences on familiar topics with simple syntax in the present tense.


3. Identify basic ideas in descriptive passages about familiar cultural topics written in the present tense.
