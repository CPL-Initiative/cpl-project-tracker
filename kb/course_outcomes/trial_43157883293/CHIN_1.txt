Catalog Course Description
This introductory course is designed to give the student thorough and intensive practice in speaking and listening to Chinese and reading and writing Chinese characters. The course will focus on communicative competence in situations relating to daily routines, home life, college life, and everyday activities such as meeting and describing people. Students are introduced to the culture of Chinese speakers in China and in other countries. This course may be offered in a distance education format.

Education
Objectives
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

Discuss, in Chinese, the culture of Chinese speakers in China and around the world, including home, holidays, education, foods, and leisure activities.

Ask and answer questions in Chinese.

Pronounce Chinese words and phrases with 70% accuracy and understandable to a sympathetic ear.

Write a selected set of core characters in Chinese, with a high degree of accuracy.

Present an oral report describing oneself in Chinese and understandable to a sympathetic ear.

Produce a letter or description of self, personal history, work experiences, or goals for the future in Chinese.

Answer questions verbally about themselves and their immediate environment in Chinese.

Demonstrate comprehension of information in ads, schedules, weather reports, biographies, dates, and times with a level of accuracy consistent with ACTFL standards for novice learners and relate that information verbally and in writing.

Read from a selected list of Chinese words with 80% accuracy.

Student Learning Outcomes
Broader than Objectives, SLOs are often comprised of what the student can do as a result of combining several course objectives.

Students will give an oral presentation in class, including a brief conversation with a classmate and/or the instructor which includes responding to oral questions. Student performance will be evaluated according to a grading rubric.

Students will demonstrate written proficiency on a variety of topics based on course work and the course objectives which will appear on the final exam.  Success will be measured by the instructor in the form of a percentage of correct answers or by evaluation according to an appropriate grading rubric.

Outside-of-Class Assignments
Typical Assignment
Using information provided in the textbook and class lecture, write out a daily routine in Chinese, including times, days of the week, and things you do.  Explain the routine to a classmate orally in Chinese. Your partner will analyze and interpret the information provided and give feedback and responses in Chinese.
Invent a conversation in Chinese similar to those provided in class and textbook, and model it with a classmate, including greetings and farewells.  The two students must ask each other’s names and ask each other how they are doing.
Write a questionnaire that requires the application of concepts covered in class and in the textbook.  Interview a classmate in Chinese to find out his or her likes and dislikes, what classes s/he takes at what times and in what locations, and what s/he does in his or her spare time. Respond appropriately in Chinese to the information provided.
