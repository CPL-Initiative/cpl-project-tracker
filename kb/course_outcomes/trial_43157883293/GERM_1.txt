Catalog Course Description
This course is designed to give the student training in spoken German at a basic level, including some reading, writing, and much speaking. Students gain aural comprehension of German through basic conversation and listening skill development. Customs and culture are also emphasized. This course may be offered in a distance education format.

Objectives
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
Generate and develop statements and sentences in German with conjugated verbs and declined nouns/adjectives.
Answer questions verbally in German about themselves and their immediate environment.

Demonstrate comprehension of information in ads, schedules, weather reports, biographies, dates, and times with 80% accuracy, and relate that information verbally and in writing.

Correctly tell time, identify dates, and verbally relate this information in German.

Tell simple stories and anecdotes in German, in the present and future tenses.

Spell a selected list of German words with 80% accuracy.

Pronounce key German words, such as common greetings and requests, with correct diction and without an American accent.

Outside-of-Class Assignments
Typical Assignment
Write out a daily routine in German, including times, days of the week, and things you do.
Memorize various folk songs in German dealing with various holidays.
Compare some of the cultural offerings Germany has made to the world with some American ones.
Role playing:  students respond in German to given situations, playing the role of a parent.
Develop a script for a performance demonstrating conversational skills in German.
IDEAA/UDL
IDEAA:
IDEAA and UDL Review Statement:

In this course, students engage in activities and texts that highlight cultural norms, social roles, and diverse perspectives. For example, the text addresses changes in the social role of women in German-speaking countries and the increase in ethnic diversity as Germany seeks to attract educated immigrants.
