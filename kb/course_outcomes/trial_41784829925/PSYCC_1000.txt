Introduction to Psychology
PSYC C1000:

3.0 Units


Print

        

This course is an introduction to psychology, which is the study of the mind and behavior. Students focus on theories and concepts of biological, cognitive, developmental, environmental, social, and cultural influences; their applications; and their research foundations.

Learning Outcomes
Upon satisfactory completion of this course, the student should be prepared to:
Demonstrate a knowledge of the basic concepts and theories in psychology, and how to apply them to one's own life.
Think critically about the major theories and perspectives in psychology and be able to compare and contrast the strengths and weaknesses of each.
Communicate effectively about scientific research and be able to evaluate psychological research.
