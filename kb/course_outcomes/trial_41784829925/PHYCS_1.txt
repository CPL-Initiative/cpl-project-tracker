Learning Outcomes
Upon satisfactory completion of this course, the student should be prepared to:
Demonstrate a knowledge of using appropriate basic mathematical skills and concepts to obtain quantitative solutions in problems involving the fundamental principles in conceptual physics.
Communicate effectively in thoughtful discussions during the hands-on mini experiments and interpretation of the observations during the conceptual physics lectures.
Think critically when determining the appropriate concepts to analyze qualitative problems or situations involving the fundamental principles in conceptual physics.
