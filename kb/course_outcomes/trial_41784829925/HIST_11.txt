History of California
HIST 11:

3.0 Units


Print

        

Survey of California history from pre-Colombian period to the present. Emphasis will include the Native American, Spaniards, Mexicans, and Anglo-Americans. Considerable attention will be devoted to California's influential role in national and world events.

Learning Outcomes
Upon satisfactory completion of this course, the student should be prepared to:
Think critically by describing, analyzing, and evaluating differing interpretations regarding California History.
Demonstrate a knowledge of California History by describing California History from a variety of perspectives.
Communicate effectively through oral participation, essay examinations and research papers.
