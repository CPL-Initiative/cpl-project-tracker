High Intermediate English Language Learning
ELL 10:

5.0 Units


Print

        

This is a high intermediate, academic English language course for students learning English. Emphasis will be on writing academic compositions including essays based on multiple source materials. The focus will be on advancing academic reading, vocabulary, grammar, and critical thinking skills. This course is designed to prepare students for ELL20. Recommendation: International English Language Testing System (IELTS) Score 5-6.

Learning Outcomes
Upon satisfactory completion of this course, the student should be prepared to:
Communicate effectively by writing compositions including essays on college-level materials and texts of philosophical, historical, literary, aesthetic, and cultural importance.
Demonstrate a knowledge of academic and professional vocabulary and level-appropriate grammar.
Think critically to comprehend and analyze college-level materials and texts of philosophical, historical, literary, aesthetic, and cultural importance.
Integrate into the culture and community of American higher education by engaging in appropriate academic activities of philosophical, historical, literary, aesthetic, and cultural importance.
Excel in the workforce by engaging in professional standards and norms.
