Learning Outcomes
Upon satisfactory completion of this course, the student should be prepared to:
Think critically about real-world examples of concepts explored in coursework and their implications for the environment conservation.
Demonstrate a knowledge of environmental conservation to make informed decisions.
Communicate effectively on the subject of environmental conservation.
