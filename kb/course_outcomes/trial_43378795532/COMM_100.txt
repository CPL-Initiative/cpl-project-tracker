COMM 100: Introduction to Communication

3.0 Units

(No prerequisite.) An interactive course introducing the theory and practice of communication, rhetoric, and public speaking. Students will deliver informative and persuasive speeches, learn about key areas in communication studies, such as speaking in a democratic society, rhetorical theory, active listening, intercultural and interpersonal communication, and small group communication), and provide peer speeches. Focus on confidence building. (CSU/UC) AA/AS COM GE Area 1B, Cal-GETC Area 1C (formerly AA/AS Area E, CSU Area A-1, IGETC Area 1C)
