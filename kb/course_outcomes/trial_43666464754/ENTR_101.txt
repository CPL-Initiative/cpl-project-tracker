Catalog Description
Students will embark on one of the most exciting adventures ever known: launching a business. This course identifies the methods for developing a business idea, starting a business, acquiring resources, and writing a business plan.

Course Objectives
describe the components of a business plan.
identify the characteristics of an entrepreneur.
define a break-even analysis, cash budgets, and pro forma statements and describe how the statements are interrelated and affect continuing operations and growth.
explain start-up, fixed, and variable costs.
recognize and evaluate opportunity and risk from the prespective of an entrepreneur.
describe the legal requirements of a new venture launch.
identify and effectively utilize the methods of idea generation.
define effective leadership traits and practices in an entrepreneurial environment.
identify the components of a marketing plan from the perspective of an enterpreneur.

Assignments
Outside Assignments
Assignment Text
1. Read assigned textbook chapters. 2. Review and analyze case studies. 3. Review and analyze current entrepreneurial articles. 4. Interview local entrepreneurs. 5. Write a basic business plan. 6. Participate in group projects. 7. Complete critical writing assignments.
Sample Assignment(s)
Assignment Text
1) Locate and interview a local entrepreneur. Write a two-page paper discussing the following: - why he/she entered into business - what steps he/she took to obtain start-up funding - how long it took to reach the point of profitability - would he/she do it again. Finally, what is the best piece of advice he/she might give to a potential entrepreneur?

Writing Requirements
1. Complete a basic business plan. 2. Complete case study analyses. 3. Complete peer evaluations for group projects. 4. Complete writing assignments including market research and critical thinking exercises. Objective exams: Exams will consist of multiple choice, true/false, matching, and essay questions. Sample essay questions: 1. List and describe at least four characteristics found in most entrepreneurs. 2. Identify the major components of a marketing plan from the perspective of an entrepreneur.
