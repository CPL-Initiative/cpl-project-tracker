Catalog Description

An introduction to the interior design program, industry and profession that includes the introduction to the interior design process and the exploration of basic technology skills necessary for the Interior Design student, examination of career opportunities, professional development, and the personal and professional characteristics required in the field of Interior Design and the interdisciplinary design professions careers.

Lecture Content

 

0: The Interior Design Program at AHC
Campus resources
Use of Canvas
INTD Samples Library
The INTD Lab and use
Software used in ID and systems requirements
The use of critiques and feedback in INTD
 1: Introduction to Interior Design as a Career
Overview of the interior design profession.
Key differences between interior design and related fields (architecture, interior decorating).
Career opportunities and specializations.
Guest speaker: A professional interior designer.
2: History of Interior Design
Brief history of interior design, from ancient to modern.
Evolution of design styles and influences (Baroque, Victorian, Modernism, etc.).
Key designers who shaped the industry.
3: Design Process and Methodology
The stages of design: from concept to execution.
Design thinking and problem-solving approaches.
Applying Design thinking to life and school management
Introduction to client-designer relationships and project management.
4: Careers in Interior Design
Career paths: Residential vs. commercial design, hospitality, retail, etc.
Freelance vs. working for a firm.
Professional development, certifications, and industry organizations (e.g., ASID, NCIDQ).
5: Portfolio Development
How to create an interior design portfolio.
Selecting and presenting projects effectively.
Feedback session on student project portfolios.
6: Ethics and Business Practices in Interior Design
Ethical considerations in interior design.
Client management and contracts.
The business side: marketing, branding, and entrepreneurship in design.
7: Site Visits
Visits to local design firms or ongoing projects.
Discussion of real-world design challenges and solutions.
Meeting with industry professionals and networking opportunities.
Central Coast Interior Design Association -CCID
8: Pathways to Professional Development

Internships 
9: Career Planning and Finding Jobs in Interior Design

Course Objectives

become familiar with various industry networking associations and how they benefit the interior designer

introduce design thinking and how it can be applied to problem solving in daily life

differentiate the roles and responsibilities of interior design professionals and their interaction with allied professionals

become familiar with the various college support systems, i.e. computer lab, library, career center, student services, etc

use various basic computer programs to produce INTD related documents and course work requirements i.e. learning management system, Word, MyHancock, etc

use computer technology to research INTD careers and write career research paper using the American Psychological Association (APA) Style format

develop the foundation for a professional portfolio

demonstrate the impact that an interior environment has on human behavior, identity, health productivity, comfort, and well-being

defend the interior environment as an aesthetic creation and an expression of the human experience

analyze their personal characteristics and values that makes for an effective interior designer

identify the various career opportunities within the Interior Design (INTD) field
