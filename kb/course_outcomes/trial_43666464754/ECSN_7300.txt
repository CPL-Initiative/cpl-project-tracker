Catalog Description

Interested in learning about how to start your own family childcare business? Learn about Community Care Licensing Regulations, marketing techniques, contract development, budgeting strategies, and fees associated with owning and operating your own licensed family childcare business.  This course is also 1 of 5 required courses to increase your confidence and competence in working with young children in a community-based setting. English Language Learners should also enroll in the ELDV 7300 support course.

Objectives
At the end of the course, the student will be able to:
discuss personal and financial issues before starting a child care business.
discuss the impact that rates and fees may have on business viability, sustainability and profitability.
understand the basic Title 22, Division 12, Chapter 3 requirements and qualifications that apply to licensing family child care homes.
identify typical expenses needed to operate a child care business.

understand the components needed for a parent handbook.

Methods of Evaluation

SP (Satisfactory Progress) Grade Requirements

Students are assessed according to the Student Learning Outcome (SLOs) in order to earn an SP grade.  A grade of SP is required in order for this course to be eligible for the certificate.  If students do not earn an SP grade, they will be given no grade and will need to repeat the course.

Students must earn a grade of 70% or higher for every course SLO to earn a grade of SP.

Class participation of 70% or higher is expected to earn an SP grade in addition to SLO requirements.

Student Learning Outcomes

ECSN 7300 SLO 1 - Understand the comprehensive role of the family child care provider as it relates to effective business practices, finances, and Title 22 regulations.
