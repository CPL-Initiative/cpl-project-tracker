Catalog Description

The course explores how multimedia transforms experiences of creative expression and communication and impacts our cultural, business, and personal lives. Topics include project planning, development, and exploration through multiple projects. Projects include art for interaction, web imagery, animation, motion graphics, UI/UX, gaming, visual effects, video production, and transmedia storytelling. Course software: Adobe Creative Suite and other relevant software.

Course Content
Lecture Content

Introduction to multimedia design and processes - discussion and evaluation of diverse media projects and processes.

The Multimedia Team - basics of interactive and time-based media project design and collaboration. Defining the scope of the project and story-telling. Operating systems (such as Apple, Windows) and authoring tools (such as Adobe Creative Suite and other relevant software).

Multimedia Tools - an introduction to the software used to create or modify video, photos, interactive graphics, sound, music, and animation. 

Media design project planning and development process - defining scope of project and target audience; research and development of concepts, flowcharts and storyboards; testing, review and editing cycles, deliverable/s process, requirements, formats and deadlines;

Image and sound asset acquisition, creation and editing - software used; copyright law;

Professional skills and critical analysis - developing skills, professional practices specific to communication, collaboration, project analysis; time management and accountability.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

Lab Content
Research, view, and discuss multimedia projects to implement interactive and time-based concepts.
Project planning, storyboarding, flow-charting and critical analysis.
Hands-on exploration of digital media software with in-class activities, tutorials and creative projects. Students create or modify video, photos, interactive graphics, sound, and animations. (Adobe Creative Suite and other relevant media design software.)
Development of best practices - importing media assets, design principles and layout considerations, principles of interaction.
Technical issues - bit-depth and resolution, sampling rates, bitmap versus vector, bandwidth, file size, frame rates, aspect ratios.
Testing and revising media projects - troubleshooting, and debugging.
Professional skills and critical analysis - developing skills, professional practices specific to communication, collaboration, project analysis; time management and accountability.
Publishing completed projects to required specifications in the appropriate digital medium.

Course Objectives

analyze culturally diverse multimedia works, both western and non-western, in relation to their context of origin and intended audience, and in terms of the design strategies, technology and aesthetics employed.

distinguish appropriate techniques for the creation and use of vector, raster, sound, and time-based imagery.

develop a project proposal, concept, design strategy and basic technology skills needed in response to a specific communication goal.

collaborate with others to design and develop a media project that communicates effectively to a specific audience: identify project goals, technology skills needed; roles and responsibilities, deadlines and deliverables.

test, troubleshoot, and edit a multimedia project as needed to successfully publish the project in the appropriate format.
