Catalog Description

Students will learn and synthesize information concerning the impact of the social determinants of health, including education, socioeconomic status, race, sexual orientation, disability and gender.  Students are provided with a broad foundation of knowledge dealing with such social determinants in mental health, stress management, fitness, diet and weight control, prevention and control of communicable and non-communicable diseases, drugs and alcohol, first aid, cancer prevention and control, and the scope of, and access to community health services.

Course Objectives
evaluate the results of a health risk appraisal and lifestyle assessment inventory.

outline a behavior management program for enhancing personal wellness based upon health risk appraisal and lifestyle assessment inventory incorporating gender, race, homelessness, disability and socioeconomic status. 

discuss the components of wellness and the relationship to an individual's medical plan.

identify methods for coping with prevalent stressors as related to social determinants.

make recommendations to improve diet and design a personal fitness/exercise program. 

identify the hazards of substance use and abuse.

assess and utilize credible information to identify major diseases, their hazards, risk factors, prevention and current treatments, such as peer-reviewed journals, libraries and databases. 

enumerate methods of managing medical problems, social determinants, and equitable access to care. 

recognize contributing factors impacting relationships, communication and environment.
