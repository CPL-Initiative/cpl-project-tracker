Course Objectives
identify conceptual foundations of play, recreation and leisure.
identify the psychological, sociological, and physiological significance of play, recreation, and leisure.
identify the technical, economic, and political significance of play, recreation, and leisure.
identify the history and development of the leisure service profession.
identify the contemporary issues impacting the leisure service profession.
identify the motivations to participate in recreational activities on both a personal and professional basis.
identify practices that encourage inclusiveness in the design and operation of leisure services.
identify the mission, philosophy, programs and services of the recreation profession.
identify the government, non profit, commercial, and private agencies that provide recreational services.
demonstrate appropriate professional behavior in the conduct of recreation activities.

Other Assignments
Assignment Text
1. Exploration of Personal Recreation Opportunities 2. Applying Technology to the Recreation Field 3. Team Project and Presentation 4. 10 Hours of Volunteer Work in the Recreational Field 5. Read the Textbook 6. Writing Assignments Sample Assignments: 1. Personal discovery by individual student of a new recreational activity (one never previously experienced), followed by a written report on their experience 2. Writing assignments for individual students: a.) Internet research project on building a travel itinerary for a destination a minimum of 1,000 miles away; b.) Interview five persons about what they do during leisure time and approximately how much money is spent per month on recreational activities. Compare and contrast their responses; explore the reasons for similarities or differences. Develop conclusions; c.) Attendance and assessment of a personally selected recreational event (i.e., admission value, security, spectator interest level, etc.) 3. Team project and presentation: Groups of five students working together to research and make an assessment of a specific recreational program (i.e., municipality, county, academic institution, service club) 4. Volunteer work: Each student required 10 verified hours of volunteer work in a recreational field (i.e., lining a youth soccer field, security for a high school athletic event, officiating a youth sport)
