Catalog Description
An introductory class where students will learn to read engineering drawings, evaluate print specifications, recognize orthographic views and visualize the actual objects or projects shown in the illustration. This course is not open to students who are enrolled in or have received credit for AB 330, AT 330, ET 330, MT 330, or AT/ET/MT 117.

Course Objectives
read and interpret various engineering drawings by completing numerous assignments.
identify surface finish marks, tolerance, basic architecture, and welding symbols and be able to explain their meanings.
use an engineering drawing accompanying specifications and materials lists to solve industrial questions, to complete a project, or solve a related problem.
use related handbooks, codes, and other references as they may be needed to solve a print reading question.
