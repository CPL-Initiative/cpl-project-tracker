Catalog Description
A study of managing bar and beverage service for profit. Types of beverages (including mixology), equipment, sanitary operations, staffing, promotions, purchasing, storage, inventory, and pricing strategies are discussed.

Course Objectives
identify and describe the types and styles of beers, wines, spirits, and non-alcoholic beverages used in bartending.
utilize appropriate service techniques for styles of beer, wine, spirits, and non-alcoholic beverages.
describe the four basic mixing methods and know the recipes for the most common cocktails, tall drinks, frozen drinks, and alcoholic coffee drinks.
list the steps to properly close a beverage to include bar tools; glassware; beer draft systems; inventory count and storage; and wine dispensers.
identify equipment, materials, and operational procedures necessary for safe and sanitary conditions.
describe typical staff positions and determine staffing needs and schedules for varied size and type operations.
recognize inventory control procedures and develop beverage orders, receiving and storage guidelines, and storage issuing procedures.
