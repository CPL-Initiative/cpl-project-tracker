ENGIN 110 - Introduction to Engineering
Units: 3.0
Print

Description:

This course is an introduction to different engineering disciplines and careers, the role of an engineer in society, engineering ethics, the engineering approach to problem-solving, engineering design process and project development, engineering analysis, concurrent engineering, and application of computers in engineering including design and presentation tools. The emphasis is on hands-on creative problem-solving, teamwork, and effective communication. Students will develop design, analysis, and computer skills through work on projects drawn from various engineering majors.

Student Learning Outcomes:

Identify the activities of engineers in the field and the office.
Describe academic pathways to degrees in engineering.
Identify the steps required to solve problems in engineering.
Identify individual engineer's role in interdisciplinary projects.
Describe a variety of software applications used by engineers.
Recognize ethical and legal requirements while engaged in engineering practice.
Develop library and internet research skills as preliminary research tools before the start of an engineering project. (Laboratory)
Use software to produce reports and carry out basic calculations and graphs. (Laboratory)
Apply Computer Aided Drafting (CAD) skills to create objects and produce working drawings. (Laboratory)
Use electrical measurement equipment such as multimeters and oscilloscopes. (Laboratory)
Apply basic computer programming skills to solve mathematical problems arising in engineering. (Laboratory)
Use computers for simple control applications. (Laboratory)
Apply rapid prototyping methods using 3D printers and laser cutters to complete projects. (Laboratory)
Perform basic operations with tools and shop equipment. (Laboratory)
