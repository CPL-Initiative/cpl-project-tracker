ADJUS 010NC - Public Safety Employment Application & Personal History Statement-NC
Units: 0.0
Print

Description:

This open entry/open exit noncredit course will assist and support students with the key components of the hiring process to attain careers in public safety. Students will be instructed and supported through the public safety application and hiring process, which includes the preparation of the employment application, resume writing, completing the personal history statement, and the background investigation process. This course also creates a pipeline to employment for students and will include contact and access to police, fire, and emergency medical services employers and recruiters.

Student Learning Outcomes:

Describe the requirements for effectively completing an entry-level public safety position job application.
Identify and organize their own personal history information into the various job application documents.
Identify the key personal attributes sought by public safety employers.
Identify all key components in the hiring process for a public safety career.
Discuss the role and significance of the personal history statement and background investigation.
Effectively complete the job resume and cover letter.
Identify the most common disqualifiers made by public safety job applicants.
Discuss the hiring requirements of CA Police Officer Standards and Training and other similar public safety organizations.
