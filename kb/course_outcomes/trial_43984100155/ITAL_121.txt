ITAL 121 - Second Term Italian
Units: 5.0
Print

Description:

This is the second course in a sequence of Italian language courses. The course continues skill building in understanding, speaking, reading, and writing of the Italian language. The expansion of vocabulary and more advanced communicative functions and structures, as well as a deeper examination of the cultures of Italian-speaking countries are emphasized.

Student Learning Outcomes:

Communicate verbally in Italian at a basic-intermediate level with clear pronunciation.
Apply the rules of basic-intermediate Italian grammar, syntax, and vocabulary to written and oral communication.
Demonstrate auditory comprehension by participating in basic-intermediate oral conversations in Italian.
Demonstrate reading comprehension by reading basic-intermediate texts originally written in Italian.
Write essays of at least 150-300 words in Italian.
Compare and contrast cultures of Italian-speaking countries.
