CHIN 120 - First Term Mandarin Chinese
Units: 5.0
Print

Description:

This course provides an introduction to the Chinese language and the culture of Chinese-speaking countries. Topics include the four language skills: speaking, listening, reading, and writing. Emphasis is placed on active use of the language in class as well as basic communicative functions and structures.

Requisites:

Course Note:

This course is equivalent to two years of high school study.

Transferable:

Transferable to both UC and CSU

Repeatability:

None

General Education:

Not approved for General Education

Student Learning Outcomes:

Communicate verbally in Mandarin Chinese at a basic level with clear pronunciation.
Apply the rules of basic Chinese grammar, syntax, and vocabulary to written and oral communication.
Demonstrate auditory comprehension by participating in basic oral conversations in Mandarin Chinese.
Demonstrate reading comprehension by reading basic texts in Chinese.
Write short paragraphs of 100-150 words in simplified Chinese characters and their corresponding pinyin romanization.
Describe cultures of Chinese-speaking countries.
