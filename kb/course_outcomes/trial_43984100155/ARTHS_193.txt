ARTHS 193 - History of Asian Art
Units: 3.0
Print

Description:

This course examines major artistic traditions in Asia from prehistory to the present. It tracks the interdependence of artists, patrons, cultures, and religions. Artistic changes are contextualized in relation to political developments and cross-cultural exchanges.

Student Learning Outcomes:

Distinguish and describe characteristics of various artistic styles, forms, and works from Asian cultures.
Apply art historical concepts and vocabulary to the art of specific Asian cultures and time periods.
Describe the intersection of culture, politics, religion, the arts, and artists' roles in specific Asian cultures and time periods.
Compare the art of specific Asian cultures and time periods with other artistic traditions.
