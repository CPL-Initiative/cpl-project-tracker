CONST 105 - Survey of the Trades
Units: 1.5
Print

Description:

The course presents a survey of career opportunities and requirements of the skilled trades as well as basic theoretical and practical skills common to all construction trades.

Requisites:

Course Note:

Transferable:

Transferable to CSU only

Repeatability:

None

General Education:

Not approved for General Education

Student Learning Outcomes:

Describe construction job site protocols and safety standards.
Compare and contrast various trades, interactions, interdependencies, and how the basic construction process flows from one trade to another.
Apply basic construction principles and techniques across several trades, and adapt those basic skills to various construction situations.
Select specific trades to pursue.
