MUSIC 100 - Applied Music
Units: 1.0
Print

Description:

This course consists of individualized study of the appropriate techniques and repertoire for the specific instrument or voice being studied. The emphasis is on the progressive development of skills needed for solo performance. Achievement is evaluated through a juried performance. Students receive six hours of lessons from an instructor scheduled throughout the semester. Students are required to practice at least 3.5 hours per week during scheduled supervised practice hours in the department practice rooms. Students will meet an additional 24 hours during the semester for group discussion and performances.

Requisites:

Course Note:

This course is limited to students majoring in music and intending to complete the A.A. or A.A.T. in Music, and must therefore be concurrently enrolled in one of the ensemble courses listed in the degree requirements (A.A. or A.A.T.) and in a theory and musicianship class (MUSIC-122, 123, 222, 223).

Student Learning Outcomes:

Perform music with metric and rhythmic accuracy as a soloist. Private lesson (laboratory by arrangement) objectives.
Perform music with technical facility using one’s instrument or voice. Private lesson (laboratory by arrangement) objectives.
Perform music with accurate intonation and/or harmonic awareness using one’s instrument or voice. Private lesson (laboratory by arrangement) objectives.
Perform music with appropriate articulation, dynamics, phrasing, and expression as a soloist. Private lesson (laboratory by arrangement) objectives.
Perform music in a manner appropriate to the style, period, and genre of the literature using one’s instrument or voice. Private lesson (laboratory by arrangement) objectives.
Perform improvised solos as applicable. Private lesson (lab by arrangement) objectives.
Practice new music from written sources. Supervised practice (laboratory by arrangement) objectives
Devise appropriate approaches to practicing music from one’s repertoire. Supervised practice (laboratory by arrangement) objectives.
Perform music from memory as appropriate.
Demonstrate coordination with one's accompanist as applicable.
