Catalog Description:
APSF 9701 - Sprinkler Fitter Apprenticeship I
3.00 Units
First half of year one of a five-year Sprinkler Fitter Apprenticeship Program. Topics overview include related safety and health, OSHA 10, Rigid 300, hand tools, ladders, scissor lift certification, scaffolds; reading sprinkler drawings (part one); care and use of hand tools; operation of the sprinkler head; reading a ruler, performing calculations with whole numbers and fractions; using a MacBook for online instruction; communicating pipe dimensions. Registration in this course is limited to apprentices accepted into, and registered with, the state of California Department of Apprenticeship Standards. Each apprenticeship has different eligibility and admission requirements but includes being at least 18 years of age and having earned a high school diploma or GED. For information, see https://www.apprenticeship4you.com/our-programs.

Measurable Objectives:
Upon completion of this course, the student should be able to:
demonstrate how to safely operate the Ridgid 300 pipe threading machine;
recognize pipe threading and grooving techniques used in the fire protection industry;
interpret standard fire sprinkler blueprints including symbols and abbreviations, find unlisted dimensions through mathematical calculation;
operate a ruler with the accuracy of 1/16th of an inch;
recognize the related safety and health aspects of the industry and demonstrate the necessary protective measures;
complete OSHA 10 certification;
identify the standard and reducing fittings used in the fire protection industry;
recognize and demonstrate knowledge of basic pipe dimensions;
recognize automatic sprinkler systems and their components;
complete scissor lift training and receive certification;
analyze the work environment in order to employ proper safety measures;
demonstrate a working knowledge of the use of a MacBook Air and Blackboard in an educational setting;
identify flexible and rigid grooved couplings and corresponding fittings.

3. Student Learning Outcomes
Upon the completion of this course, the student should be able to:
Demonstrate the care and use of hand tools and the operation of sprinkler heads.
Read and apply blueprint drawings and ruler measurements for pipe dimensions and placement.
Apply safety and health rules when using hand tools, ladders, scissor lifts, and scaffolds.
