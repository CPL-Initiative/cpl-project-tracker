Catalog Description
Textile Product Innovator 1 introduces students to the building block concepts and tools needed to create textile product businesses, tackle production processes, and increase employability with entrepreneurial mindset skills for the cut-and-sew industry. From home-based to contract manufacturing, product ideation, market research tools, sourcing, pricing, and production are surveyed. The course is enriched with industry interactions, an emphasis on ethical, sustainable practices, and access to digital technologies.

Goals
Course Goals
Goals Text
 Introduce the characteristics and skills of a successful innovator and compare and contrast with personal characteristics and skills. 
Goals Text
Gain an understanding of the essential cut-and-sew industry terminology to confidently communicate within the industry. 
Goals Text
Introduce the key concepts and tools necessary to start a business, produce products and manage home-based, e-commerce driven, mobile, brick-and-mortar or commercial textile product enterprises. 
Goals Text
Gain a basic understanding of the financial and legal requirements and regulations specific to a textile product enterprise, including intellectual property. 
Goals Text
Learn the types of business models and their application in sewn products enterprises. 
Goals Text
Learn to conduct research to discover and develop target customers for a consumer centric enterprise.  
Goals Text
Develop effective material sourcing, production costing, product pricing and resource management skills for textile product enterprises. 
Goals Text
Survey industrial and digital equipment used in sewn product manufacturing and the procurement of the equipment.
Goals Text
Examine the current trends regarding sustainable textile product manufacturing and retail operations.

Student Learning Objectives
Upon successful completion of the course the student will be able to:
Objective Text
Describe and assess characteristics and skills of a successful innovator, comparing them to their personal skills. 
Objective Text
Identify and use essential textile product enterprise terminology. 
Objective Text
Explain the key concepts and tools necessary to start a business, produce products and manage home-based, E-commerce driven, mobile, brick-and-mortar or commercial textile product enterprises. 
Objective Text
Identify the types of business models and provide examples of their application in sewn products enterprises. 
Objective Text
Write a clearly defined statement describing a product’s target customer. 
Objective Text
Create a source guide for materials, resources, and production needs. 
Objective Text
Explain and develop a networking strategy, which identifies and incorporates mentors, industry peers, advisory groups, and target customers. 
Objective Text
Explain current trends, regulations and advancements in sustainable sewn product manufacturing and retail operations.
