Catalog Description
An introduction to the foundations of early childcare, including: basic child development, health and safety, nutrition, and behavior management and discipline. Course includes practical guidelines for childcare and explores options for careers and vocations in early childcare. (FT)

Goals
Course Goals
Goals Text
Understand the normative similarities and differences of intellectual, emotional, social and physical developmental of children, especially in infants and toddlers.
Goals Text
Develop knowledge of theoretical and appropriate practical elements of caregiving to facilitate discussions for a healthy environment designed for early childhood caregiving.
Goals Text
Consider the effects of society, culture and special circumstances upon the family microsystems, family types and the subsequent macrosystems with which they co-exist.
Goals Text
Understand the roles of adults and caregivers in professional areas of service providing for the needs of infants and toddlers.

Consider the effects of society, culture and special circumstances upon the family microsystems, family types and the subsequent macrosystems with which they co-exist. 4. Understand the roles of adults and caregivers in professional areas of service providing for the needs of infants and toddlers.

Student Learning Objectives
Upon successful completion of the course the student will be able to:
Objective Text
Identify and describe normative similarities and differences of intellectual, emotional, social and physical theories at each developmental stage in early childhood, especially in infants and toddlers.
Objective Text
Critically assess research of theoretical and appropriate practical elements of caregiving to facilitate decisions for a healthy environment designed for early childhood, especially in infants and toddlers.
Objective Text
Apply knowledge of family microsystems, family types and the subsequent macrosystems to childcare providing strategies.
Objective Text
Demonstrate an understanding of the roles of adults and caregivers in professional areas of service providing for the needs of infants and toddlers.
Legacy Data
1. Identify and describe normative similarities and differences of intellectual, emotional, social and physical theories at each developmental stage in early childhood, especially in infants and toddlers. 2. Critically assess research of theoretical and appropriate practical elements of caregiving to facilitate decisions for a healthy environment designed for early childhood, especially in infants and toddlers. 3. Apply knowledge of family microsystems, family types and the subsequent macrosystems to childcare providing strategies. 4. Demonstrate an understanding of the roles of adults and caregivers in professional areas of service providing for the needs of infants and toddlers.
Assignments
Reading Assignments
Optional Text
Assignments

Periodicals, magazines, instructor written materials, instructor selected URL’s and other publications related to the childcare profession.

Writing Assignments
Optional Text
Assignments

Taking class notes, completing worksheets related to lectures, submitting analytical essays that compare and contrast topics learned in class, developing a course portfolio, and satisfactory performance on class written exams.

Appropriate Outside Assignments
Optional Text
Assignments

Participating in field trips, completing Internet assignments and observing childcare environments.

Appropriate Assignments that Demonstrate Critical Thinking
Optional Text
Assignments

Comparing and contrasting assigned readings and online materials with field research, written assignments which include analyzing and implementing course concepts, visiting a currently operating day care business, teaching an age appropriate lesson in a classroom environment and applying problem-solving skills in case studies.
