Catalog Description
This course provides an introduction and overview of the graphic communications industry and the qualifications necessary to match career opportunities. An emphasis will be placed on the basic knowledge required for advancement towards certificate and/or degree programs in visual communications or employment in related professional fields. Students will develop a professional portfolio to include but not limited to a resume and digital portfolio. Hands-on exercises in planning, designing, and creating computer-based digital media through various projects will be utilized during the course. (FT)
