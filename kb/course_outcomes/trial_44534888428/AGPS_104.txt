Catalog Description
Introduction to plant science including structure, growth processes, propagation, physiology, growth media, biological competitors, and post-harvest factors of food, fiber, and ornamental plants.

Student Learning Outcomes
Learning Outcomes
Categorize the biological competitors of higher plants.
Categorize the roles of higher plants in the living world.
Define sexual and asexual reproduction in higher plants.
Define the climatic influences on plant growth and development.
Define the physical and chemical properties of soils and soil erosion problems.
Explain the scientific method and its application in solving problems in plant and soil science.
Explain photosynthesis, respiration, and translocation in higher plants.
Explain the standard plant propagation methods.
