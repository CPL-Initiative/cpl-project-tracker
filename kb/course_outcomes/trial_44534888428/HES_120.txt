Catalog Description
This course covers techniques of weight training, an individually adapted exercise program, basic development of strength, endurance, speed, and agility through a controlled weight-training program. Activities for injury prevention, treatment and rehabilitation will be discussed in order to encompass the athletic training field.
Justification for this course
This course fulfills part of the Feather River College general education requirements and is part of our TMC AA degree for Kinesiology.
