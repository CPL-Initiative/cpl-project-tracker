Catalog Description

This course is designed to provide the student with instruction regarding the principles and practices of intravenous therapy and blood withdrawal. Topics include legal aspects, anatomy and physiology regarding intravenous and blood withdrawal, equipment used, assessing and treating complications, blood transfusions, total parenteral nutrition, and parenteral fluids. Certificate of achievement will be issued after the students becomes licensed as a Vocational Nurse. Intravenous sticks cannot be performed on clients until the Board of Vocational Nursing is notified by Feather River College.

Justification for this course
This course is part of the Licensed Vocational Nursing program. It is also offered to other currently Licensed Vocational Nurses.

Student Learning Outcomes
Learning Outcomes
1. Define the terminology related to infusion-related professional practice
2. Perform successful venipunctures on at least 3 live human subjects
3. Demonstrate universal precautions
4. Describe potential complications of intravenous therapy and blood withdrawal
5. Demonstrate safety precautions utilized to prevent complications
6. Identify nursing diagnoses and interventions related to fluid and electrolyte balance
7. Compare the advantages and disadvantages of various parenteral fluids
8. Successfully document care of the client while performing venipuncture and assessment of client with ongoing intravenous and blood transfusion therapy
