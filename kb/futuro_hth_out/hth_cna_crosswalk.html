Why the match points away from the CNA course itself

A California CNA program is a CDPH-approved 160-hour course — 60 hours of theory and 100 hours of supervised clinical practice — with its hours and content fixed in regulation. A college cannot award credit against it for an 80-hour online course with no clinical component. So HTH does not articulate into the CNA course.

What it does map onto is the course sitting next to the CNA course in the same catalog: interpersonal communication, intercultural communication, healthcare ethics, communication for allied health. That is the course named in the table, and it is a far easier faculty conversation — it asks a college to recognise 80 hours of documented soft-skills instruction against a course whose outcomes are already soft skills.

Every candidate had to pass two independent tests: its title matches one of HTH’s six modules, and its TOP code sits in a related discipline family. Title matching alone returned “Library Teamwork Supervision Skills” and “Compassion Training for Yoga Teachers”. Courses teaching another occupation’s law and ethics — funeral service, dental, veterinary — are scored down: they carry health TOP codes but they are not this course.

What this does not tell you. No college has confirmed it will grant credit. Nothing here has been validated with discipline faculty, and no college website was reviewed — these sessions cannot reach college domains, so every row rests on state data files and MAP, not on catalog pages. Treat a named course as the best available starting point for a conversation, not as an approval.
