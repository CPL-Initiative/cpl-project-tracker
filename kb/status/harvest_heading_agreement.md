# Harvest heading agreement

Written by `kb/_program_requirements_heading_agreement.py` from committed files only. Each record's own heading against the program it is filed under (COCI title and award). A snapshot; rerun the script for today's.

All colleges: 18775 records, 14384 agree, 456 disagree (226 of them pass), 3935 cannot tell (601 say the heading is not in their text).

The heading check (a heading that names another award type) would fail 192 records that pass today, 38 of them a transfer degree and a local degree crossed. It is in no pass rule.

A window bound to the next program (`after`) opens on the program's own control-number line and then names another program's title and award: the catalog prints the number after its program, and the reader cut the page from it. Seen only where the capture kept each pick's opening.
