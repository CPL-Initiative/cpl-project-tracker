# Harvest heading agreement

A dated snapshot of main at commit cbcfeee7c22c (2026-11-22; the newest capture it reads started 2026-11-22), written by `kb/_program_requirements_heading_agreement.py` from the files that commit holds. A dispatch of `harvest-gap-census.yml` rewrites it beside the gap census; nothing gates a PR on it, so it can trail the records. Each record's own heading against the program it is filed under (COCI title and award).

All colleges: 18958 records, 15413 agree, 276 disagree (168 of them pass on the committed files), 3269 cannot tell (278 say the heading is not in their text; 76 name an award and none of the program's title words, 70 of them passing, set aside).

The heading check (a heading that names another award type and some of the program's title) would fail 136 records that pass on the committed files (passes_live), 31 of them a transfer degree and a local degree crossed. It is in no pass rule.

A window bound to the next program (`after`) opens on the program's own control-number line and then names another program's title and award: the catalog prints the number after its program, and the reader cut the page from it. Seen only where the capture kept each pick's opening.
