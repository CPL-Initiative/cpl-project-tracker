# CER proposals: a title in use, and no course title on a certification (S642, 2026-11-12)

Sam's two rulings, in session, 2026-11-12:

> "also think about how you could apply this to your intelligence moving forward...being able to suggest the best canonical title based on what's in use..." (`sam-classifier-suggests-title-in-use-2026-11-12`)

> "The MID title works well for cx because those are single course exhibits...but many if not most industry certs like Firefighter 1, POST, Real Estate Sales, Comptia Network+ have several CRs and need a non-course based canonical title that best aligns with the official name of the cert or license or training program..." (`sam-cert-title-official-name-not-mid-2026-11-12`)

Measured over the committed Review queue, `kb/exhibits_review.json` written 2026-11-12T11:53:55Z (procedures run 2026-11-11T21:41:24Z): 177 held titles, 140 in Proposed and 37 in Wording check, none in conflict. No live read; the counts are of the committed files on `main` at `77fb96ae`.

## The counts

- **(a) a new title proposed while a same-issuer title is in use: 22.** By #3160's rule as merged the count is 61. The other 39 were most-used matches on shared words, most of them naming a different thing: *Emergency Medical Services (EMS 10)* named *Emergency Medical Responder*, *Residential Insulation Blow-In* named *Blueprint Reading-Residential*, *Administration of Justice (AJ 54)* named *Introduction to Corrections*, and eight ESL levels all named *English as a Second Language*. A few may have been right (*Automotive Technology (AUTO INT)* and *Introduction to Automotive Technology*); they wait for a person on their cards. The shared rule now asks more of a most-used title (one title's words hold all of the other's) and of a title ending in a course code (the candidate carries the same code); the card's line follows the same rule (`kb/title_in_use.py`, `exhibits.js` relAligned, pinned by `tests/fixtures/title_in_use_cases.json`). 18 are statewide exhibits, 4 most used.
- **(b) a course title proposed for an exhibit that is not single-CR credit by exam: 53** (43 M-ID, 9 college course, 1 C-ID). Every one is an industry certification or apprenticeship by its MAP id, and 51 of the 53 carry more than one credit recommendation. One course-titled proposal is credit by exam with one credit recommendation and keeps its M-ID title (*Credit by Exam - ACADEMIC PLANNING*, M-ID COUN M1004).
- **(c) both: 1** (Firefighter Recruit Academy Certificate).
- One more proposal moves that is in neither list: *Industry Training Evidence for FST 1* proposed *Principles of Emergency Services*, a title already in the reference, and now joins the most used same-issuer title, *Principles of Fire and Emergency Services*.

After this PR the builder changes **75 of 177** proposals: 48 in_use_statewide, 22 classifier, 5 in_use_most_used. 38 proposals with nothing in use and no official name on file now carry `needs_official_name`, and the card says so in words. Every record carries `proposal_source`; after this PR: 117 classifier, 54 in_use_statewide, 5 in_use_most_used, 1 course.

Nothing here changes the reference. A proposal is acted on only when a person saves it on its card, or presses Review's *Accept all proposed titles* and then *Save all*.

The ASE cards join the reference's titles in the form they carry today (*ASE A1 — Engine Repair*), with the format's title (*Engine Repair (ASE A1)*) beside it as an alternate. A card never renames a reference title; putting those titles in the format is the rename batch's work (`docs/reference/lanes/cer-procedures.md`, S637).
