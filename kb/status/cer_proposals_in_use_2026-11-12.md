# CER proposals: a title in use, and no course title on a certification (S642, 2026-11-12)

Sam's two rulings, in session, 2026-11-12:

> "also think about how you could apply this to your intelligence moving forward...being able to suggest the best canonical title based on what's in use..." (`sam-classifier-suggests-title-in-use-2026-11-12`)

> "The MID title works well for cx because those are single course exhibits...but many if not most industry certs like Firefighter 1, POST, Real Estate Sales, Comptia Network+ have several CRs and need a non-course based canonical title that best aligns with the official name of the cert or license or training program..." (`sam-cert-title-official-name-not-mid-2026-11-12`)

Measured over the Review queue on `main` (`kb/exhibits_review.json` written 2026-11-12T11:53:55Z, procedures run 2026-11-11T21:41:24Z, which main's builder reproduces exactly): 177 held titles, 140 in Proposed and 37 in Wording check. The rule is the one in `kb/title_in_use.py` and `exhibits.js` (relAligned), pinned to `tests/fixtures/title_in_use_cases.json`, as revised after the review of #3168 (rules d to h in the module's docstring).

## The counts

- **(a) a new title proposed while a same-issuer title is in use: 12** (10 statewide, 2 most used). #3160's rule as merged counts 61. The revised rule reads levels (Firefighter 2 is never Firefighter 1, Level 1 never Level 4), keeps a card's own reference title unless a statewide title has the same words, and asks a most-used title to sit inside the card's words.
- **(b) a course title proposed for an exhibit that is not single-CR credit by exam: 53** (43 M-ID, 9 college course, 1 C-ID). All are industry certifications or apprenticeships by their MAP ids. One keeps its course title because the college issues the exhibit (Industry Training Evidence for AJ 61).
- **(c) both: 1** (Firefighter Recruit Academy Certificate).

After this PR the builder changes **63 of 177** proposals (40 in_use_statewide, 21 classifier, 2 in_use_most_used). **42 join a title in use**: 12 move to another title (10 statewide exhibits, 2 most used, the (a) list), and 30 Carpenters cards take the classifier's own title, itself a statewide exhibit. The other 21 take the classifier's title in place of a course title. **38** carry `needs_official_name`, and Accept all leaves them for a card. Every record carries `proposal_source`; after this PR: 127 classifier, 46 in_use_statewide, 2 course, 2 in_use_most_used.

*Industry Training Evidence for FST 1* keeps its own reference title, *Principles of Emergency Services*. The first version of this report named its target wrongly: the first version of the rule moved it to *Principles of Fire and Emergency Services Safety and Survival*, another course.

Nothing here changes the reference. A proposal is acted on only when a person saves its card, or presses Review's *Accept all proposed titles* and *Save all*; Accept all now leaves out every card flagged `needs_official_name` and says so.
