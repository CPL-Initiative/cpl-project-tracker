**The single resolvable boundary.** The 3 HARD misses reduce to two questions only
Sam can settle:

- **P-1 vs D-4 for same-college level/module pairs** (#32, #40 — and Q-SAMECOLL / Q-LADDER).

If Sam rules v0.6-correct on the P-1↔D-4 pattern (the likely call under his own lens),
the fine gate rises to ~92% and **the doctrine graduates** — batch-apply unlocks.
