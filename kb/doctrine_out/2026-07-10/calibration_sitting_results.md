# Calibration sitting — results (2026-07-13)

Sam walked the 52-group v0.2 calibration (`calibration_review.md`). The headline
outcome is **the lens** he articulated, now ratified as the doctrine's P-1:

> **"If a student showed up with evidence they'd already completed it — or that
> they have the skills — would you make them repeat your local course under its
> local name?"  No → merge (one identity). Yes → keep separate.**

Pure CPL equivalence — *learning*, not textual similarity or discipline codes.
Folded into `kb/merge_doctrine.md` **v0.6** as P-1 (RATIFIED) + the refinements
P-1a / P-7a / P-9a / P-10a.
