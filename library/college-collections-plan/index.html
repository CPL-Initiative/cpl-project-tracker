CPL Commons strategic plan, v2 agreed final, November 11, 2026

College Collections Plan

A person names what they have learned, and the Commons shows every course at every college it likely counts for, how strong the match is, and how to ask for it.

The California Community Colleges train more people for careers than any other system in the country. The Commons closes the loop from college to career and back, so that learning in every setting is recognized and kept aligned, which matters more as AI changes the skills employers need. Job postings and occupational layers frame that purpose and stay out of the data for now.

The current dates for each phase are on the College Collections ETA. Every date there that moves with a connection is a projection, not a partner commitment.

The four parts

The plan's nine priorities sort under four parts, and none is dropped.

1

The data the answer needs

MAP exhibits and aggregates; catalogs and program requirements with their program learning outcomes as printed; course outlines with their SLOs and objectives; issuer-published credential skills, verified only; and the ACE credit-recommendation reference.

Priorities 2, 3, 4 and 8
2

The match

One three-level indicator everywhere, strong, partial or limited, on two scales: identity, set by agreement among a named list of signals, and alignment of learning to course. The reasons always show beside the level, and title words alone never reach strong.

Priorities 2 and 7
3

The path

A best-effort stamp on every page, with a source page and read date under each fact. A suggested petition goes through MAP's Request CPL Review. The page never says credit is awarded; faculty award it. A transcript is read in the browser and never sent.

Priority 6, the front door
4

The loop that corrects it

One route, Report a problem, beside any fact and open to anyone, which feeds that college's reading procedure. A monthly audit on a fixed sample, by college and by level, sets precision; recall is scored against MAP articulations held out by college or by time.

Priorities 1 and 7

Priority 5, the 50 percent law, and priority 9, partners and portability, stand as agreed.

The top line

One number: across every credential in MAP and every college, the share of pairs where a person gets an answer with a next step. Each pair sits in one of three states. No answer yet is a gap to close, and the student still has every other route open.

How the college collections grow

Phase 1 is everything the Commons collects today. Phases 2 and 3 are the partner and college connections that would change its pace, its cost and the quality of what it holds. The plan placed ASSIST and the COCI reload under Next; the clock shows them in Phase 2, and the plan stands as agreed.

Phase 1, now

What COBI collects itself

  • Catalog programs, course requirements and program learning outcomes
  • Course SLOs and objectives from public course outlines
  • Issuer-published credential skills
  • The common references: exhibits, courses, credit recommendations, subjects
  • MAP exhibits and aggregates, nightly
Phase 2, partner data

Official, shared files

  • COMIS Data Mart, for program and course lists
  • COCI, the full course and program dataset on a schedule
  • Program Pathways Mapper maps
  • ASSIST articulations, toward transfer
  • CVC course links
Phase 3, live connections

College and Chancellor's Office systems

  • College curriculum systems, for outlines, SLOs and objectives
  • College student information systems, two ways
  • The Chancellor's Office data lakes
  • Common Cloud, for student totals and schedules
  • CCCStart, for totals only

The first slice, and the order after it

ASE goes end to end first. It is done when every ASE credential in MAP at every college sits in one of the three states, with its level, its reasons, and Request CPL Review where the college has it on. The slice ends with a receipt of the data gaps it found, and those gaps set the harvest order for the next.

The Phase 1 exit gate

Each primary dataset's coverage and its named gaps are published on the Plan view, with no sign-off in the path. The MAP platform team and ITPI receive them to correct. The one hard dependency is ITPI confirming MAP's own counts.
