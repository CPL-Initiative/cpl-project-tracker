Where credit for DSP training could start

California's Direct Support Professional Training gives direct care staff two 35-hour, competency-based segments, each ending in a state test. We matched its topics against the course titles and program outcomes our colleges publish. A small set of colleges already teach this work, and one of them builds a full degree around it.

Online and the CVC Exchange

What we still need to confirm

An online section on the CVC Exchange would let a DSP anywhere in the state take the matched course from their home college at local fees. Our data does not record delivery mode, so every card above reads "to confirm." The psychiatric technician programs include clinical work and are likely to stay in person.

Practical next steps

  1. Obtain the DSPT Year 1 and Year 2 student resource guides and the Medication Administration Skill Check Guide, and compare their objectives with the outlines for Taft's DS courses and Porterville's P020 series.
  2. Confirm online sections and CVC Exchange listings for the colleges above.
  3. Ask DDS how a college can verify a DSP's certification notice for each year.
  4. Invite Taft College faculty to examine a pilot crosswalk together.
