Where credit for DSP training could start

California's Direct Support Professional Training gives direct care staff two 35-hour, competency-based segments, each ending in a state test. We matched its topics against the course titles and programs our colleges publish. Several colleges already teach this work, and Taft College builds two degrees and a certificate around it.

After the Overview, a tab per college, ordered by fit, each ending in examples a college's faculty could examine first and links to the college's CPL opportunities and its credentials in the Credential Catalog.

Online and the CVC Exchange

What we still need to confirm

An online section on the CVC Exchange would let a DSP anywhere in the state take the matched course from their home college at local fees. Our data does not record delivery mode, so every card above reads "to confirm." The psychiatric technician programs teach their clinical work in person.

Each example pairs a DSPT topic with a course whose title names it. The faculty who teach the course decide whether the training meets its outcomes, how many units it carries and whether the award is credit or noncredit. MAP holds no DSPT exhibit yet, so the first college to articulate it sets the precedent every other college can adopt.

The topics come from the tables of contents of DDS's Year 1 and Year 2 student resource guides; comparing the guides' objectives with the course outlines is the next step below.

Practical next steps

  1. Compare the DSPT Year 1 and Year 2 student resource guides and the Medication Administration Skill Check Guide with the outlines for Taft's DS courses and Porterville's P020 series, so faculty can see which topics meet which course outcomes.
  2. Confirm online sections and CVC Exchange listings for the colleges above.
  3. Ask DDS how a college can verify a DSP's certification notice for each year.
  4. Invite Taft College faculty to examine a pilot crosswalk together.
  5. Record the first confirmed match in MAP, so every college can adopt it.

Taft College

Disability Services, Direct Support Education and the Self-Determination Program

Active in the state's program inventory: Direct Support Education (certificate); Direct Support Education (A.S.); Disability Services (A.S.); Self-Determination Program: Person-Centered Planning (noncredit); Self Determination Program: Independent Facilitator (noncredit)

Short medication administration courses
