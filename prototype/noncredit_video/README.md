# Noncredit Summit in Motion

A 100-second film for Chancellor Christian's 2026 Vision 2030 Noncredit Summit, made from the five CPL slides built for her keynote (CPLBrain `04-projects/cpl-initiative/20261005_Noncredit_Summit_CPL_Slides_1.md`), with a music cut and a narrated cut. Sam asked for it on 2026-10-05, after the deck (*"Deck is amazing"*): *"Go ahead on the explainer vid and do one version with just music and another with voice over from our ElevenLabs narrator. See if we have access to a female voice with a slight hispanic accent."* He picked the voice by ear the same day, from three samples: *"Let's go with Ladypatty"*. Draft v1, S335 SkyMotion.

## Files

- `noncredit_in_motion.src.html`: the source. The engine (motion helpers, the logo lockup, the arrow and its trail, the pixel barriers, the score, the player, the narrated clock and captions) is the Cerritos film's `prototype/ironworker_video/ironworker_in_motion.src.html`, itself the funding film's, forked; the scenes, the arrow's flight, the barriers' timing and the score's section order are this film's.
- `build.py`: `FACTS` holds every figure on screen with its source, and `CONFIG` the page, the barriers and the close. `python3 build.py` writes `noncredit_in_motion.html` (the music cut, every image inlined); `build.py narrated` writes `noncredit_in_motion_narrated.html`; `--render` also writes the 1920×1080 frame page.
- `render.sh [narrated]`: the MP4, through the funding film's `render.mjs` (headless Chromium, the score rendered offline, ffmpeg). `FFMPEG` must point at a build with libx264 and aac (the `imageio-ffmpeg` wheel's binary).
- `20261005_Noncredit_Summit_in_Motion_v1.mp4`: the music cut, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 101 seconds.
- `20261005_Noncredit_Summit_in_Motion_Narrated_v1.mp4`: the narrated cut, 3:08, the captions as a subtitle track a viewer can switch off.
- `narration.json` (the script, each clip's read, the cues), `voice/` (the ten ElevenLabs clips), `narrate.py` (the layout and the track: `narration.mp3`, `narration_layout.json`), `cues.py` (each reveal pinned to the word that names it: `narration_words.json`), `narrate_spans.py` (each scene's span, read from the source). `narrate.py` and `cues.py` are the funding film's, forked: only the paths, the spans and the letters the voice spells differ.
- `assets/`: the four portraits from the deck (illustrations, generated in a documentary style; the film says *Illustrative learner and photo*).

## What it says

The deck's five beats in ten scenes: the title; **the promise** (her 2025 words, *"Noncredit and not-for-credit learners will be key to meeting this goal,"* 52,394 served toward 250,000, and the deck's 2030 line, CPL for about 7,500 noncredit learners a year); **the scale** (1 in 4 students take noncredit; 29,649 noncredit award earners in 2024–25, nearly five times 2012–13's 6,291; +34% median quarterly wages, $7,833 to $10,479, worded as the deck's `.md` says to say it); **four learners** (Mira, Cabrillo; Emilio, Moreno Valley; Carla, Mt. San Antonio College; Nadia, North Orange Continuing Education), each a portrait beside a four-step path with gold on the CPL step and a dashed outline plus the words *Being documented now* on the two steps a college is documenting; **ready and waiting** (EMT 78% at 28 colleges against 48% across all CPL; 49 statewide determinations at 253 college sites; 3,269 units, about 4% of CPL); **the funding**, the celebration ($35 million one-time and $2 million ongoing; last year's $50,000 grants to the three noncredit programs; this year's $1.8 million for every college noncredit program; credit and noncredit FTES size every max award); and **the close** (mirror a course, post your certificates in MAP, invite your completers back; *Our Time is Now!* on the deck's navy band, landing on the score's last chord).

52,394 was checked against `live_metrics.json` (`raw.Students`, scraped 2026-10-05 16:38Z). The deck's two refresh items apply here too: refresh the students served and confirm the funding figures the week of the summit, then change `FACTS` and re-render.

## The barriers

Five, each at the top of the scene whose fact brings it down: *Retaking what you know* (Mira), *Training with no transcript* (Emilio), *A ladder missing rungs* (Carla), *Credit nobody claimed* (ready and waiting), *Noncredit as afterthought* (the funding; Sam's own framing of how the field feels).

## The score

The funding film's orchestra and theme, in this film's order: the horn call under the title; the theme on bell and pizzicato under the promise; the strings and the snare under the scale; the breakdown under the four learners, quiet and in A minor here (the relative minor, so its G7 leads home to C), its level climbing from Mira to Nadia and rolling into the horn countermelody under the ready credit; the theme in the horns under the funding, the peak; the last statement a whole step up under the invitations, ending on the chord as *Our Time is Now!* lands at 96.8 s. The beat is 88/160 s, so 40 bars reach the close at 88 s. Each section's chords and theme start from the top where the section starts. Measured on v1 (mean volume per scene folded to mono, dBFS): title -23.3, promise -22.5, scale -20.6, the learners -24.0, -23.1, -22.7, -21.4, ready -19.8, funding -15.8, invitations -16.2, the last chord -15.7; stereo peak -3.6 dBFS, no clipped samples. The first render peaked over full scale after AAC encoding and played the invitations as loud as the funding, so the master gain went from 0.6 to 0.48 and the last statement's level and horns came down (`cl` .8, horns 0.13); the score alone now peaks at -3.3 dBFS. If you rebalance, keep the funding the loudest section. Both MP4s took the retuned score by a remux: the picture was unchanged, so each kept its rendered video stream and took the new score through `render.sh`'s own audio chain.

The narration drives the clock, as the funding film's `n2`: each scene is a lead-in, its clip and its air; the picture stretches between anchors, never plays faster than the music cut, and the score is a bed 14 dB down that dips 6 dB more under the voice, its sections arriving just ahead of their scenes and its last chord landing where the narration lands the band. Four scenes ask for more air (`air_s`, each with its reason in `air_why`): the title (3.4 s), Mira (1.9), Emilio (1.8) and Nadia (2.3), where the picture runs on after the voice. All 38 cues are pinned; no reveal trails its word, and the largest lead is 0.16 s. ## To change it

Edit a figure in `FACTS` when the deck's figure changes, then `python3 build.py`, `build.py narrated` and `render.sh` (and `render.sh narrated`). A scene whose spoken text changes needs a new read in ElevenLabs and `narrate.py`; `narrate.py --check` refuses a clip read from other text. A re-version keeps the date code and takes a version suffix (`..._v2.mp4`), named once in `CONFIG['mp4']` and the narrated variant's `mp4`. Check a text change on the render page (`build.py --render`, the `.fonts` page), never the preview: the preview's fonts are narrower than the render's.
