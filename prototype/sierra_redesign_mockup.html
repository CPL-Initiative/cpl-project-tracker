What is credit for prior learning?

Example answer Sierra

Credit for prior learning is college credit for what you already know: skills from military service, work, industry certifications, apprenticeships and other training. California's community colleges grant it under Title 5, section 55050.

A college assesses that learning in one of several ways: an exam its faculty set, a review of your industry credential or military transcript, or a portfolio of your work. Faculty in the subject decide which course your learning matches.

To start, ask the CPL coordinator at the college you attend or plan to attend, and bring what documents your learning, such as a Joint Services Transcript or a license.

From the CPL Initiative's knowledge base and Title 5, section 55050.
