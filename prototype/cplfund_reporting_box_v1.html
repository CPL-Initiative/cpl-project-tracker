The Reporting box, expenditures first

You ruled that the Reporting box starts with expenditures. This is how it would sit in an institution's drill-in on the Implementation Funding tab, under the CO Monitor's note, for signed-in reviewers. The figures are examples for Sample College, whose maximum award is the model's $345,220. Nothing saves outside this page.

What the box does

  1. A reviewer records what a college reports it has expended, one report per quarter, in NOVA's expenditure categories: the object codes 1000 through 7000 and indirect costs.
  2. The box totals the report and adds it to the institution's expended to date, shown beside its maximum award.
  3. Each report keeps who at the college reported it, and which reviewer recorded it and when. A correction is a new report for the same quarter; the newest one counts, and the history keeps both.
  4. Signed-in reviewers see it, as they see the note. The public explainer and My College show none of it.

Governance: the reports table joins DR-09 beside the CO Monitor's notes, as the same funding decision. Privacy: each report is an institution's dollars and carries no student record, so the student-detail disclosure boundary does not reach it.
