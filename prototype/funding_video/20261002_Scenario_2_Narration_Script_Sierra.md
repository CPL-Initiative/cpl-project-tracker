## The script

| # | Scene | Sierra says |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title | Hi, I'm Sierra. Here's how CPL Initiative funding works for your college. |
| 2 | The funding | The state set aside $35 million in one-time funding. About $25 million goes directly to 118 institutions. The rest funds statewide CPL projects, technology, and two Chancellor's Office positions that support colleges. |
| 3 | Maximum allocation | Each institution has a maximum allocation for the two years, from $150,000 to $400,000. The average is about $214,000. |
| 4 | Credit and noncredit | Each allocation splits into a credit share and a noncredit share. Only noncredit outcomes count toward the noncredit share. |
| 5 | Two priorities | Two priorities carry the funding, in equal shares. Access counts applied CPL units. Completion counts transcribed CPL units, with the MAP counselor step checked. The Chancellor's Office reports on career attainment together with the innovation projects. |
| 6 | How a target is set | The model sets each priority's statewide target from its funding and the FTES reimbursement rate: about 4,367 FTES for each. |
| 7 | Targets | Your college has its own target under each priority. Thirty semester units of CPL make one FTES. For the average allocation, the Access target is about 34.5 FTES. Reach half the target, and your college qualifies for half the share. The full target qualifies for the full share, and that share is the ceiling. |
| 8 | Minimum conditions | Before the Chancellor's Office releases any funding, your college meets three minimum conditions by December 30: name a CPL coordinator, put your local confirmation of participation on file, and upload Joint Services Transcripts for at least 75 percent of enrolled veterans. Until then, the funding your college demonstrates is held in reserve for it. **(Not yet read: see below.)** |
| 9 | Timing | The Chancellor's Office releases funding twice a year, based on your college's CPL to date. The full two-year amount is available from year one, and what remains carries forward to year two. |
| 10 | Support | You won't do this alone. Every priority comes with recommended strategies, a CPL Initiative help team supports every college, and detailed guidance will follow. |
| 11 | Find your college | Find your college on the CPL funding page. |
