## Scenario 1

**Title.** Here is how the 2026 to 2028 CPL Initiative funding works for your college.

**The funding.** The state set aside thirty-five million dollars in one-time funding
for 2026–27. Twenty-five point two million goes directly to one hundred eighteen
institutions.

**Maximum allocation.** Every institution has a maximum allocation for the two
years, sized by its share of instruction, credit and noncredit together. It sits
between a base of one hundred fifty thousand dollars and a cap of four hundred
thousand. Sample College's is three hundred forty-five thousand.

**Credit and noncredit.** Each allocation splits into a credit share and a
noncredit share, following the institution's own instructional mix. Only noncredit
outcomes count toward the noncredit share.

**Three priorities.** Three priorities carry the funding. Access counts applied CPL
units from students who start at the CPL Portal, a college landing page, or a batch
upload. Completion counts transcribed CPL units with the MAP counselor step
checked. Career attainment counts CPL units for students who reach a career
outcome, which the Chancellor's Office measures in EDD wage records.

**Targets.** Every priority has a target in FTES. Thirty semester units of CPL make
one FTES, or forty-five on a quarter calendar. Reach half of a target, and your
college qualifies for half the share. The share is the ceiling.

**The baseline.** Meet the baseline by November first, 2026. Assign a CPL
coordinator, and list that person in MAP and on your college's CPL landing page.
Put your local confirmation of participation on file. And upload Joint Services
Transcripts in MAP for at least seventy-five percent of your enrolled veterans.
Until then, the funding your college demonstrates is held in reserve for it.

**Timing.** The full two-year amount is available from year one. The Chancellor's
Office releases it twice a year, based on cumulative CPL, and what remains after
year one carries forward to year two for your college.

**Support.** You won't do this alone. Recommended strategies sit under every
priority, a CPL Initiative help team supports every college, and detailed guidance
will follow.

**Find your college.** Find your college's maximum allocation, targets, and
progress on the CPL funding model page.

## Scenario 2 (only the priorities scene changes)

**Two priorities.** Two priorities carry the funding, at fifty percent each. Access
counts applied CPL units from students who start at the CPL Portal, a college
landing page, or a batch upload. Completion counts transcribed CPL units with the
MAP counselor step checked. The project allocation funds career attainment and
innovation projects statewide; they add nothing to an institution's allocation.
