# NNNN Title in plain words

- **Proposed:** YYYY-MM-DD, by (party, person)
- **Decided:** YYYY-MM-DD, by (who holds the decision right; see decision 0001, item 6)
- **Status:** proposed, in force, or replaced by NNNN

## The question

One or two sentences.

## The proposal

What changes, stated so a reader can act on it.

## Why

The evidence, with the file or measurement it comes from.

## What it asks of each party

Who does what once it is in force.
