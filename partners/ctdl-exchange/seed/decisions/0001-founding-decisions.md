# 0001 Founding decisions

- **Decided:** 2026-10-20, by Sam for the CPL Initiative
- **Recorded by:** COBI, from the CPL Initiative's decision sheet of that date
- **Status:** in force

## The decisions

1. **One private exchange.** The three organizations share files through this private repository under
   CPL-Initiative, which Malone administers. Each party writes only its own folder; COBI reads partner files at a
   pinned commit and keeps a receipt for each load. Nothing filed here starts a write in COBI on its own.
2. **The pilot covers Credential Engine's ten Ready to Publish colleges at once**: Antelope Valley, Berkeley City,
   City College of San Francisco, Coastline, El Camino, Fullerton, Moreno Valley, Mt. San Antonio, Norco and Taft.
   Records are keyed on COCI control numbers. COBI's catalog harvest stays the record for program requirements;
   Credential Engine's extracts enter COBI as a second reading of the same catalogs, as the source of CTIDs, and as
   the source of fields COBI does not hold. COBI measures, college by college, how often the two readings agree, and
   returns what it finds before anything is published.
3. **CTDL reaches each of COBI's references as a last step.** COBI tracks, for each reference it keeps, how many of
   its complete records carry a confirmed outside number (a COCI control number, and a CTID once published).
4. **COBI's common course layer stays out of the Registry** until the CPL Initiative declares it faculty-published.
   Its identifiers may still change, and a CTID names one version for good. The same reasoning keeps those
   identifiers out of this exchange (`contract/identifiers.md`).
5. **WestEd's reviewers propose; a COBI curator confirms.** Reviews of COBI's common courses happen on COBI's own
   surfaces, and minting a common course identifier stays with the CPL Initiative.
6. **Three decision rights.** Which partner data COBI accepts: Malone. Who confirms a match between a COBI record and
   an outside number: Sam, until he delegates it to a curator. What COBI publishes to the Credential Registry under the
   Chancellor's Office: Sam.

## What follows

Malone creates this repository and invites one technical contact from Credential Engine and one from WestEd.
Credential Engine files the ten colleges' extracts; WestEd files a COCI sample for the same ten; COBI returns the
identifier crosswalk, its findings and three sample Pathways. The four leads read that comparison together and set
contract version 1.
